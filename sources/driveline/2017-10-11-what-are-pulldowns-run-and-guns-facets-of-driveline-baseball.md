# What are Pulldowns/Run and Guns? | Facets of Driveline Baseball

## TL;DR
- A pulldown (also called run and gun or compression throw) is a running throw on a line, done on the way back in from max distance in a long toss progression.
- Its purpose is to teach the athlete to be athletic and throw as hard as possible, rather than guide the ball the way they do on the mound.
- It is presented as the most old-school Driveline velocity drill. No numbers or outcome data are given.

## Claims
- [00:06](https://youtu.be/2agC6NeYNUM?t=6) Pulldowns are the most old-school Driveline technique for developing throwing velocity.
- [00:06](https://youtu.be/2agC6NeYNUM?t=6) The drill derives from Alan Jaeger's long toss model ("Jagger" in the auto-captions (sic)): extend slowly to max distance, put arc on the ball, stay relaxed.
- [00:06](https://youtu.be/2agC6NeYNUM?t=6) On the way back in from max distance the athlete moves their feet and throws on a line to the partner.
- [00:06](https://youtu.be/2agC6NeYNUM?t=6) The aim is a running start into a good throwing position, then throwing as hard as possible.
- [01:10](https://youtu.be/2agC6NeYNUM?t=70) Driveline avoids doing this on the mound much, because a plateaued athlete there tends to aim at a "12 by 12 box" and guide the ball.
- [01:10](https://youtu.be/2agC6NeYNUM?t=70) The mentality being trained is to get out of "pitching" and just be athletic, so the athlete carries that into pitching.
