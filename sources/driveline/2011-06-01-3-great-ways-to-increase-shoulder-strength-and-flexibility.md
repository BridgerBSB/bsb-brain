# 3 Great Ways to Increase Shoulder Strength and Flexibility

## TL;DR
- Shoulder health work for pitchers and hitters rests on three tools: a resistance band cycle, suspension training for the posterior shoulder, and medicine ball ballistic work.
- Forced external rotation stretching that imitates the peel-back position is not advised as a habit; Driveline uses it only for one athlete with surgical history.
- No numbers are given beyond an 8 lb medicine ball as an all-purpose size.

## Claims
- Section "Intro": Athletes often do poor shoulder stretches on the belief that looser is better, per Eric Cressey.
- Section "Intro": Driveline would not use a forced ER stretch without a specific reason. Their one case is a former pro pitcher with multiple shoulder surgeries and limited throwing-shoulder ER, where intensity is increased gradually.
- Section "Intro": Some pro pitchers get the shoulder cranked back twice per day, every day, which the author calls madness.
- Section "#1 - Getting on a good resistance band cycle": Scale band cycle work back in season, because bullpens, long toss and games already load the rotator cuff.
- Section "#1 - Getting on a good resistance band cycle": A light band for warm-up and scapular pull-aparts suits a pre-game routine.
- Section "#2 - Adding Suspension Training for the posterior shoulder": A suspension trainer adds multiplanar pulling work for shoulder integrity, strength and stability.
- Section "#3 - Using Medicine Balls for ballistic training": Rotational med ball work builds ballistic shoulder strength without rapid elbow extension forces.
- Section "#3 - Using Medicine Balls for ballistic training": Variants include overhead slams, jumping slams and overspeed work at varied weights, speeds and intensities. An 8 lb ball is recommended as a general tool.

## Drills
- **Tethered resistance band cycle** - band exercises done on a cycle, scaled back in season; builds rotator cuff and shoulder integrity; for pitchers and batters
- **Band warm-up with scapular pull-aparts** - light band before games; builds shoulder readiness; for pre-game routine
- **TRX reverse fly to pull-apart** - suspension trainer, multiplanar pulling; builds posterior shoulder strength and stability; for pitchers and batters
- **Side scoop toss** - rotational med ball throw; builds ballistic shoulder strength without elbow extension stress; for pitchers and batters

## Evidence cited
- Eric Cressey's article on shoulder mobility drills, cited for the criticism of forced ER stretching.
- One Driveline athlete (former pro, Olympic national team) with limited ER after surgeries; no outcome given.

## Open questions

- Band volume and frequency during the off-season are not specified, so the "cycle" cannot be programmed from this source.
