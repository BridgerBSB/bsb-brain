# "You're Not Capable of Getting Drafted" | Stefan Raeth's Journey to the Fall League

## TL;DR
- Stefan Raeth went from 86 mph to a career-best 95.8 mph over roughly four years, driven by a multi-year strength program at Driveline after his college coach told him getting drafted was unrealistic.
- Key number: he gained 400 newtons of strength in six weeks during an initial three-month strength accumulation phase, after testing at "the strength of a 15-year-old boy" at age 20.
- The story frames velocity gain as downstream of sustained strength work and buy-in, not a single mechanical fix.

## Claims
- [00:02](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=2) Raeth was throwing 86 mph as a college pitcher; his head coach told him getting drafted "doing what you're currently doing" was not realistic.
- [01:02](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=62) His first Driveline strength assessment, at age 20, showed "the strength of a 15-year-old boy"; his first tested deadlift was 135 lbs.
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=125) He entered a strength accumulation phase lasting three months, described as "you just have to get strong."
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=125) In six weeks of that phase his strength assessment showed a gain of 400 newtons.
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=125) His gray ball velocity went from 88 mph to 92 mph during a training phase he calls the "Vivo (sic) phase."
- [03:05](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=185) Back at Washington in 2021, he became one of the top pitchers in the Pac-12, closing (3 innings Friday) and starting (4 innings Sunday) in the same weekend, finishing second on the team in innings as a reliever.
- [05:08](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=308) His fastball climbed from 86 mph to an all-time max of 95.8 mph over about five years of tracked training data.
- [05:08](https://youtu.be/e2rBQk8UHT0?t=308) He was invited to represent the Mariners organization in the fall league after a strong season, which he attributes to trusting the Driveline training process.
