# Link: The Truth About "Quick Feet"

## TL;DR
- Driveline links an Eric Cressey post quoting Mike Boyle: slow feet are a leg strength problem, not a foot-speed problem.
- A quick first step is the result of a powerful first push from the back leg.
- Single-leg strength, single-leg stability and landing skills (deceleration) are the foundation for speed and agility.

## Claims
- Quoted passage (Mike Boyle): the best solution to slow feet is stronger legs. "Feet don't matter. Legs matter."
- Quoted passage: a quick first step without a push from the back leg goes nowhere, so a quick first step is the result of a powerful first push.
- Quoted passage: coaches should replace the "quick feet" buzzword with "great first push".
- Quoted passage: lower body strength is the real cure for slow feet and the key to speed and agility.
- Quoted passage: developing quick feet rests on single-leg strength, single-leg stability work and landing skills.
- Quoted passage: "If you cannot decelerate, you cannot accelerate", at least not more than once.
