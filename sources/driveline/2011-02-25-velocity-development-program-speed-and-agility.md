# Velocity Development Program: Speed and Agility

## TL;DR
- Throwing velocity depends on speed-strength, so slow lifts (back squat, deadlift, bench) should be paired with work that cannot be done slowly.
- Speed versions of the big lifts are programmed at 40-60% of 1RM: 8 sets of 3 for box squats, 10 sets of 2 for banded deadlifts, 1-2 minutes rest.
- Jumps and short-interval metabolic conditioning (carries, sled work) round out the program.

## Claims
- Speed and Agility, intro: Maximal throwing velocity and bat speed mainly require speed-strength, the ability to produce high power very quickly. Training must cover both ends of the absolute speed / absolute strength continuum.
- Speed and Agility, intro: Slow lifts help, but should be paired with speed work so the athlete can use the strength quickly.
- Speed and Agility, intro: Two ways to add speed work: use exercises that cannot be done slowly (power cleans), or modify a lift such as the back squat into a speed exercise.
- Speed and Agility, intro: This is called Dynamic Effort (DE) training, popularized by Louie Simmons and Westside Barbell, and it started with the Soviets.
- Speed and Agility, box squats: Speed box squats are done for 8 sets of 3 reps, 1-2 minutes rest, at about 40-60% of 1RM.
- Speed and Agility, deadlifts: Speed deadlifts against bands are done for 10 sets of 2 reps, 1-2 minutes rest, at 40-60% of 1RM.
- Speed and Agility, jumps: Training for maximum broad jump and vertical leap helps the athlete produce more ground reaction force. The article calls this "the very heart of fastball velocity and bat speed" and it also carries over to running speed.
- Speed and Agility, conditioning: Metabolic conditioning should be short, interval-based, high intensity, with short rest, because baseball efforts are short intense bursts.
- Speed and Agility, conditioning: Farmer's walks, yoke walks, and weight sled push/pull sprints are the examples given.

## Drills
- **Speed box squat** - back squat to a box at 40-60% 1RM, 8 sets of 3, 1-2 min rest; builds speed-strength (Dynamic Effort)
- **Speed deadlift against bands** - banded deadlift at 40-60% 1RM, 10 sets of 2, 1-2 min rest; builds speed-strength
- **Max broad jump and vertical leap** - maximal-effort jumps; builds ground reaction force power
- **Farmer's walk** - loaded carry as short high-intensity interval; builds metabolic conditioning
- **Yoke walk** - loaded carry as short high-intensity interval; builds metabolic conditioning
- **Weight sled push/pull sprint** - sled sprints, short interval, short rest; builds metabolic conditioning and sprint power
