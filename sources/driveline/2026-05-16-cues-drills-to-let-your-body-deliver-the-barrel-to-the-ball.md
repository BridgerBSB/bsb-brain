# Cues & Drills to Let Your Body Deliver the Barrel to the Ball

## TL;DR
- Jack McDonald works Parker Mitzel through cues to hold hip flexion longer and stop the hips from opening early, rather than rolling out of the legs.
- An offset open step-behind drill is used to fix an inconsistent attack angle (reps ran roughly 22 down to 6) tied to a pull-oriented swing, by teaching the hitter to turn behind the plate and catch the ball deeper.
- Hand cues focus on keeping the hands passive and back rather than casting or dropping them, so the barrel gets to the ball smoother and faster.

## Cues
- **"Keep your hips level, keep them down the whole time"** - fixes opening the hips early / rolling out of the legs; for Parker Mitzel (HS senior)
- **"Land soft, like you're landing on an egg"** - fixes pointing or breaking the front foot on the step-behind; for Parker Mitzel
- **"Turn behind [the plate], catch it as deep as possible"** - fixes a narrow, pull-oriented path and inconsistent attack angle; for Parker Mitzel
- **"Leave your hands back, don't get too handsy"** - fixes dropping or casting the hands; for Parker Mitzel

## Drills
- **Offset open step-behind** - start offset open, step behind, land soft, turn behind the plate; builds a deeper catch point and wider swing coverage; for a pull-oriented hitter with inconsistent attack angle (Parker Mitzel)
- **Two-tee hold-through** - two tees set front and back, hit the front pitch and hold the swing through to the back tee; builds holding the load and turn longer; referenced by the coach, not performed on screen in this clip
