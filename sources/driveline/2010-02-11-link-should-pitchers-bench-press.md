# Link: Should Pitchers Bench Press?

## TL;DR
- Boddy reposts Eric Cressey's case that squat and deadlift build more usable strength for pitchers than the barbell bench press.
- Dumbbell bench variations give more range of motion, freer humeral movement, and more scapular and core activation than a straight barbell bench.
- Boddy still has his pitchers barbell bench once a week, paired with a dumbbell neutral-grip bench day and chin-ups/pull-ups.

## Claims
- Link: Should Pitchers Bench Press? - dumbbell bench press gives better range of motion, freer humeral movement (not locked into internal rotation), increased core activation, and more scapular movement than a barbell bench press, per Cressey.
- Link: Should Pitchers Bench Press? - Cressey says he has never seen a correlation, in research or anecdotal experience, between a good barbell bench press and throwing velocity.
- Link: Should Pitchers Bench Press? - Cressey argues squat and deadlift muscle mass carries over to throwing better than bench press muscle mass, particularly through the lower half and upper back.
- Link: Should Pitchers Bench Press? - Boddy has his pitchers barbell bench once a week, in exchange for a dumbbell neutral-grip bench day plus chin-ups and pull-ups on their other upper-body day.
- Link: Should Pitchers Bench Press? - Boddy cites Brady Quinn's 225 lb bench press for 24 reps at the 2008 NFL combine as an example of bench numbers that haven't translated to on-field success.

## Drills
- **Back squat / front squat** - barbell squat, back or front loaded; builds sport-specific and general lower-body strength; for pitchers, favored over barbell bench press
- **Deadlift** - barbell hip-hinge lift; builds sport-specific and general strength that Cressey says carries over to throwing; for pitchers
- **Dumbbell neutral-grip bench press** - dumbbells, neutral grip, done once a week alongside the barbell bench day; builds pressing strength with fuller shoulder range of motion and more scapular movement; for pitchers who still want to bench
- **Chin-ups and pull-ups** - bodyweight pulling volume paired with the dumbbell bench day; builds upper-back and pulling strength; for pitchers

## Evidence cited
- Brady Quinn's 225 lb bench press for 24 reps at the 2008 NFL combine, cited by Boddy as an example of bench numbers not translating to on-field success.

## Open questions
- Cressey states there is no correlation between bench press strength and throwing velocity "in the research or my anecdotal experience" but names no specific study or dataset.
- Boddy still has his pitchers barbell bench once a week despite the article's argument against it, without explaining why that compromise is worth keeping.
