# Why 'Pushing' His Hands Led To This MLB Draft Prospects' Power Leak

## TL;DR
- Eric Kozak runs a swing design session with Coastal Carolina commit Marcus Cantu, whose power leak was pushing his hands and getting vertical instead of holding coil and rotating.
- The fix is sequencing: finish coiling the load before starting the forward move, using two-foot pivot-pick variations as a checkpoint so the stride happens on its own.
- Target ball flight for the corrected pattern is 10 to 20 degree line drives to the pull side.

## Claims
- [00:03](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=3) / [01:03](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=63) - Cantu wasn't coiling during his load; he was coiling late, during the forward move and into foot plant, which limited how much he could rotate his pelvis.
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=125) - Target ball flight for the offset-open pivot-pick long bat drill is 10 to 20 degree line drives pull side.
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=125) - Keeping both feet on the ground while coiling gives a more stable platform and creates a checkpoint before the forward move starts.
- [04:09](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=249) / [05:09](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=309) - Rotation, not just hinge, creates space for the barrel; without separation there's no room for the barrel to slot.
- [09:18](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=558) - Named cue "pick it up and let it happen naturally" fixes drifting forward and flaring pitches to the opposite side under this system's orientation.
- [10:21](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=621) - The big poppy drill is built to increase hip-shoulder separation; Kozak prefers the hitter over-rotate the lower half rather than under-rotate at this stage.
- [13:27](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=807) - The handle-load drill, combined with a pivot pick, is meant to build feel for the barrel turning through the zone instead of guiding it with the hands.
- [16:31](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=991) - Named cue "catch it deep and still pull home" is used to force more separation regardless of actual point-of-contact timing.
- [18:33](https://youtu.be/WaPqnvfP_PI?t=1113) - Better sequencing lets the hitter cover the same swing length in less time, which the coach ties to being able to operate in a tighter decision window.

## Cues
- **"Pick it up and let it happen naturally"** - fixes drifting/pushing forward out of the load; for hitters who force the stride instead of letting weight-forward positioning carry them.
- **"Catch it deep and still pull home"** - fixes insufficient hip-shoulder separation and guiding the barrel to center field; for hitters who don't finish rotating.

## Drills
- **Offset-open pivot pick (long bat)** - offset-open stance with back foot in its normal spot, pivot pick done with both feet on the ground, long bat implement; builds a stable two-point base to finish the coil before committing forward; for hitters who drift while coiling.
- **Pivot pick into hook up** - pivot pick establishes a load checkpoint, then a standard hook up finishes the pattern (lower half opens, front foot picks up and strides); builds separating the load from the forward move so the stride happens on its own.
- **Big poppy** - stride-phase drill where the hitter clears the lower half and lets the rest catch up, coached to over-rotate rather than under-rotate; builds hip-shoulder separation.
- **Pivot pick into big poppy** - pivot pick load combined with a big poppy; builds pairing the improved coil with greater hip-shoulder separation.
- **Handle load** - pivot-pick variation cued to feel the swing "in the handle" rather than pulled/guided; builds barrel turning through the zone without steering, aimed at hitting balls the other way when caught deep.

## Open questions
- The assessment is framed around goals of increased bat speed and exit velo, but no baseline or post-session numbers are reported.
- Kozak explicitly prefers over-rotation of the lower half to under-rotation during this progression; whether that's a permanent target or a training-wheels bias for this drill stage isn't stated.
