# 6 Drills To Improve Your Posture! (EASY)

## TL;DR
- Six drills target two opposite posture faults: weight shifted too far onto the toes (anterior pelvic tilt) and weight shifted too far onto the heel (excessive spinal extension).
- Toe-fault drills (front foot elevated, wedge, walk back) train shifting weight back toward the heel and the middle of the foot before the throw.
- Heel-fault drills (rock back, reverse wedge, step-in) train shifting weight forward toward the toes and the middle of the foot while staying stacked.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/-zjNdGLVJ7k?t=0) A common flaw is athletes dumping into anterior pelvic tilt and shifting weight too far over the toes, which leads to early rotation, overuse of the torso, and harder sequencing.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/-zjNdGLVJ7k?t=0) Three drills (front foot elevated, wedge, walk back) are given to shift weight back toward the heel and the middle of the foot.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/-zjNdGLVJ7k?t=61) Wedge drill: toes on a wedge or mound, weight shifted into the heel, posture upright, then leg lift and throw; weight should land staggered to the middle of the foot, not the toes.
- [02:03](https://youtu.be/-zjNdGLVJ7k?t=123) The opposite flaw is shifting too much weight onto the heel and falling into excessive spinal extension too early in the throw, which causes directional issues and makes it harder to send energy toward the catcher.
- [02:03](https://youtu.be/-zjNdGLVJ7k?t=123) Three drills (rock back, reverse wedge, step-in) are given to shift weight forward toward the toes and the middle of the foot while keeping posture neutral.
- [03:05](https://youtu.be/-zjNdGLVJ7k?t=185) Reverse wedge drill: stand angled down with weight more on the toes, lift, keep posture upright and neutral, then drop over the middle of the foot.
- [03:05](https://youtu.be/-zjNdGLVJ7k?t=185) Step-in drill: start with weight behind, step forward, lift, keep weight over the toes toward the middle of the foot, stay stacked, unwind through the target.

## Cues
- **"Keep the ribs down"** - fixes falling into excessive spinal extension too early in the throw; for pitchers shifted too far onto the heel
- **"Stay stacked"** - fixes losing center of mass off the middle of the foot during the leg lift; for pitchers shifted too far onto the heel (reverse wedge, step-in drills)
- **"Almost like the toes are going to lift off a little bit"** - fixes weight shifted too far over the toes (anterior pelvic tilt fault); used in the walk back drill
