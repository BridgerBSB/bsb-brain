# Christian Coppola DRAFTED by the Philadelphia Phillies

## TL;DR
- Christian Coppola's fastball command and velo collapsed into a case of the yips during his sophomore year at Arizona; he transferred a second time, to Saint Joseph's, and rebuilt around his two breaking balls before the fastball came back.
- Senior year at SJU he ran his velo back from 88 up to 94-95 mph and closed the season with 37 IP, 69 K, and a 1.69 ERA, becoming one of the best relievers in the A10.
- This is a recruiting/profile piece narrating one player's transfer-portal arc to a 12th-round Phillies pick; it does not teach a drill, cue, or method.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=0) Walked on to Rutgers, took the Friday night starter role as a freshman, and pitched for Team USA that summer.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=0) Sophomore year he "began to struggle," citing "a little bit of injury, a little bit of mechanical issues, and just an inability to use his fastball."
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=0) Transferred to Arizona, where the fastball command issue became "a small case of the yips" - better in bullpens, but it "would really come out" in games.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=0) Transferred again for his senior year to Saint Joseph's University, described as a "clean slate" with a new environment and teammates he already knew.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=61) Entered fall camp planning to lean on his two breaking balls and rebuild the fastball gradually: a slider with 20 inches of horizontal sweep at 85 mph, and a "negative 15 bear (sic) curveball" at 80 mph.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=61) Fastball velo climbed through fall from 88 to 90-93 mph, then reached 94-95 mph by the season.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=61) Senior-year totals: 37 IP, 69 K, 1.6 ERA (spoken; the video description states 1.69 ERA).
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/9O0uBasyF_0?t=61) Drafted in the 12th round by the Philadelphia Phillies.
