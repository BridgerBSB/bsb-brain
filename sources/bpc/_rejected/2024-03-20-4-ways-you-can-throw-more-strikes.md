# 4 Ways you can "Throw more Strikes"

## TL;DR
- Source lays out four talking points for a staff to use when trying to fill up the zone: catcher targeting, arsenal design, pitch decision-making by role, and knowing your own miss tendencies.
- No mechanics, drills, or data are given; it is a checklist for pitcher-catcher conversations, not a teaching progression.
- Central idea: pitches that force a hitter to decide earlier produce worse swing decisions and weaker contact.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/sZwlYfLnDWM?t=0) Moving the catcher's target to the middle of the plate and talking through setup and visual cues helps a pitcher get the ball over the dish.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/sZwlYfLnDWM?t=0) Adding a pitch that forces hitters to make swing decisions earlier leads to worse hitter decisions.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/sZwlYfLnDWM?t=0) An aggressive pitch that starts and finishes on the plate generates swings and weak contact.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/sZwlYfLnDWM?t=0) Understanding each pitch's role in the arsenal (which get takes, which get swings, whiffs, contact, and which are hardest to command) improves in-game pitch selection.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/sZwlYfLnDWM?t=0) A pitcher who consistently misses to the same spot should have the catcher shade toward that miss so it still catches the plate.
- [01:00](https://youtu.be/sZwlYfLnDWM?t=60) Combining targeting, arsenal design, pitch decision-making, and known miss tendencies helps a pitcher fill up the zone.
