# Pelvic Dissociations | Learning Hip/Shoulder Separation

## TL;DR
- Pelvic/torso dissociation is the ability to move the pelvis independently of the torso. It is a different thing from hip/shoulder separation, which is the degree of segmentation.
- A basic regression drill holds the upper half closed while the pelvis rotates under it, in a hinge posture, to feel the two work independently.
- Do a couple of sets of 10 reps pre-throwing, after the dynamic warm-up, then carry the feel into throwing drills, long toss, catch play and bullpens.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=0) Separation is the degree to which hips and shoulders can be segmented. Dissociation is the ability to get the hips going independently of the torso instead of everything coming together.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=0) Moving the pelvis under a held-back torso gives maximum rotation and energy transfer up the chain, the whip-like effect into the arm.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=0) The drill is for pitchers who triple extend off the back leg early, rush forward, or rotate everything together.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=61) Set up in a rocker-drill stance, a little shorter than stride length, holding a pole or bar to keep the upper half closed.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=61) Replicate the position just before pelvis rotation begins, after leg lift and center-of-mass drive, with a stable glute load in the hinge.
- [02:03](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=123) The back knee follows the pelvis. It is pelvic driven, not knee driven.
- [03:07](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=187) If too closed off on the front leg, cheat the front foot open to straight on or about 45 degrees.
- [03:07](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=187) The front leg extends on its own as the pelvis rotation pulls the front knee into extension.
- [03:07](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=187) Holding the bar too far out patterns flying open with the torso.
- [04:09](https://youtu.be/-DK-U7sdcAU?t=249) Do not expect immediate transfer to the mound (description); this is a first building block, adapted from the TPI movement screen.

## Cues
- **"Rotate that bowl, which is my pelvis, down into landing"** - fixes spinning open with the torso; for pitchers who rotate everything together
- **"It's a pelvic driven movement, not a knee driven movement"** - fixes driving the back knee instead of the pelvis; for pitchers learning dissociation
- **"Cheat that foot open"** - fixes being too closed off on the front leg in the drill; for pitchers who cannot get the pelvis to rotate

## Anti-cues

## Drills
- **Pelvic dissociation pole drill** - rocker stance with a pole held to keep the upper half closed, hinge posture, drive the pelvis under the torso; builds independent pelvis and torso movement; for pitchers who triple extend early, rush, or rotate as one unit
