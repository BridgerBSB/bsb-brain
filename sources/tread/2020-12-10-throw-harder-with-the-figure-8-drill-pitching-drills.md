# Throw Harder with the Figure 8 Drill | Pitching Drills

## TL;DR
- The figure 8 drill trains arm path fluidity and timing: capturing momentum from hand break so the arm is up and on time.
- Three fixes: match the 8's angle to the arm slot, make the 8 pass through the normal set position, and start slow before adding speed.
- Most important: a too-horizontal 8 produced over-rotation and a late arm in the demo pitcher; a more vertical 8 let the arm get up on time.

## Claims
- [[00:00](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=0)] The figure 8 drill works on arm fluidity, capturing momentum from hand break, and getting the arm up and on time through the arm spiral.
- [[01:01](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=61)] The plane of the 8 should match the arm slot or torso rotational plane; a horizontal "infinity sign" 8 promotes over-rotation in pitchers who already reach back and over-rotate.
- [[01:01](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=61)] The fix for the demo pitcher (Tyler Zombro) was a more up-and-down 8, so momentum takes him down and in line with his rotational plane instead of cross-body and back behind him.
- [[02:01](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=121)] Over-rotation led to counter-rotation, a late arm, and throwing uphill; a vertical 8 gave the arm time to flip up behind the body.
- [[02:01](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=121)] Match the 8 to the slot: a more vertical 8 for an over-the-top arm, an 8 roughly matching a three-quarter slot, and an exaggerated initial swing for a near-sidearm pitcher who over-rotates.
- [[03:02](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=182)] The 8 should intersect the pitcher's normal hand set position (chest level for Zombro), or the drill is hard to transfer to throws that start with hands together.
- [[03:02](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=182)] The hand does not need to pause at the set point, but should feel the energy flow back through where hands normally break.
- [[04:03](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=243)] Start slow and build speed so the body learns not to rotate the torso until the arm is up; this matters most for pitchers with arm timing issues.
- [[05:03](https://youtu.be/OJMYGqwhxhw?t=303)] Slow reps are for the learning phase only; it does not mean pauses or lags at full intensity, and the slow start can be dropped once the pattern is mastered.

## Cues
- **"Slow is smooth, smooth is fast"** - fixes arm not getting up before torso rotation; for pitchers remapping or tweaking arm path (attributed to Dave Coggin, PFA)

## Anti-cues
- none

## Drills
- **Figure eight drill** - arm draws an 8 matched to arm slot, passing through the normal set position, started slow then sped up; builds arm path fluidity, momentum capture from hand break, and arm timing; for pitchers who over-rotate or have a late arm

## Evidence cited
- Case example only: Tyler Zombro (Triple-A pitcher for the Rays) corrected over-rotation and late arm by changing the angle of his 8. No data given.
