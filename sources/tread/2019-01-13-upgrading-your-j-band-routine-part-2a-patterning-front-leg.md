# Upgrading Your J-Band Routine Part 2a: Patterning - Front Leg Bracing

## TL;DR
- A four-step J-band progression for pitchers who "crash" the front leg instead of letting hip rotation pull it into brace and extension.
- Moves from static rotation (hips/shoulders closed together) to a dissociated pattern (hips open, shoulders closed, lateral tilt) to a dynamic step-in version.
- Feeds directly into a medicine ball jump-back drill once the pattern is grooved.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/7b3kEW78N0s?t=0) This is part two of a three-part J-band series; low-load patterning drills target either a lead-leg blocking issue or a pushing arm action issue (this video covers lead-leg blocking only).
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/7b3kEW78N0s?t=61) The lead-leg blocking fault comes from misunderstanding what extends the front leg: it's not the quad crashing and bracing, it's the hips pulling the front leg into extension by completing hip rotation.
- [02:02](https://youtu.be/7b3kEW78N0s?t=122) Progression 1: band at waist height, hips and shoulders start closed, athlete rotates them together, driven by the front (glove) arm, cheating the front foot open slightly to avoid getting stuck.
- [03:04](https://youtu.be/7b3kEW78N0s?t=184) Progression 2: hips start open, shoulders closed, back hip works up and forward with rib cage/scap tilted down and back (lateral flexion), finishing rotation over the front leg.
- [04:04](https://youtu.be/7b3kEW78N0s?t=244) Progression 3 combines the first two: cheat the front foot open, hips and shoulders closed, let the hips go first, finish with hips open and lateral tilt.
- [05:07](https://youtu.be/7b3kEW78N0s?t=307) Progression 4 adds a dynamic step into the band resistance, counter-rotating the shoulders on foot plant while keeping the hips open, then finishing.
- [05:28](https://youtu.be/7b3kEW78N0s?t=328) This progression feeds into a medicine ball jump-back drill (linked separately) as the next step once the pattern is grooved.

## Cues
- **"Cheat the front foot open"** - fixes getting stuck mid-rotation during the closed-to-closed drill; for pitchers who default to shooting forward and crashing the front leg
- **"Step into the movement like you're stepping down a steep slope"** - fixes slamming the brakes on foot plant in the dynamic progression
- **"Step, plant, finish"** - cadence cue to sequence the dynamic step-in progression

## Drills
- **J-band hip rotation patterning 1** - band at waist height, hips/shoulders closed, rotate together, front arm drives it; builds feel for hips initiating the movement; for lead-leg blockers
- **J-band hip rotation patterning 2** - hips open, shoulders closed, back hip up and forward with lateral tilt, finish over front leg; builds hip-shoulder dissociation into the front leg finish
- **J-band hip rotation patterning 3** - combines 1 and 2: closed start, hips go first, finish hips open with lateral tilt; builds the full patterning sequence
- **J-band hip rotation patterning 4 (dynamic step-in)** - step into band resistance, counter-rotate shoulders on plant, keep hips open, finish; builds a more dynamic, game-like version of the same pattern; precursor to a medicine ball jump-back drill
