# Former D3 Outfielder Now Sitting 96 MPH | Will Girardi

## TL;DR
- Will Girardi, a former D3 outfielder who converted to pitching, went from sitting 90-92 mph to sitting 95-97 mph over roughly 5 months of velocity training at Tread.
- His coach attributes the gain to fixing a disconnect between lower half and upper half (he was working almost entirely off his spine and arm) plus a parallel arsenal rebuild.
- He is now throwing a high-carry fastball, a gyro slider, a new changeup, and a developing curveball, targeting a January pro day.

## Claims
- [04:18](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=258) Girardi's bullpen velocity climbed week over week during the block: 3-4 mph gains, then 4-5, then 5-6, sitting 95-96 mph on the day filmed.
- [05:19](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=319) His coach says the "floor has risen" - off-pitches now clock higher than his previous max-intent velocity.
- [05:19](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=319) Coach Justin Nicolino has worked with Girardi for about 2.5 years; velo was 90-92 mph at intake.
- [06:19](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=379) Girardi's college path: D3 outfielder, then UNLV, then Lenoir-Rhyne, then finished at DBU.
- [06:19](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=379) Initial assessment found his lower half and upper half "were not working together as well as they possibly could" - he relied heavily on spine and arm.
- [07:20](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=440) Summer program focused on more hip flexion and more spine flexion to set up extension and better arm timing.
- [07:20](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=440) Velo pens started about a month before filming at 93-94 mph max intent; he has PR'd every week since.
- [07:20](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=440) Parallel Friday arsenal rebuild: he had no changeup and an inconsistent gyro slider before; now has a changeup, a gyro slider, and is developing a curveball to go with the fastball.
- [08:21](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=501) His end-of-week velo pen numbers are now matching what he throws in Friday game-like bullpens as the arsenal rebuild progresses.
- [08:21](https://youtu.be/LSfh0AZJXVQ?t=501) Target is pro day in January.
