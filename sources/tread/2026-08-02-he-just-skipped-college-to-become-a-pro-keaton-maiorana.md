# He Just Skipped College To Become A Pro | Keaton Maiorana

## TL;DR
- A single mic'd-up bullpen between Tread coach Ryan Flippo and Brewers 15th-rounder Keaton Maiorana, working fastball command, slider starting-point consistency, and a first cutter rep.
- Maiorana touched 92.8 mph in the session; his senior year (60 K in 34.2 IP, 2.22 ERA) came after adding the slider that got him drafted.
- The cutter is taught as an offset, near-four-seam grip ("horseshoe in," pull through the bottom-right corner) rather than a separate pitch shape.

## Claims
- Description: Maiorana was drafted by the Brewers in the 15th round of the 2026 draft after a senior season of 60 K in 34.2 innings with a 2.22 ERA, built around a slider added at Tread.
- [00:06](https://youtu.be/x94dpqhmJks?t=6): Coach frames the bullpen's cameras and attention as "the new normal" for his first pro season.
- [01:11](https://youtu.be/x94dpqhmJks?t=71): Maiorana's fastball reads up to 92.8 mph during the session.
- [02:13](https://youtu.be/x94dpqhmJks?t=133): Coach flags getting "a touch early with the gas pedal" as the cause of inconsistent slider starting points, since it doesn't give the hand time to work out in front.
- [03:14](https://youtu.be/x94dpqhmJks?t=194): Tread promo insert claims remote athletes "make huge gains" without ever training at the Charlotte facility in person.
- [03:14](https://youtu.be/x94dpqhmJks?t=254): Cutter grip introduced as offsetting the middle finger to bias "horseshoe in" rather than "horseshoe out," pulling through the bottom-right corner of the ball for a counter-clockwise, near-four-seam shape.
- [05:15](https://youtu.be/x94dpqhmJks?t=315): Early cutter misses diagnosed as getting "too behind" the ball; coach compares finger placement rep to rep to narrow the offset.
- [07:17](https://youtu.be/x94dpqhmJks?t=437): Coach ties the feel of "working directly through" the pitch to projected outcomes, saying consistent execution of that move would put him at "four to six" in his first pro season with better shape and command.

## Cues
- **"Stay tall as you work that direction"** - fixes losing tall posture through the initial move; for Maiorana's fastball direction work.
- **"Don't let yourself die"** - fixes losing finish/direction through the pitch.
- **"Pick your starting point, throw through it"** - fixes inconsistent off-speed starting points; for slider command.
- **"You go horseshoe in, not horseshoe out"** - fixes cutter grip/spin direction; for a pitcher adding a cutter off an existing four-seam.

## Open questions
- The "gas pedal" timing fault is asserted as the cause of inconsistent slider starting points with no explanation of mechanism.
- The claim that remote-only athletes make comparable gains without in-person training is promotional and given without evidence.
