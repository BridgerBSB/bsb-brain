# Why 1 MPH Is Worth Millions to MLB Players

## Description

One mile per hour of fastball velocity is worth $3.4M. One mile per hour of bat speed is worth $2.2M. Director of Baseball Operations Jack Lambert breaks down the math behind the engine, and the guys who moved it.

Alex Cobb added 2 mph to his sinker and beat his WAR projection by two wins. Lars Nootbaar added 8 mph of bat speed in two years and hit his way to the big leagues. Mookie Betts needed just 1 mph and a cleaned-up bat path to outperform projection by 2.4 wins.

The league average fastball climbed from 93 to 95 in a decade. The bar for "good enough" keeps moving. Standing still means falling behind.
