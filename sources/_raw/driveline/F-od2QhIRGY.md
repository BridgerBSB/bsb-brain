# Stop Getting Greedy on Your Pitch (Swing Design)

## Description

Florida hitting trainer Eric Kozak works with Cornell graduate outfielder Jakobi Davis on a full swing design.

Jakobi has elite bat speed — but when he takes intensity down, his sequencing falls apart. His load and stride happen at the same time, his hands take over, and his path goes out and around.

This session builds a constraint progression designed to separate his load from his stride — utilizing a few hybrid drills that you won't want to miss.
