# From Off the 40-Man Roster to Triple-A MVP: The Trey Lipscomb Story

## Description

Trey Lipscomb already knew what was wrong with his swing. He brought a document to his assessment laying it out — the bat speed, the ball flight, the power that wasn't there. He was right about all of it, and he'd been stuck on it for two years. Then he came to Driveline. The assessment found the one thing underneath all of it: he was never creating space in his load, and everything else was a compensation for that.

One year later, he hit 32 home runs after hitting 11, slashed .287/.325/.534, and was named Rochester Red Wings team MVP.
