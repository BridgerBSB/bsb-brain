# The 2 Skills That Actually Make Up Pitching Command

## Description

Most people think command just means "throwing strikes" but the data says it's more complicated. In this video, Director of Pitching Connor White breaks down what command actually is (it's two separate skills, not one), how MLB pitchers really miss their targets, and how our Intended Zones Tracker (IZT) measures and trains it. We cover diagnosing targeting problems vs. skill problems, building an aim plan from a pitcher's actual miss pattern, why a "repeatable" delivery isn't about throwing the same way every time, and how long it really takes to see results.
