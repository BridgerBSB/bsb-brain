# Troy Rallings | Fighting Tommy - Data-Driven Rehabilitation of UCL Reconstruction

## Description

READ THE FULL STORY HERE: https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/2017/05/data-driven-rehabilitation-tommy-john-surgery-troy-rallings-case-study/

From being named 1st Team All American to shortly after, having to get Tommy John surgery, Troy Rallings career was up in the air. Deciding against taking the traditional approach to rehabbing UCL reconstructive surgery; Troy decided to rehab with us. Troy was constantly assessed for physical deficiencies and was given a specific individualized program featuring a custom strength program, plyocare, and weighted ball routine. Through this journey Troy Rallings weathered the physical and mental fatigue of the training but finished his rehab in just 9 months. Shortly after, Troy received a phone call from Angels offering a contract to play professional baseball. 

This is the story of Troy Rallings: Fighting Tommy.
