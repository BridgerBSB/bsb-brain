# Pitching Mechanics: "What's the One Thing I Need To Do To Throw Harder?"

I get this question all the time - just like [Matt Antonelli does](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/2010/09/29/matt-antonelli-theres-no-magic-potion/) (2b for the San Diego Padres). I made a bunch of videos last night (you can check them out at our [YouTube channel page](https://www.youtube.com/user/drivelinebaseball)), including a video response to this question. While there's not "one thing" that is going to make you throw 90 MPH, there's a common mechanical flaw that I talk about in the video that might help you add a few ticks to your fastball's velocity.
