# Your Kid Was Taught To Swing The Bat Slow

## Description

Every 1 mph of bat speed your kid adds is worth about 6 feet of batted ball. Every 1 mph the pitcher throws harder is worth about 1 foot. The hitter supplies the power, six to one.

"Let the pitcher supply the power" is one of the most common things said at youth fields, and physics has shown it's wrong for about twenty years. In this video we break down what bat speed actually is, why it matters more for a 12-year-old than almost anything else, how youth baseball quietly teaches kids to swing slow, and exactly how to train and measure bat speed the right way.

This one is for parents and coaches of hitters ages 9-15.
