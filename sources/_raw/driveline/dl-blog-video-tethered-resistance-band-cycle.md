# Video: Tethered Resistance Band Cycle

Don't have time for a huge update today, so I figured I'd film our tethered resistance band cycle and upload it for everyone to check out. These are the basic [SPRI resistance band](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/products/resistance-bands/) exercises our baseball athletes do to warm up - typically with a [medium-tensile red band](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0000AJ050?ie=UTF8&tag=drivelmechan-20&linkCode=xm2&camp=1789&creativeASIN=B0000AJ050) to get decent bloodflow to the areas. They may repeat it once or twice throughout the day depending on what they have planned - bullpens, long toss, free weight training, speed/agility training, and so forth.
