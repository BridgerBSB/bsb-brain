# Stuff to Read, 3/23/11

Pretty busy with my biomechanics research, lab development, and training, so I'm light on content. Here's some links to tide you over for the time being!

![The Extra 2% The Extra 2%](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0952/2295/6313/files/2percent.jpg?v=1779554102)

- 
[The Extra 2%: How Wall Street Strategies Took a Major League Baseball Team from Worst to First](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0345517652/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=drivelmechan-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=0345517652) is an excellent[Moneyball](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0393324818/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=drivelmechan-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=0393324818) -like book on the Tampa Bay Rays' success under Andrew Friedman's tenure. While it doesn't have the same heartwarming stories about Scott Hatteberg like[Moneyball](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0393324818/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=drivelmechan-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=0393324818) does, it's an excellent must-read for fans of the game. (And if you haven't read[Moneyball](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0393324818/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&tag=drivelmechan-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=0393324818) , get that too!)
- Eric Cressey is putting together his [In-Season Baseball Strength and Conditioning](https://ericcressey.com/in-season-baseball-strength-and-conditioning-part-3-college-baseball) series of posts. He's got three parts up right now - you can find the other two parts from that post that I linked to. Definitely go check it out.
- We kicked off our [Research Study](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/research-studies) on our**Velocity Development Program 2.0** to help improve the quality of our[Elite Baseball Development program](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/elite-baseball-training) ! We have 10 participants and we're looking forward to collecting some data over the next six weeks. A big part of it is[our under/overload training principles with weighted baseballs](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/2010/10/27/weighted-baseball-training-for-pitchers-overload-underload-principles/) , a link that continues to get a lot of hits on the website!
