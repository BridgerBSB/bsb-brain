# Stop Picking Between Bat Speed and Contact

## Description

Bat speed or contact. Hitters get told to pick one. The best hitters in baseball have both.

Pitching is nastier than ever, and the answer isn't choosing between power and contact. It's fast swings plus a bat path with margin. Bat speed sets the ceiling on how hard you can hit the ball. Bat path decides how often you actually cash it in.

Driveline's Director of Hitting Tanner Stokey breaks down the three components of bat path straight from Baseball Savant (attack angle, attack direction, swing path tilt), why the 2025 Blue Jays improved bat speed and strikeout rate at the same time, and why Riley Greene and Yandy Diaz produce with swings that look nothing alike.
