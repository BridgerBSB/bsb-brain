# Casey Weathers - May 2nd Session w/ Radar Gun

## Description

UPDATE: Casey has signed his contract with the Tampa Bay Rays for the 2014 season!

Casey Weathers (2007, 8th pick overall out of Vanderbilt by the Colorado Rockies) throws to Puyallup HS hitters in a live session on May 2nd. Casey was cut by the San Francisco Giants in 2014 Spring Training where he sat 90-91 and topped out at 92 MPH. 

In Casey's first session, he touched 95.9 (proof: http://i.imgur.com/EgcTXEh.jpg) and sat 93-95 with an 83-85 slider.

In this session, Casey sat 94 MPH the entire workout with one at ~95 MPH (94.8 MPH) with a vastly improved slider from 82-85 MPH. JUGS Pro Sport Radar gun in the shot for verification with secondary verification from an Assistant Scouting Director's Stalker Sport 1 sitting behind the plate.

Casey is currently a free agent and is represented by Mark Pieper of Relativity Sports. Mark can be reached at mpieper@relativitysports.com. Kyle can be reached at kyle@drivelinebaseball.com.

Special thanks go out to Coach Mark Wiese at Puyallup HS for providing us a place to work out, Adam Stump and Brendan Illies for catching Casey, and the PHS hitters for getting in the box!
