# The Fall Ball Decision: Which of These 3 Pitchers Is YOUR Kid?

## Description

Fall ball registration emails hit every August with the same pressure: spots are limited, sign up today. But for most players, fall ball isn't really a decision, it's a reaction to a deadline.

Games test what a player already has. Training builds what they don't. After a full spring and summer of games, the real question isn't whether fall ball is good or bad. It's whether your pitcher needs more reps, or more rest, size, strength and skill.

This video breaks down three types of youth pitchers and what each one actually needs this fall:

1. The workhorse who threw a ton of innings and got outs on competitiveness, not stuff
2. The dominant arm who's about to face a much tougher level of competition
3. The pitcher who barely got the ball and still needs game reps

Includes velocity benchmarks from roughly 1,700 pitchers ages 12-18, and what typical velocity gain looks like for players who train through the fall and winter.
