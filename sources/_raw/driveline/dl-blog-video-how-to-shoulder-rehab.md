# Video: How to Shoulder Rehab

Here's an oldie but a goodie: Diesel Crew's "[How to Shoulder Rehab](https://www.dieselcrew.com/how-to-shoulder-rehab/)" video series. We use a lot of the same exercises that Diesel Crew does, and we'll be shooting a ton of video when we finally move into the NSBA facility (tentatively this weekend). Until then, enjoy this great video and link!

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A0ONHZmsFec&ab_channel=DieselSC

We prefer to use the [TRX Suspension Trainer](https://www.fitnessanywhere.com/Merchant2/merchant.mvc?Screen=SFNT&AFFIL=n47F98Q1) for a lot of the same exercises, and we add Face Pulls, Serratus Strengthening, and Reverse Flys amongst other movements. Of course, we heavily use [resistance bands](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/products/resistance-bands/) for many of these movements as well - just as shown in the video and with a few changes.
