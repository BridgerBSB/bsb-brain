# 75 MPH vs. 59 MPH: Same Arm, Same Size. 16 MPH Apart.

## Description

Two youth pitchers. Same age, same size, nearly identical arm action. One throws 75 mph and the other throws 59 mph.

The difference isn't the arm. The harder thrower produced 18% more peak force on the force plates, rotated his hips 57% faster, and shoulder 17% faster. Elbow speed was dead even.

Across 430 pitchers aged 14 and under, force production tracked most closely with velocity, even after controlling for age.
