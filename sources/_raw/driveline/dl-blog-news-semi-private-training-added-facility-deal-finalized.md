# News: Semi-Private Training Added, Facility Deal Finalized

Big news this weekend at Driveline Baseball - we've finalized our deal with North Seattle Baseball Association and will now be offering [semi-private instruction](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/training/semi-private-training/) starting **early to middle of October**!

![NSBA NSBA](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0952/2295/6313/files/5572_AutoBanner.png?v=1779553872)


- [Lots of baseball training related YouTube videos on our channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/drivelinebaseball)
- More programs offered, including semi-private training
- Content to ramp up on the site - lots of pictures of the facility coming soon
- Significant progress on our motion capture laboratory (very excited about this!)

- Pitching/throwing instruction
- Hitting instruction
- Strength and conditioning
- Agility and speed training
- Nutrition consultation

Get more information on costs and launch dates at our [Semi-Private Training page](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/training/semi-private-training/), and [contact us today](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/contact/) to get started!
