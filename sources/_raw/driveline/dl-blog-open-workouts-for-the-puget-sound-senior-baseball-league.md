# Open Workouts for the Puget Sound Senior Baseball League

Driveline Baseball is proud to host open workouts at [North Seattle Batting Cages](https://www.northseattlebattingcages.com) in partnership with the [Puget Sound Senior Baseball League](https://www.pssbl.com) (PSSBL)!

All batting cages will be opened up for 3 hours for unlimited use by prospective and current PSSBL members for just **$13**. Yep, thirteen bucks gets you unlimited use with some of your fellow PSSBL brethren. Get ready for [PSSBL tryouts](https://pssbl.com/league/information.aspx#tryouts) (March 5th, March 12th, and March 20th) or just get some more work in before you report to team practices.

For more information, check out the [PSSBL Open Workouts Page](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/pssbl/) and fill out the form to indicate you'll be coming!
