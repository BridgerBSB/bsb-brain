# Glove Side Elevation Drill w/ Overload

## Description

This drill was inspired by Nick Pereira of Elite Velocity Pitching in Santa Clara. Instead of a baseball, we used 1 kg Green Driveline PlyoCare Balls. Progression was throwing them into the bucket with a baseball hold, then throwing them into a bucket with a baseball simulated throw, then throwing them into a bucket with a full baseball throw at a TAP Command Trainer.

Lots of fun and works on keeping the glove side stabilized and closed properly into rotation. Great drill by Nick, modified by us!
