## Transcript

[00:15](https://youtu.be/rroYrqBO1MQ?t=15) Throwing a 10 pound wrist weight may seem scary at first, but it is actually safer if done right than throwing a baseball, as the arm speed is slower so less stress is applied to the arm. Wrists weights are an essential piece to the Driveline protocol. They promote healthy arm action and build strength and endurance in the shoulder. The wrist weight exercises train the decelerators by encouraging pronation as well as strengthening the posterior shoulder through specific exercises like the Cuban press. Wrists weights also aid in creating a better arm action by training the elbow spiral. This deters the athlete from casting out their forearm or pushing the ball. By reinforcing proper movement patterns with the wrist weights, athletes will see a high carryover to when they throw.
