# What 1,000 Bullpen Days Told Us About Pitch Counts

## Description

Pitch counts only capture a fraction of a pitcher's throwing workload. Across thousands of bullpen days tracked with PULSE, pitchers made 3-5x more throws on the day than they did in the pen. Long toss, warm-ups, between-inning throws, and plyos all count.

Director of Pitching Connor White breaks down how Driveline builds throwing programs around total workload: what workload actually means, how it's measured, and how volume and intensity get distributed across the offseason to chase velo, refine an arsenal, and stay on the field.

Workload management isn't about taking more rest. It's about lining up the work so athletes get the right stimulus now and are ready for what comes next.
