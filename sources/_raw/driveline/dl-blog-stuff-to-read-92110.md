# Stuff to Read: 9/21/10

Here's some links worth checking out - including some old content on this blog:

- 
[When Did "Just Rest" Become a Viable Recommendation?](https://ericcressey.com/when-did-just-rest-become-a-viable-recommendation) - A great short read about how simply resting is terrible advice given to either overtrained or injured athletes.
- 
[Elbow Injuries and What Causes Them (Stephen Strasburg Bonus Content)](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/2010/08/27/elbow-injuries-and-what-causes-them-bonus-stephen-strasburg-content/) - An article I wrote about elbow injuries and the craze that surrounded Strasburg's elbow damage.
- 
[P90x for Baseball?](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/2010/03/03/p90x-for-baseball/) - I thoroughly took apart "P90x" as a viable workout routine for baseball athletes. It's also terrible for the general population, but that's another subject entirely...
- 
[Kinematic Analysis: Wrist to Elbow Relationship](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/2010/01/17/kinematic-analysis-wrist-to-elbow-relationship/) - As we build our motion capture laboratory at our facility, we can examine these types of kinematic parameters more closely. This is a great introductory post to those types of concepts.
