# How Hunter Ray Hit 32 HOME RUNS

## Description

Hunter Ray Swing Transformation: From 6 HR to 32 💣

Hunter was a solid D1 hitter but with a few swing adjustments, he turned into one of the top HR hitters in all of college baseball. How did he do it?

1. His Set-Up: Moved from 50/50 to Preset loaded on the back leg.

2. Controlled the Forward Move: Stopped crashing on the front leg, now loading around/coiling the back leg.

3. Barrel Turn: No pushing the hands down to the ball, crunching the backside and whipping the barrel deeper into the zone.

Hunters success is a reminder that buying in, embracing change, and being consistent over long periods can be career changing. Excited to see what he does at Vandy in 2027. ⚡️
