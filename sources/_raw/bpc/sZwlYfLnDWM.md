# 4 Ways you can “Throw more Strikes”

## Description

This is a post for the “Just throw strikes crowd”

Here’s 4 ways you can use to help yourself or your staff fill up the zone: 

1. Targeting 🎯
-Sit the catcher middle early
-Use visuals/targets to help you attack intended zones
-Understand where in the zone you want to go with each pitch 
-Attack middle early, then expand when ahead
-Eliminate waste pitches 

2. Arsenal Design 🎨
-Add pitches that force hitters to make swing decisions earlier
-Pitches that will get swings/action behind in counts
-A pitch you can throw aggressive that stays on plate the entire time and forces off barrel contact

3. Pitch Decision Making 📝
-Learn the role of each pitch in your arsenal
-Know which pitch generates takes/swings? (Takes: CBs, big sweeping SLs, Chup to same sides Hitters)
-What counts can you steal strikes with certain pitches? (CBs early, back door SLs early, Big Sweepers)
-What pitch can you throw behind in the count?(Cut, Hard SL, Sink/4S) 
-What pitches are best used UNDER the zone? (So not pitches you want to use behind in count…CH or Split)
-What area of the zone do you struggle to execute?

4. Know your misses 🧠
-If you consistently miss to a specific spot, use that to your advantage and set you catcher up to shade the opposite way. 
-What targets give your more room for errors
-How can we attack but give room for our misses to get plate

There is a ton more that we can elaborate on with each of these points but we can be creative to help ourselves/pitchers get in the zone. Communicate with your catcher, know how hitters see your stuff, make better decisions, and use your misses to your advantage.
