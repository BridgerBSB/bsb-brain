# Not Getting Playing Time? Here's What You Should Do

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/VolIfmWvydg

Are you a college athlete who isn't getting playing time? 

What not to do:
🚫Complain
🚫Blame coach/politics
🚫Feel sorry for yourself

Instead:
✅Have honest self assessment
✅Ask coach where you can improve
✅Play the long game (1-3 yr plan)
✅Leverage every opportunity

It's not always easy to keep a positive long term outlook when you feel that you're good enough to be competing, but it's FAR more productive than the alternative.

Use this as a chance to re-calibrate your goals as well as your plan for getting there.🔑
