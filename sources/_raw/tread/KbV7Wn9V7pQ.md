An inside look at a pitch design session with Performance Coordinator Turner Givens and Tread Athlete Dawson Gause. 

Not all sweepers are created equal.😤

Take into consideration where you're releasing the ball from, as the angle this pitch tracks towards the plate with induce different types of reactions. Dawson creates a very sharp angle coming from the far right side of the mound.

His sweeper gets out to -20 inches of HB and gyro slider within -5. This combination of sliders coming from his slot could make it extremely difficult for hitters to make a swing decision.
