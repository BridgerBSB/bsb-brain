# Are Baseball Showcases Worth It?

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/1d-ZclIQH-0

Are showcases worth it?🤔

In some cases, yes. But there are lots of parents stretching their resources to "expose" their son who isn't ready to be exposed yet.

A solid player doesn't need tons of marketing.

If you're 14, 15, 16...spend your time/resources developing first.
