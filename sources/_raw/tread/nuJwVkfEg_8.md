# Should Pitchers Train Overhead?

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/nuJwVkfEg_8

-------------------------------

Should pitchers train overhead? My answer is yes.

This doesn't mean to start maxing out on overhead press or power snatches - but pitching DOES happen overhead, and our athletes must find a way to be stable and strong in these positions.💡

For some ideas on exactly how to strengthen that overhead position watch the full video.
