# What is the TRUE Role of Soft Tissue Work? | #AsktheDoc Episode 6

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/T8nQANoNjUY

-------------------------------

In this #AsktheDoc episode, I asked Dr. Nevin Markel about what the true role of soft tissue work is in an athlete's warm up. While he is a fan of individualized tissue work, it's not for the reason you probably think.

"It's not about making arbitrary gains in range of motion...it's about stimulating their system so that all the joints are at their maximum. When the tissue is at a higher quality, it's warmed up, and the fluid dynamics are working better...you will generally see, at least for a time, an increase in function."
