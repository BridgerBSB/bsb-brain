# Reverse Throw: Our Favorite Cues

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/G3RKCmTEyKs

-------------------------------

We like to use Reverse Throws in order to help strengthen the backside of the shoulder. It is not a backwards chaining drill like a Roll-In or Rocker, so it does not matter whether you do these pre-throwing to get some blood flow into the shoulder, or post-throwing as part of your arm care routine.
