# How To Immediately Improve Pitching Command

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/IC
-
If you're struggling to throw strikes, here's a cue that can be helpful. Rather than trying to hit a pinpoint spot, wherever the catcher sets up try to: "Blow it through the center of the catcher."
-
It seems trivial, but this can improve BB% without hurting a pitcher's stuff.🧐
-
✅Shifting from fine to soft focus
✅Shifting from a defensive to offensive mentality
✅A larger target giving you more perceived room for error & conviction
-
This can also be a great cue if a pitcher cuts balls but only to his glove side.💡
