# Overlooked Concepts in Shoulder Pain for Pitchers | #AsktheDoc Episode 5

In this #AsktheDoc episode, I asked Dr. Nevin Markel about what are some commonly overlooked concepts in addressing shoulder pain for pitchers.

➡️Understanding the function (or lack thereof) of entire kinetic chain.
➡️Mobility vs stability tradeoff in the shoulder.
➡️Losing centration as a precursor to shoulder pain.

Shoulder function also takes into account the synergy of the primary movers + secondary stabilizing muscles around the joint.

The cleaner the movement above + below, and the better the synergy of the stabilizers, the best chance you have to avoid the shoulder paying the price.
