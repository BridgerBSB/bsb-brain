# Is Slow Motion Video Overrated?

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/3E1_7fnhc2E

-------------------------------

I generally prefer my athletes send me full-speed, 30fps video over slow mo. With slo-mo, you may lose a real-time sense of tempo, rhythm, and intensity. We can always slow down 30fps video as well (more difficult to speed up slow-mo video).

There are specialized times where slow-mo video is definitely warranted, but it's absolute overkill for viewing most issues (and ironically loses context despite the higher framerate).
