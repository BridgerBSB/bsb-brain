# 2026 Mid-Season Update: 21 Draft Picks & 50+ Signs

It’s been an epic start to the year for Tread’s athletes – with tons of work being done behind the scenes across thousands of athletes.

We frankly have too many stories to tell (a good problem to have), so for those of our athletes who aren’t mentioned here – know that we appreciate every single one of you for trusting your careers to Tread.

We don’t take that lightly.

That being said, as you all take a breather at the halfway point of the pro season, here is my yearly update on some of our most notable stories, and what’s been happening in the Tread ecosystem.

Let’s dive in.

## **1. 21 Tread pitchers were drafted** 👏

A big congrats to this year’s draft picks.

This year was a record for for Tread in terms of draft picks, and I couldn’t be prouder of the great work being done by our team and by these athletes.

This isn’t as much about Tread, as it is about **the achievement of a lifelong** dream for these guys.

Just take a look at the absolute *squad* that is this list:

![](https://treadathletics.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026-MLB-Draft-Total-Graphic.png)

![](sources/_assets/tread/2026-update/01.png)

Tread athletes came off the board from Round 4 ([Ethan Norby](https://www.instagram.com/p/DarJJiDjODf/?img_index=1), Reds) through Round 19 ([Sam George](https://www.instagram.com/p/Davn2pyFM4y/?img_index=1), Brewers).

Notably, four UNLV arms were drafted – a strong showing for the Mid-Major.

One cool part for me is that nearly every athlete here had visited Tread in person over the last year (and we have [video](https://www.instagram.com/p/DayfOjWAY9t/) of nearly all of their pens). That’s not an accident – that’s the hybrid training approach in action.

Year-round programming, communication (including coordinating with their school development staffs in almost every case) and continuity, with scheduled in-house touch points at key inflection points during the year.

In our mind, this model is the best of both worlds, providing the flexibility of remote year-round coaching with the benefits of hands-on touchpoints, at a value that is still within reach for most of the amateur population.

A couple stories worth mentioning:

- [Ryan Marton](https://www.instagram.com/p/DatozCFiatQ/?img_index=1) (Cubs, 17th Round) was an upper-80s arm touching low 90s just 3 years ago. After a full mechanical and arsenal overhaul, including a new cutter and changeup, he was sitting 93-95 for UNLV and had committed to Mizzou before the Cubs came calling.
- [Carson Lane](https://www.instagram.com/p/DatszV1FAAc/?img_index=1) (Guardians, 14th Round) rebuilt his arsenal and mechanics from the ground up this year, driving his velo up to 93-95 mph, a jump from 88-90 three years ago.
- [Ariston Veasey](https://www.instagram.com/p/DatXos9D7nE/?img_index=1) (Cubs, 11th Round) had thrown all of 10 innings before working with Tread last year. This spring at Clemson, he sat 95 and touched 98 with a 20″ IVB cutting fastball, paired it with a nasty slider, and posted a 60% whiff rate on that two-pitch mix alone.
- [Parker Dillhoff](https://www.instagram.com/p/DatlXWmDHhZ/?img_index=1) (Guardians, 18th Round) was a success story from the 2025 Transfer Portal Showcase after going from 7 strikeouts in 7.1 innings in 2025 at Louisiana-Lafayette to 94 strikeouts in 69.1 innings as UNLV’s workhorse this spring, a massive jump in both role and results.
- [Jase Evangelista](https://www.instagram.com/p/DatgNQbDGtB/?img_index=1) (Red Sox, 14th Round) started training with Tread in 2023, sitting 87-88 mph. Now, he’s worked his way up to 94-96, even recently touching 97 mph. Jase struck out 64 across 46.2 innings at UNLV this spring, he committed to Texas A&M and is now getting a professional opportunity. 📈
- [Fabio Bundi](https://youtube.com/shorts/wy0rRYwX_uA?source_ve_path=MjE0Mjgz&embeds_referring_euri=https%3A%2F%2Ftreadathletics.com%2F) (Marlins, 11th Round) spun a 2.20 ERA with 101 Ks in 86 IP at Monterey Peninsula College, flashing a mid-90s fastball.[Here’s](https://youtube.com/shorts/wy0rRYwX_uA?source_ve_path=MjE0Mjgz&embeds_referring_euri=https%3A%2F%2Ftreadathletics.com%2F) a brief look at his progress.

This is especially exciting considering how much more difficult it is to get selected these days.

The combination of 20 rounds and a *much* more competitive landscape is a totally different dynamic from when I was selected over 10 years ago.

I still remember the rush and excitement that a lifelong goal had finally been achieved.

I was sitting in the bullpen late in a do-or-die Super Regional when our coach came down to break the news…while also letting me know I was pitching the next inning.

And that’s just the perfect representation of the emotions of the situation.

There’s a huge sense of relief and accomplishment – recognizing the work it took to get there. But then, just as quickly, that disappears and it turns to a sober recognition of the huge uphill battle still to come.

A big congrats to these guys.

My reminder to them: the work is just getting started.
