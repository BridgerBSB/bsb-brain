# Level Up Your Arm Care With These 5 Exercises

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/ybqSLyOJ6G0

-------------------------------

Arm care exercises are traditionally seen in the baseball world as isolation exercises to train a particular muscle or pattern. At some point, consider integrating these patterns into standing, "athletic" variations that incorporate pelvic/thoracic rotation + core stability.🤔🕺

Anecdotally, these feel much more natural + athletic than prone/static alternatives and I actually feel like I get better recruitment in the target areas. I DO think there is still value to isolation movements to teach an athlete to recruit the right muscles in I's, Ys, T's, etc.
