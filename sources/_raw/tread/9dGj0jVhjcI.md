# Are You Drifting Too Much?

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/9dGj0jVhjcI

-------------------------------

How much drift should a pitcher have?🧐

A common misconception is that more is better. However, over-drifting tends to lead to rushing & losing control of the back leg.

The real key is experimenting & finding the sweet spot for you.
