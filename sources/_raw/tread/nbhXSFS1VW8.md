# The Number 1 Plyocare Ball Mistake

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/nbhXSFS1VW8

-------------------------------

Narrow plyo walls (less than 6 feet) usually do more harm than good. Why?🧐

To avoid missing the wall arm side, pitchers tend to cheat the elbow forward to hit the center of the "target" in front of them.

This can lead to a drop in velo, posterior elbow pain & cutting the ball.
