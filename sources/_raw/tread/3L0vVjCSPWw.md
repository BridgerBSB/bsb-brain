# Why Some Pitchers Throw Nastier Breaking Balls But Others Cant

## Description

Feeling stuck in your career? Go here: https://treadathletics.com/connect/3L0vVjCSPWw

How does your forearm range of motion influence your breaking ball / change-up?🧐

Something that is rarely talked about is the role of pronation / supination and the ability to execute breaking balls / changeups respectively.

major limits in supination can affect a pitcher's ability to get around a breaking ball, and the equivalent struggle exists for limited pronation and changeups.
