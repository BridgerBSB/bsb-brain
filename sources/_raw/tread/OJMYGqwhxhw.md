I'm a big fan of using figure 8 drills to improve arm path, smoothness and efficiency, but there are three things to watch out for to get the most out of this drill.

➡️Make sure to draw an 8 or ♾ that reinforces the plane you're trying to create.
➡️Make sure the figure 8 intersects with your normal hand "set" position to maximize the transfer to other drills. 
💡This is incredibly important or you'll just be getting good at figure 8 drills with no easy way to transfer it!
➡️If you're not feeling smooth and sequenced, start SLOW for the first few reps to have some awareness of how the arm path coordinates with the pelvis, torso, etc. This can help you feel how everything syncs together. 🤔
