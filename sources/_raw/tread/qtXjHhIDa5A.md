## Description

Meal replacement shakes are an awesome way to squeeze in extra calories when trying to gain weight. I drank some form of this every night to go from 160 to 215 lbs, using it to push myself into a caloric surplus.

Three variations are shown in the video - low, medium and high calorie.

Low Calorie - 250 calories
8oz fat free Fairlife milk
1 scoop whey protein
0.5-1 cup frozen berries
1 handful frozen spinach
Cinnamon to taste
1 handful ice

Moderate Calories - 650 calories
1-2 cups skim Fairlife milk
1 scoop whey protein
1 frozen banana
2 tbsp natural peanut butter
1 handful frozen spinach
1 handful ice
Cinnamon to taste

High Calories - 1,100 calories
2 cups whole/chocolate Fairlife milk
2 scoops whey protein
2 frozen bananas
1/2 cup dry oats
2 tbsp peanut butter
1 handful frozen spinach
Cinnamon to taste
1 handful ice

2 tbsp light-tasting olive oil (optional - not shown)

Try not to use this shake as a substitute for real food, but as a way to bridge the gap between your whole food meals and your caloric goals. In other words, if you're shooting for 4,000 calories a day and hit 3,200 on a given day from your 3-4 meals, make an 800 calorie version of this shake and drink it before bed as your final meal of the day.
