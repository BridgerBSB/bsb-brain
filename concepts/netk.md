NetK — Total Strike Probability Added (catcher framing). Sum over taken pitches of the difference between the actual call and the modeled called-strike probability; positive = catcher stole strikes. **AdjNetK** controls for pitcher and umpire.

Framing run value is reported as RAA / RAA650 — Framing. A pitch is "framable" at a 5–95% called-strike chance.
