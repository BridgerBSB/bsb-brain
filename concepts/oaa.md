Outs Above Average — cumulative count of outs a fielder makes above (or below) the expected number given the difficulty of his chances (driven by out probability, EO = sum of expected out probabilities by year/level/position).

The counting sibling of the rate metric [[paa-eo]]; both rest on the [[comp-plays]] difficulty model. Tracking inputs (TopSpd, React, Arm) are percentile-pooled.
