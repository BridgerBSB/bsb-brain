Weighted On Base Average — an offense rate stat where the linear weights for each outcome (BB, HBP, 1B, 2B, 3B, HR) are derived by **year, level, and league**. Per-PA contribution divided by PA-style denominator (AB + BB − IBB + SF + HBP).

The estimator side ([[xwoba]]) swaps actual outcomes for hit-spec expected stats and, per GC2, treats IBB as a walk (opposite of wOBA's IBB subtraction).
