Competitive Plays — the gating definition for defensive tracking metrics: a play where the fielder's own out probability is ≥ 0.05 **and** the combined out probability of all fielders is ≤ 0.95 (i.e. hit probability between 5% and 95%). Filters out routine plays and impossible balls.

This is the conceptual core of the Astros fielding Tier-1 gate that controls which plays feed tracking percentiles.
