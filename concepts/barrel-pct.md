Barrel% — share of batted-ball events (no bunts) hit in the "barrel" zone. A ball is barreled at EV ≥ 98 mph with launch angle in an EV-dependent band (26–30° at 98, widening every mph above).

Formula: % BBE where `EV ≥ 98.0 + 0.07 * (LA − 28)²`. Predictive of future wOBAcon / xwOBAcon for hitters.
