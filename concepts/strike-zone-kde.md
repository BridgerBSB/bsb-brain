# Strike-zone KDE heatmaps

Kernel-density heatmaps over the strike zone (plate_x × plate_z) to show pitch
location / outcome density, with umpire-adjusted called-strike boundaries and
per-batter zone geometry. Scatter fallback when data is singular.

**Used in:** [[dudz-batter-pitcher]] (YlOrRd KDE per matchup), [[og-pena]]
(umpire-adjusted called-strike differentials).
**Related:** [[statcast-pipeline]] · [[swing-path-bat-tracking]] (per-batter zone).
**Astros tie-in:** strike-zone framework + heatmaps — see `rules/visual-standards`
(ABS 17" zone, Tango heart/shadow/chase) and catcher framing surfaces.
