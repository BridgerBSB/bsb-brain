Offensive Runs Produced — Batting — a hitter's run contribution from the bat, prorated to **650 plate appearances** for cross-player comparability. The batting leg of total offensive value (paired with [[orp-br]] on the baserunning side).

Rolls up into [[rar]] (Runs Above Replacement) alongside defense and baserunning.
