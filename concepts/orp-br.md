Offensive Runs Produced — Baserunning — the run value a player adds on the bases (steals, advancement, taking extra bases). Pairs with [[orp-bat]] to form total offensive contribution, feeding [[rar]].

Underlying tracking is summarized on **max-effort plays**: home-to-first where the batter neither rounds 1B nor decelerates, plus stolen-base attempts (lead distances: Prim at release, Sec at contact).
