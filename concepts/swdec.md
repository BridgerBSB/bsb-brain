SwDec — Average Swing Decision Grade. Quantifies how well a hitter chooses which pitches to swing at, graded against the run-value consequence of each swing/take decision.

**Direction is side-specific:** for hitters higher is better, but a *pitcher's* SwDec reflects the hitter's decision grade against him, so for pitchers lower=better. The projected version (SwingDec) uses what ML hitters would swing at.
