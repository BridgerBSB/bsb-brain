# Example Individualized Hitting Workout (Chase Fluke)

A real, individualized constraints-led warm-up/session — the worked example of how
[[drills-programming]] + [[training-implements]] + ball-flight intention come together into a
prescribed daily plan. Each row = one drill block: **Environment · Drill · Bat · Ball · Reps ·
Ball-Flight Intention** (machine angle / pitch location / type / notes available but unused here).

| Environment | Drill | Bat | Ball | Reps | Ball-Flight Intention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tee | Offset Rotation | Speed Trainers & Game Bat | Hitting Plyos | 12 | Line Drives to CF |
| Tee | Walk Through | Short Bat | Hitting Plyos | 12 | Low Line Drives |
| Tee | Step Backs | Barrel Load & Handle Load | Hitting Plyos | 10 | Line Drives to CF |
| Tee | Hook Em | Speed Trainers & Game Bat | Hitting Plyos | 12 | Low Line Drives |
| Mini Hack | Offset Rotation | Short Bat | Smash Balls | 10 | Low Line Drives |
| Mini Hack | Pivot Pickoff | Long Bat | Smash Balls | 10 | Line Drives to CF |
| Mini Hack | Step Backs | Speed Trainers | Smash Balls | 10 | Line Drives |
| Mini Hack | Around the World | Underload | Smash Balls | 10 | Between 10-30° |

## What this illustrates (the template)
- **Low-intensity → higher-intensity ramp:** Tee blocks first (constraint + plyo balls), then
  **Mini Hack** (live-ish, smash balls) — the "prime → swing fitness → skill" arc from
  [[training-implements]].
- **Every block names an external ball-flight intention** (LD to CF, low LD, 10–30°) — the
  intention-action model from [[meta/skill-dev-principles]], never a body-part cue.
- **Bat varies per block** to attack a specific quality: Speed Trainers (overload, bat speed),
  Short Bat (postural adjustability), Barrel/Handle Load (sequencing/connection), Long Bat
  (separation), Underload (overspeed). See [[training-implements]] for the 20% rule.
- **Ball varies:** Hitting Plyos (flush-vs-glancing feedback) on the tee, Smash Balls (game-
  speed, lighter, exaggerated shape) in the Mini Hack.
- **Drills** are all defined in [[drills-programming]] (Offset Rotation, Walk Through, Step
  Backs, Hook, Around the World). "Pivot Pickoff" is a load/transition variant not in the base
  drill PDF — capture its definition when available.

## How used
The build-the-plan answer key: when a candidate or coach is asked "how would you build a plan"
([[hitting-coach-candidate-prompt]] slide 6), this is the shape of a good answer — sequenced
drill blocks, each with a specific implement + ball + external intention tied to a flaw. Feeds
Module 4 (Training Environments) + Module 6 (Compensations/Drills) of [[pd-onboarding-curriculum]].
