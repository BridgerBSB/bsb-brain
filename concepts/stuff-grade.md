GC2's pitch-grade family — predictions of run-value gain translated to a **20–80 scale**, nested by how many inputs they use: **StuffVel** (movement + velo + max velo), **StuffRelVel** (+ release & extension), **All** (+ location), **Component** (+ count), and **Grade** (+ time through order — the most predictive). InZ% is weighted by called-strike chance.

Astros' internal analog to public [[stuff-plus-4s-pitching]].
