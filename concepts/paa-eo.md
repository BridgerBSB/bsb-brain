PAA/EO — Plays Above Average per Expected Out. PAA = plays made vs the year/level/position expectation; EO = sum of out probabilities; the ratio normalizes a fielder's plays-above-average by his opportunity volume.

Gated to **competitive plays** (combined out probability 5–95%). The cumulative PAA / RAA value metrics use a per-(position, season) calibration offset.
