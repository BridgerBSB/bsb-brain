Earned Run Average — earned runs allowed per 9 innings, the classic descriptive run-prevention stat. ERA- adjusts it to a league-relative index (100 = average, lower better).

Descriptive (what happened) rather than estimating (what should happen) — see the estimator family [[fip]] and [[gcera]].
