The org's stated pitching-development philosophy, as published on Astro World
(`pitching/philosophy` page blurb).

> Develop high-quality **Major League starting pitchers or closers** through a
> highly individualized approach. Ultimate goal: **"Dominate the White with Nasty
> Stuff."** Each pitcher gets an individualized plan to maximize his skillset —
> a **systemic approach with hyper-individualization** across all components of
> pitching: **velocity development, command development, and stuff development.**
> Use **historical data + our internal systems** to leverage every decision and
> build the best path forward for each individual, aimed at performing at the ML level.

**Why it matters:** the north star that every pitching KPI, review, and dev plan
should ladder up to. "Dominate the white" also frames the post-outing
[[prp-player-review-process]] (Attack Plan / Stuff / Command / Delivery).
