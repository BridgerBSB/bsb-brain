Fielding Independent Pitching — estimates a pitcher's run prevention using only the outcomes he controls directly (K, BB/HBP, HR), stripping out defense and BABIP luck. FIP- is the same, adjusted to a league-relative index (100 = average, lower better).

Contrast [[gcera]], GC2's contact-quality-aware estimator that does fold in batted-ball quality.
