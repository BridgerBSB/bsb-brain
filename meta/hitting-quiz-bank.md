# Hitting Quiz Bank (Astro World assessment seed)

Six topic quizzes (~40 questions) that map 1:1 onto the hitting concept notes. These are the
**assessment layer** for the Astro World hitting modules ([[pd-onboarding-curriculum]]) — one
quiz per module to check comprehension. Mix of multiple-choice (knowledge) and open-ended
(application/coaching) items.

> **Voice rule:** rebrand for Astro World; never name Driveline. Source for our reference only.

## 1. The Big 3 → module [[big-3-hitting]]
1. What are the Big 3? Short summary of the purpose + value of each.
2. Which of the Big 3 has the highest correlation to in-game performance, and why?
3. In training, 3 ways a hitter can improve bat speed.
4. Significance of improving bat speed by 1 mph.
5. What metric measures bat-to-ball? What inputs does it need, and what 2 things does it quantify?

## 2. Skill Development Principles → module [[meta/skill-dev-principles]]
1. (MC) Internal cues occur more ___ than external, leading the CNS to be ___ involved →
   undesirable movement breakdown. *(Ans: subconsciously; more)*
2. (MC) Intention-action model — a good example of intention is… *(Ans: All of the above)*
3. (MC) We use ___ design to facilitate self-organization toward positive adaptation. *(Ans: Environment)*
4. (MC) Physical constraints we use in programming include… *(Offset Rotation, Launch Angle
   Strings, Contact Point Parameters)*
5. (MC) An example of blocked practice. *(Tee w/ overload bat; FB machine from the mound)*
6. Explain the intention-action model and how it applies to training hitters.
7. Two types of attentional focus + cues for each; which is more effective; when to use each + together.
8. Two types of feedback; which is more impactful; examples + use in assessing/training.
9. Hitter struggles vs velo + rolls over to pull — practice environment / external cues; design
   a session with blocked + random practice.

## 3. Training Implements → module [[training-implements]]
1. (MC) Training with an ___ implement tricks the hitter's ___ → higher-velocity movement,
   more fast-twitch recruitment. *(Ans: overloaded; CNS)*
2. (MC) Overload/Underload applies the principle of… *(Specificity)*
3. Why use a variety of training bats?
4. Purpose of the long / short / bazooka bat, and how specificity applies.
5. How does training with weighted implements improve movement quality?
6. Why are speed trainers ~20% heavier than the game bat, and what happens past that threshold?
7. Purpose of hitting plyocare balls + the hitter's intention when using them.
8. Why use smash factor balls?
9. "Pitch" to a hitter on what bat speed is, how to gain it, how it helps.

## 4. Blast Motion / bat-sensor metrics → ([[swing-path-bat-tracking]]; consider a `blast-metrics` note)
1. Define + how measured: bat speed, peak hand speed, time to contact, rotational acceleration,
   attack angle, vertical bat angle, early connection, connection at impact.
2. Why is increasing bat speed integral to in-game performance? Bonus: feedback for a bat-speed focus.
3. **We want avg attack angle between 4 and 16°** — why is this range ideal, and when should a
   hitter be at either extreme? *(This is the literal source of our `AACon% (4-16)` snapshot metric.)*
4. Relationship between attack angle and point of contact — is it the same for bat speed?
5. Train a hitter with high early-connection, low rotational accel, high avg VBA.

## 5. Anatomy / Physiology / Kinesiology → module [[anatomy-physiology-kinesiology]]
1. Mobile vs stable joints; a stable joint becoming mobile — why + swing implication.
2. Sagittal/frontal/transverse planes; how the t-spine moves through all three in the swing.
3. Adduction vs abduction, internal vs external rotation — upper-body examples in the swing.
4. Concentric vs eccentric vs isometric — examples + when each happens in the swing.
5. Flexion vs extension — how both produce power in the swing.
6. Stretch-shortening cycle — what, why it matters, swing examples.
7. Kinetic link principle — what + why it matters for assessing/coaching hitters.

## 6. Hitting Biomechanics → module [[hitting-biomechanics]]
1. Vertical vs horizontal swing plane; biomech-lab metrics for each.
2. Why prefer a posterior-dominant over anterior-dominant load strategy; video + force-plate tells.
3. Maintaining spine angle / posture — what to look for on video / K-Vest / mocap / Blast.
4. Why is timing critical for hip/shoulder separation; other timing keys.
5. Implications of losing forward bend early (sequencing, bat path); strategies for a hitter who
   struggles to change it.

## How used
One quiz per Astro World hitting module = the comprehension gate. Open-ended items double as
**interview prompts** (cf. [[hitting-coach-candidate-prompt]] Rapid-Fire / Principles slide — Q3
"4-16 attack angle" already lives there). Knowledge MCs auto-grade; application items need a rubric.
