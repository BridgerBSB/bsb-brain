## Magnet board: COMPARE mode (hiring/main)

Shipped compare on the Astros Multipurpose magnet board - your projection
beside other people's, and beside your own past. Eleven commits, 526 tests,
19 browser checks. Zac: "the magnetboard is littttt".

**Decisions Zac made that changed the build:**
- **Compare is PERSON TO PERSON.** "we arent comparing current - im talking
  about person to person, my project board vs sams. comparing against current
  is not a feature we need." I had built it against Current; that framing died.
- **Multiple people at once**, not two-at-a-time.
- **Level and position as SQUARES that populate**, and the "how far apart"
  column killed as useless. He was right - it was a number you had to
  translate back into levels.
- **History infinite.** **Coordinators must be able to compare.**

**Things I got wrong, worth remembering:**
- Told him coordinators were blocked. They never were - I read a docstring
  written before the role existed and believed it over the code, in a review
  whose whole job was to check the code. Anti-pattern filed.
- Shipped three wrong COUNTS in two days (a header reading 7 over buckets
  summing to 10, "5 lower" over 4 rows, two mocks disagreeing about a player's
  level). One cause each time: a number typed by hand beside rows typed by
  hand. Fixed by deriving everything from one fixture.
- **Two of my own guards were lying** - one searched for " 0 not" in text that
  renders "0not" and could not fail; another called zero differences a failure
  when the save it picked was byte-identical to the head.
- The **heredoc-eats-backslashes** trap fired THREE times despite
  `windows-toolchain.md` documenting it. Ended up authoring the repair as a
  file with newlines built from `chr(10)`.
- Said "I can't tell from outside" whether the deploy landed. He pointed out I
  have a browser. Real reason: extension not connected + no credentials. Say
  the precise thing.
