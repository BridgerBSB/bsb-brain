## Session - swing angles (HBA / VBA / AA)

Made HBA legible, then built the same for VBA and AA, then measured all three on
Christian Walker and Xavier Neyens, then specced the org-wide study.

**Three geometry explainers**, each drawn in the plane its angle is actually
measured in - HBA overhead, VBA catcher's view, AA side view. AA gets ONE panel
on purpose: it has no `bat_side` term, so a mirrored second panel could only
repeat the first. DB-free, so they regenerate for AstroWorld or docs.

**What running the query settled:**
- **Whiffs DO have bat frames** (486 rows vs 497 BIP). `db-columns.md` said they
  did not, and had been contradicting Brodie's own June answer for two months.
- **Takes have frames too** - 24 rows with `did_swing = 0`, HBA scattered -80 to
  +63. `e1x_con IS NOT NULL` is not a swing filter. The shipped tracker has the
  same gap on four metrics; flagged, not fixed.
- Whiff HBA is **2.2x wider** than contact HBA for both hitters. Same ratio, two
  hitters, two levels.
- Hard-hit HBA is **not** tighter than contact. I expected it to be, measured it,
  and it was not - opposite directions per player.
- **Damage is already per-batted-ball.** Zac thought it was only a rate; it is
  the mean of a per-ball score. But the median ball scores 0.0010, so it behaves
  like a top-third detector, which is the DSL/FCL risk.

**Zac's correction, and he was right:** the -30/+45 band I drew as "the swing
window" is the Km30..K45 CURVATURE sampling span. It describes where contact
concentrates, not where swings live. Figure fixed, station row dropped.

**Rules drift, the scary one:** blocking rule 18b + `query-cost-before-handoff.md`
existed in `bsb-wt-bullpen` ONLY. `sync-rules.sh` copies canonical -> siblings,
so running the documented sync command would have silently deleted a blocking
rule. Recovered into canonical first, then synced. 114 files now identical
across 4 worktrees.
