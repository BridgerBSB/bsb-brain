fabricated starter velo, three made-up due-up hitters, a five-man pen with hand-written
development plans. It is now three slides off the Aug 2 game vs the Greenville Drive: the
real opposing nine and the 8 A+ arms who started under a third of their own appearances.
Generated by a script from committed CSVs, so it regenerates rather than being hand-edited.

went on a slide.

**Zac's corrections, all fair:** AvgOutLen has to be innings and thirds, not a decimal
average. Strip the club, the date and the affiliate for an outside audience. The grey note
under the lineup goes. The setup slide reads before the clip, not after.

**M is high home.** He had corrected the `H` code back in August for exactly the opposite
reason, which is why I kept refusing to guess. Now written into `db-columns.md` and synced
to all four worktrees, along with the fact that a pitch carries about eleven clips across
two tables and handing back one angle is not "the video".

**The finding.** Low-A 2026, full count, two outs, man on first: 68 qualifying pitches and
the runner stayed on 28 of them - 41%. With only a man on first: 30 qualifying, 12 stayed,
every one of them with both camera angles on file. Situation 1's real game turned out to be
Aug 6 vs the Salem RidgeYaks, and Waner Luciano is the runner on THIRD who never goes.

**What cost the time.** Finding the base-out state took far too many rounds. What finally
worked was a funnel counting pitches surviving each gate one at a time - it ruled Hickory
out in a single run (733 pitches, zero with a runner on at a full count with two outs).
**Gating on the thing you are searching for is what hides it.** Camden did the final cut by
hand faster than I got there. The slow half was the search, not the deck.

**Three misses, all caught by Zac, all the same shape:** I wrote a column name from memory

putting the count and outs in the output, so he had no way to check me. A filter you cannot
see on the row is a claim, not evidence.

## Session - Candidate Rubrics board + Internal Board notes (hiring/main, `30b3`)

Shipped the Candidate Rubrics board and its CSV import, then built the notes
drawer on the Internal Board. Nine commits, all pushed. 899 tests / 7 skip, up
from 798. `drive_internal.py` 121 / 0, up from 105.

**The design call worth keeping.** No single "mean" column on the rubric board.
The three live forms summarise to three different values in three different
units, so each form declares its own headline question and the table is grouped
BY FORM - the scale becomes a property of the section, and no arrangement of the
data can put a 4-out-of-5 above a 4-out-of-10 in one column. Sorted by
disagreement rather than score, because four people agreeing is not the thing
that needs a screen.

**Zac's model for the notes, in his words:** "these will be private like if sam
has his and i have mine we only can see ours - however they will be accessible
to us on both the current and private boards but to no one else - however we
could share if we want to!!!! remember the overarching gate is owner here."
That last clause is why the magnet board's `notesOn()` gate was NOT ported: it
hides notes on Current, and here a note follows the person.

over between boards.
