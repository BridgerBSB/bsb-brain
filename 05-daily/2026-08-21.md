## Session - rule-injection tax + EOY Completion board (session 953f)

Started with Zac hitting 100% context and asking why the rules all reloaded.
Answer: a rule with no `paths:` frontmatter auto-loads every turn, and that is
the DEFAULT rather than a decision - so "always-on" had quietly become "nobody
wrote four lines of frontmatter." 34 files / 293K. One Streamlit status-page
edit pulled 76 of 111 rule files, ~220K tokens, before a line of work.

Fixed to 11 files / 58K, mean per-edit ~185K -> ~92K, with a guard
(`test_rule_routing.py`, 7 injections proven red). Deliberately made SQL work
~12K MORE expensive: the metric canon never matched `.sql` at all, which was a
hole, not a saving.

**My own measuring tool was wrong.** It mishandled a trailing `**`, so it
mispredicted 6 of 22 REAL observed rule loads. Only surfaced because I validated
it against the loader's own `Loaded ...` lines instead of trusting it. Zac was
right not to take the first numbers at face value.

Then the EOY Completion board, three defects deep:
1. It included MLB and DSL when he had said "AAA to Rok" - I read past it.
2. The Delivered column I added read `?` for everyone: `_srcs` keyed by the raw
   int64 gcid against `str(gcid)` lookups. Same dtype seam as the PRP rules.
3. `--wipe-notes` shipped with its FLAGS BUT NOT ITS CODE. The edit anchored on
   a line containing a printed `\n`; the backslash did not survive the heredoc,
   `str.replace` matched nothing and returned unchanged, py_compile passed. Zac
   ran all three commands and got identical output.

Underneath all of it: **check marks meant "a box was filled in" and were read as
"the deck was sent."** Schiavone was 6/6 and never delivered, so the reset tool
correctly did nothing and looked broken. Fixed by adding the missing fact in its
own vocabulary (SENT / blank / `?`), never by redefining the first.

Last catch, from Zac asking "how will we know if delivery happens": `mark_sent`
returned a bool NEITHER call site read. A failed record was indistinguishable
from a successful one - deck sent, toast says Delivered, board blank forever.

**Remember:** assert every `str.replace`; `py_compile` is not a test, RUN the
CLI; and validate an emulator against the thing it emulates before quoting its
numbers.
