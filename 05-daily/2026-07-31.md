## Session - Gyro SL + cutter starter screen (bsb-resources / feature/pd-goals)

Short build. New one-off query: `sql-queries/gyro-sl-plus-cutter-mlb-2026.sql`
(`a6f33bfc` base, `269f3db5` GS gate, `79d7fc85` share result set). Finds MLB
starters throwing BOTH a gyro-shaped slider and a cutter that still carries.

Thresholds Zac gave, in order, across three messages:
- FC avg IVB >= 4.0, min 30 thrown
- SL avg |HB| <= 8.0 and avg IVB >= -10.0
- ">= 10 GS as well"
- "out of all SPs with >= 10 GS what sample is this - what %age"

Three calls I made and flagged, none corrected:
1. HB gate on ABSOLUTE value so one number covers RHP + LHP (signed would pass
   every LHP sweeper).
2. SL min sample 30, same as FC - he only ever specified 30 for the cutter, so
   this one is mine.
3. -10 SL IVB is a floor, not a ceiling. -6 passes, -14 fails. That is the
   thing that makes it gyro instead of a downer.

Two canon details worth remembering beyond this query: GS comes from the box
(`mlbam.ytd_player_pitching_stats`), and it has to be SUMmed across
`team_id <> 0` rows - a pitcher traded in July has 6 GS on one row and 5 on
another, so a per-row `>= 10` test drops him despite 11 starts. And the share
query uses OUTER APPLY rather than JOIN so one starter cannot count twice if
his mlbam_id maps to two groundcontrol_ids.

The share result set is the part that makes the answer mean anything: the
denominator is EVERY 10+ GS starter, not just the ones who throw a cutter.
Second percentage (share of FC+SL starters) separates "few starters throw a
cutter at all" from "this shape pair is unusual".
