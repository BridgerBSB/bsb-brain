  resurrections in a browser before the fix.

  `board_blob` (resumes) were reachable over PostgREST with the anon key. Found
  in the corner of a screenshot Zac sent about something else.
- **A board dump is the whole board**, and `data/*.json` was not gitignored.

  carried it, so the totals matched and hid it. Caught by reading the file.
