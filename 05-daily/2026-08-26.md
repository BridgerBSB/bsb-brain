### Worth remembering
- **A pptx is a build artifact.** `node gen.js` overwrites hand edits silently.
  Twice his PowerPoint sizing had to be extracted back out of the saved file and
  encoded (`dataBoxes`, `boxes`). Diff his file before regenerating - and a
  screenshot is not a save: twice his were byte-identical to my own build.
- **A clamp is not a layout.** `Math.min(y, 6.32)` on the scenario page drew the
  closing question ON TOP of the last item rather than preventing the overflow.
- **drawtext does not define `iw`** - that is a drawbox constant. Same expression,
  one filter apart, parses in one and fails in the other.
- **`addMedia` stretches** a clip to the rect it is given; the 1280x1024
  Edgertronics were being drawn wide in 16:9 boxes.
- Zac tested me on whether the 30/30 query double-counted stolen bases. It does
  not - proven on a fixture with a player traded inside a level (aggregate row +
  club rows) and again after promotion (club rows, no aggregate).
