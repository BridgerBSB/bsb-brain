## Session - two-way pitcher in Advance + NordBord answer

**Grayson: Seong-Jun Kim (Hickory/TEX) missing from Barrelsville Advance.**

pitcher search filtered him out before it ever looked at the name. The tempting
fix - widening the whitelist to Arm Farm's seven codes including `'TWP'` -
would NOT have found him. Any gate on a label eBIS assigns keeps missing this
class, so the search qualifier is behavioural now: eBIS says pitcher OR has
thrown a tracked pitch in 12 months (`cb6be9ca`). Search only; the org roster
dropdown and IL query keep the position gate because those are browse lists.

Then his report came up blank except BB% and GB/FB%. His `pitch_type` is all
NULL and every aggregation does `dropna(subset=["pitch_type"])`, so all the
pitch tables emptied at once while the two event-path rates kept working.

than missing data, which is how it reached us. Zac's call: allow unclassified
**only if the player has nothing typed in that sample**. Built as
`_apply_unclassified_fallback` at the fetch boundary (`9dd072d7`), so a
partially-classified pitcher keeps today's behaviour exactly.
