## Session — Intangibles Individual position radio + two bug sweeps (feature/astros-intangibles)

Zac asked for OF/IF Individual position buttons (All/LF/CF/RF, All/3B/SS/2B/1B). Built that + faster switching (events cached, PDF on demand) + per-position percentile pools (pinned tracking, live PAA/value, 6h cache). Also pulled the Range&Difficulty page out of the weekly OF/IF PDFs (app-only feature).

The radio surfaced TWO latent bugs, both fixed everywhere:
1. **Players_Games position fan-out** — one row per position per game means Events→Players_Games joins double play-level rows and tag them with the wrong position. Common in the minors (multi-position dev reps), quietly wrong on affiliate postgame/weekly PDFs for a long time. Aggregation (OAA/PAA/tracking) was never affected — display was. Fixed via `drop_phantom_pos_rows`. New blocking rule.
2. **Trends-tab mid-season acquisitions vanish** (rule #17) — Jack Dashwood showed on Leaderboard but not Trends. `drop_duplicates(id)` collapsed a traded player to his old org → dropped by the HOU filter. Fixed on ALL 5 affiliate trackers with `_names_hou_pref`. Bonus: because trend frames aren't org-split, the fix also surfaces his full-season line.

**Zac's key insight to remember:** correct org behavior is USE-CASE dependent — a PLAYER surface (trend/tracker/report) wants the full season; an ORG surface (Org KPI/Rankings) wants strict per-play (traded player's pre-trade games feed his OLD org, not HOU). Documented in rule #17.

All committed, pushed, and deployed by Zac. Two new/updated rules propagated to all 4 worktrees.
