## 09:58 - Command CV: the box is gone, the camera stands on its own

Proved our pinhole camera solve reproduces OpenCommand's poses exactly, then proved the drawn
strike-zone box can be dropped entirely - a single camera position fitted across many clips from
ball paths alone lands within 1.08 in at the plane where the miss is read, against 3.9 in for the
old affine. That is what unblocks A+, where no broadcast draws a box.

Four things died proving it, each of which looked right first: per-clip ball-only fitting (best
naive score, 42 in wrong at the glove plane), the ball-at-plate metric itself (circular), phase
correlation as a motion instrument (non-reproducible), and 12 of 55 hand labels (ingested from
same-named zip clips).

Also found MLB clips need no token at all - Savant serves the byte-identical file. The standing
complaint about manually grabbing tokens is now only true for A+.

serves us is clean; OpenCommand's yellow box is their own overlay on a broadcast source we do not
have. That is exactly why proving the camera works without a box mattered.
