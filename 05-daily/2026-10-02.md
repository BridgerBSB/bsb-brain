## Astro World - offboarding becomes a mechanism (session 86e1)

Seven PRs merged and deployed: #82, #83, #85, #86, #88, #89, #90.

**The thing that took too long to land.** Zac kept saying "I removed them in
Azure, why are they still here", and I kept explaining the gap instead of
closing it. An explanation is not a fix. The actual shape: Astro World has no
Graph integration at all and only learns a person exists from the headers Azure
injects at sign-in. Access is granted by the Entra group `sec_app_astroworld`
(43 members, Zac is an OWNER). Removing somebody there blocks their login and
the app never finds out.

- Deactivating now clears card assignments, watches and any label linked to that
  person. History - comments, activity, mentions - is deliberately untouched.
- #88 is the manual bridge until IT grants Graph: export the group, upload,
  confirm. Needs nobody's permission because Zac owns the group.
- Labels can optionally name a person. Name-matching was rejected: the boards
  carry a label `Garcia` AND a card `Garcia, John`.

**Zac's wording wins, twice.** "USER ASSIGNMENT" not "Stands for", "NA" not "Not
a person". And A-Z should always be on, not a per-board switch.

**The UI defect I caused.** He could not find how to remove a user because the
control was one borderless tinted chip reading "Active, turn off" - a status
label, not a button. I had been telling him to "toggle them off" without ever
rendering the thing I was pointing at. Now a plain `Active` pill plus a bordered
`Turn off` button.

**Three injections came back GREEN, all real gaps.** The one worth keeping:
flipping `readSortAZ` to false passed all 53 tests - and that injection IS the
A-Z bug that shipped. When behaviour is gated by a value the tests cannot read,
the suite has to read the source.

**Waiting on Peter Yee** for one admin-consent click (`GroupMember.Read.All`).
`Mail.Send` is already requested and un-consented on the same app registration,
so that single click would unlock notification email too - and may make Matt
Mistric's SMTP work unnecessary.
