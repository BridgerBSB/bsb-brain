# 2026-06-24

## For future Claude
Heavy build day on the [[Swing Decision Score]] grader in barrelsville — model matured a lot (rate field, csc-driven takes, 2-strike protect, shrinkage) — but ended on a misread: Zac wants a per-level **average** baseline, NOT a 3000-player leaguewide leaderboard; reports stay HOU-only.

## Top 3 today
- Evolve the [[Swing Decision Score]] scoring model toward industry standard
- Surface all the metrics + definitions on the exploration PDF
- Establish a baseline so "50" actually means average

## Shipped
- **Rate-based damage field** — local-average xwOBAcon (Nadaraya-Watson, weighted KDE ÷ unweighted), not the weighted-sum density. Elite contact defines the red, not frequent weak contact.
- **Scott's-rule adaptive bandwidth** — blur scales with batted-ball count (n^-1/6); fewer balls = more smoothing.
- **csc-driven takes** — judged by called-strike probability (`called_strike_chance_mlb`): `1 − 2·csc`, minus extra for a hittable strike in his damage zone, minus more with 2 strikes. **Fixed the take-a-ball-in-damage-zone bug** (a ball is never punished now).
- **±0.83 "touches the zone" strike** + **2-strike protect frame** (damage field off; swing at a strike never docked; chase a ball + whiff = "killer" scaled by distance out).
- **Small-sample shrinkage** — regress thin-BBE hitters toward 50 by n/(n+120) so lucky barrels can't mint outrageous grades.
- Metrics + glossary: Score · GC2 SwDec (compare) · Pre2K · 2K · Sw · Tk · O-Sw% · Z-Sw% · Hot-Sw% · 2K-CW% ("killer"); pool-relative 20-80; leaderboard paginated 30/page; `int`→DSL labels.
- `--all-players` leaguewide query (PP_MASTER all 30 orgs) — works after pyodbc TVP fix (inline IN-list).

## Wins
- Researched [[SEAGER]] (Robert Orr) + [[Statcast Swing-Take]] — our approach aligns with the best public swing-decision metrics, and *personalizes* to each hitter's damage zone (the part they don't do). Called-strike probability solved the strike-width + take-a-ball debates at once.

## Friction
- Ended on a real miscommunication: built `--all-players` into a giant ~3000-player multi-page leaderboard. Zac only wanted the **per-level average** (baseline numbers) + reports kept to HOU guys. Exploration PDF, not a league dump. Frustrating end to the day.

## Notes & ideas

- **Next task:** lightweight baseline-summary mode → grade a sample of all-org hitters per level, output ONLY per-level mean/SD (csv/print), keep the leaderboard HOU.
- **End goal:** per-swing good/bad grading inside postgame reports (the same `score_pitches` net tags each swing).

## End-of-day reflection
Model is in good shape and industry-validated; the gap was communication, not engineering — surface the *intended deliverable* (per-level average vs. leaderboard) before building scale.
