## Session — IF positioning + direction %ages (bsb-resources, `feature/pd-goals`)

Built the one-pager Zac titled **"Avg MLB Positioning + Direction %ages"**: four
panels, an X at each infield position's league-average fielding spot 2023-2026,
and two arrows carrying the share of balls it fields to its left vs its right.
Then wrote `.claude/rules/tracking-position-exploration.md` so the next
positioning question does not re-learn any of it.

10 commits `ea9814b6`..`69231c71`, all pushed.

### The finding worth keeping

**`shift_type_id` cannot gate alignment.** 77% of 2026 MLB balls in play carry
"Not Quite Ted Williams Shift" — a classification that is illegal in a post-ban
season — and `count_as_shift = 0` still contains Infield Up, Corners Up, No
Doubles and Double Play Depth. Alignment now comes off the tracking coordinates
instead: the fielder must have started within 2.5 degrees of his position's
average angle.

Coordinates settled and confirmed twice for free: each position's average
standing angle matches the average angle of the balls it fields within a degree,
and 1B sits 118 ft with bases empty vs **93.6 ft on DP depth** — which is him
holding a bag 90 ft away. The data found the first baseman holding the runner
without being told to.

First real read: every infield position leans toward the **middle** of the
diamond, corners hardest, because the foul line pins them.

### Decisions Zac made

- No angular reach gate. `first_defender_id` defines the range.
- `out_prob > 0`, matching the percentage buckets.
- Launch angle under 10 — ground balls, not the advance report's 15.
- All base-out states, not bases-empty.
- Measure the gap from the league-average X, the same X the panel draws.

### Four things I got wrong, each caught by Zac

1. Invented a ±20 degree reach wedge from **one player's** conversion curve. It
   moved 1B from 41/59 to 24/76 across plausible widths — the biggest lever on
   the page, and a guess.
2. Filtered to bases-empty, throwing away 44% of balls in play.
3. Drew the X at the league average while measuring the gap per-play. Two
   reference points on one graphic, and per-play measurement **absorbs the
   shading** the page exists to show.
4. Used a trajectory + distance ball filter instead of launch angle.

All four narrowed the question to less than what was asked. That is the pattern,
and it is now the bug history in the rule.

### To remember

- A repeated named parameter inside a GROUP BY expression breaks it: SQLAlchemy
  expands each `:b3` into its own `?`, so SELECT and GROUP BY become different
  expressions — and SQL Server blames an innocent column inside them.
- Pushing to `feature/pd-goals` **works**; protection bypasses with a warning.
  The memory saying it needed a PR was wrong and is corrected.
