## MiLB salary - what we paid released in-season signings (Sam)

**Answer: ~$186,954.** 10 minor leaguers signed after Mar 28 2026 and since
released, 619 service days, no signing bonuses. Artifact:
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/bfb33faf-edbb-40b1-aac0-523c8b40c19b

The read for Sam: this is veteran AAA depth, not churn volume. Thaiss, Yajure
and D. Johnson are 55% of the spend on 18% of the days.

Nothing in `.claude/rules/` covered minor league pay, so the session was a
discovery run first. Four things measured and written up in the new
`milb-salary-and-contracts.md`:

- `MONTHLYSALARY` holds a **weekly** rate. A+ reads $27,537/yr weekly against a
  CBA minimum near $27,300; monthly would be $6,332. Zac confirmed from his own
  knowledge (~$20k for a non-40-man AAA guy), which is the same order as weekly.
- The fan-out is `ANDC_ID`, not `VERSIONNUMBER` (every contract has one version).
- The tables are league-wide, all 30 orgs back to 1965.
- Rookie is `r` / `R` / `RR` depending on which eBIS table you ask.

Two bugs caught before the number went out: a $20,098 double count (one player
signed twice, service days are per-player while rates are per-contract), and a
`LIKE '%Astro%'` filter that matched nothing because `parent_team_name` holds an
org code - which returned NULL dates that read as missing data rather than as a
broken filter.
