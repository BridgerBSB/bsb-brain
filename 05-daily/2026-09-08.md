# 2026-09-08

## Sam's stolen-base components study (intangibles, `feature/astros-intangibles`)

Sam asked three things: how tightly sprint speed tracks value on the bases, what
the components of a steal are, and whether success rises with lead length off 1B
and 2B. Zac's calls: MLB only, every season since the 2023 rule change, outcome
is GC2's ORP BR, queries and a PDF before a deck.

**Answers.** Sam's hypothesis does not hold - sprint speed correlates +0.51 with
baserunning runs per 600 PA against +0.37 for lead length, and controlling each
for the other widens it to +0.42 vs +0.17. But his question B is a clean yes at
both bases and monotone (stealing 2nd 77% to 90%, stealing 3rd 78% to 94%), it
is not a slow-delivery proxy (lead vs pitcher time-to-plate correlates -0.016),
and inside one runner his longest quarter of leads beats his shortest by 6.0
points across 8,953 attempts. The best line for the talk: a long lead is worth
about three times as much to the slowest third of runners (+12.9 pts) as the
fastest (+4.8), because the fast man already succeeds at 81% without it.

**Two decks delivered** to `C:\Users\Owner\Desktop\sb-components`. The speed-side
deck exists because Zac asked for the apples-to-apples comparison rather than
taking my word: speed is not even monotone stealing 2nd, runs backwards stealing
3rd (selection - slow runners only go when it is free), and within a runner comes
out at exactly no difference.

## Four bugs of mine, all found and fixed the same day

1. RS2 credited one steal to every runner on base - a blanket `(sb=1 OR cs=1)` on
   all three runner UNION branches. League SB 2023 read 4,418 against a real
   3,503. The BR tracker had the correct per-base gates all along.
2. RS3/RS4 read the outcome off the plate appearance. A steal is its own event,
   and the failure was one-directional: a caught stealing ends the PA so it
   joins, a successful steal never does. 934 caught and 6 safe out of 12,397.
3. The attempt charts shipped uncapped leads. Zac asked directly and he was
   right. 71 rows over 20 ft at 1B, 3 over 25 ft at 2B.
4. A missing comma after a CTE broke RS1 in his hands. Wrote a structural guard
   with a self-test proven red three ways.

An alias-resolution check I added to that guard false-positived on correct SQL
twice, so I deleted it rather than tune it green.
