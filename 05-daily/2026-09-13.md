# 2026-09-13

## For future Claude
A question Zac asked in passing - "can I see the box zone?" - overturned a written finding: the drawn
strike-zone graphic IS in our MLB clips, at all five parks, and the Sep 11 doc was wrong on its own
test case. Session ran overnight from 09-12.

## Top 3 today
1. Prove/disprove that MLB's feed carries the drawn zone box.
2. Show the zone drawn from OUR camera solve, blind.
3. Train a ball detector to replace OpenCommand's detections.

## Shipped
- 15 commits `1929e62e`..`1a887b48` on `bsb-resources/feature/pd-goals`, all pushed.
- **Zone graphic at 5 parks** (BAL/DET/COL/MIA/LAA), verified by per-frame sweep AND by eye.
- **Blind camera**: ball pixels only, no box in the fit, reproduces the drawn zone to 0.84 in with
  the camera off 0.34/0.59 ft.
- `.claude/rules/metric-must-separate-failure-modes.md`, synced byte-identical to all 4 worktrees.
- New scripts: `zone_blind.py`, `zone_zoom.py`, `box_presence_sweep.py`, `detect_ball_eval.py`,
  `detect_diagnose.py`, `render_zone_demo.py`.

## Wins
- The blind test came back WORSE than the box-assisted one (0.5-6.5 px vs 0.1-0.3). That is what
  makes it believable - an identical sub-pixel result would have meant the box leaked into the fit.
- Tiling beat full-frame at epoch 13 after losing at epoch 7, exactly as the stated mechanism
  predicted. A hypothesis surviving a test it could have failed.

## Friction
- **Five watchdog kills, then a spinning loop.** Training stalled at epoch 16/30; cycles 5/6/7 each
  ran 23-84 s against a ~335 s epoch and added nothing while looking healthy. Caught only by checking
  whether the epoch count had MOVED.
- Zac was uninstalling apps to "create space" - disk was never the constraint (32.9 -> 23.7 GB free).
  RAM was. Worth being clearer about that distinction earlier.
- He asked me to stop prefixing every message with "Privately, what I need next". Fair - it was noise.

## Notes & ideas
- **Two aggregates misled us in one day**, both in the same direction: top-1 error made "sees the ball,
  ranks it third" look like blindness; then mAP50 sat flat while pixel error fell 167 -> 44 px. The
  collapse always lives at an `argmax` / `[0]` / `LIMIT 1` over uncertain candidates.
- Reading the box off our own clips could drop the CC BY-NC-SA `strikezone_tracking` dependency from
  the MLB benchmark entirely.

## End-of-day reflection
Four of my own errors are in the commit history on purpose: misdiagnosing kill #4 as an AMP/startup
problem and building a fix for a cause that probably did not exist; a restart loop that assumed the
watchdog spares the parent process; `device="0"` into torch; and the spinning cycle. The pattern is
the same each time - I was confident about this machine's behaviour without checking it.
