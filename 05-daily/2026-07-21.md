## Session - swing shape deck rebuilt as a screen (bsb-resources, feature/pd-goals)

Resumed after the machine died mid-session Jul 19. Nothing was lost; the deck had
been committed at `9d1556cb`.

Zac asked four questions about the deck. Two of them found real bugs: bat speed had

His fifth question - "is there any rhyme or reason why the amount we flatten by" -
was the important one. I answered it wrong, said the FLATTEN magnitude was
data-grounded. It was not. The formula had a floor of 1.0 deg that dominated the
fitted term, so all 12 flagged cards printed "flatten ~1 deg" while claiming to close
most of the gap. It closed 8%.

Council review (data-scientist + skeptic, parallel) returned BLOCK and overturned two
of my own in-session fixes: cleaning bat speed inside the per-swing MODEL is deletion
selected on the outcome, and the height-adjusted loft I built is 0.3% of variance.

Deck rebuilt as a screen. No degree targets. Level-matched baselines, exact binomial
per hitter, Benjamini-Hochberg across all 68 cards, n + Wilson CI on every rate.

New research that stands on its own:
- Per-SWING damage peaks at 8-12 deg loft and falls. The old per-BIP view said it rose
  forever, which is why the flatten cue pointed the wrong way.
- Survivorship: loft->whiff slope +2.04 at MLB/AAA vs +1.01 at FCL/DSL, 2x steeper
  where selection is tightest.
- Pitch mix tested and does NOT explain the flags (absorbs 1.0%, 266/274 flags hold).
- Out-of-sample lift: shape adds +0.0033 AUC to hard-hit per swing, +0.0433 to whiff.
  It is a whiff feature, not a damage feature.
