## Two traps

- **tinyint.** The score columns are `tinyint` and tinyint arithmetic stays tinyint, so the
  moment a trailing team's lead goes negative the batch dies with "Arithmetic overflow error
  converting expression to data type tinyint" - a message that names a data type and not a
  column. Killed two queries on Zac's first run.
- **The evidence column was reading the wrong runner.** The 3-2 query joined lead-tracking at
  `occupied_base = 1` only, so it reported "runner going" on the very clip whose fault is at
  third. Now reads all three bases and sorts any not-going forced runner to the top. Good
  reminder that the column is evidence and the video is the judge.
