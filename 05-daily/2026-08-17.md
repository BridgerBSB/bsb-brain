## Session - EOY Player Care page: ForceDeck resolved, Tina's feedback built

All 13 ForceDeck discovery queries came back. The S&C database dependencies are
DONE: five metrics confirmed by exact `definition_name`, all landing on the same
trials, and step 9b returned a single row - `5, 272` - meaning every HOU player
tested since 2025 has all five. Coverage skews low, not high: DSL 68 players, MLB
24. P2:P1 turned out to be in the DB, so it does not need a pin. Brodie's flat
table carries one of the five, so the EAV chain wins and that question is closed.

Two things I had wrong and corrected: the "4x volume split" worry was a limb
artifact, not a coverage gap; and my own pin count said 8 while its own table
listed 7.

Two measured findings, both off the real export rather than argued:
- The TREND column was comparing one January test to one August test, and a CMJ
  is too noisy for that. Across 175 HOU players the arrow points the WRONG WAY on
  21% (Concentric Impulse) and 32% (Eccentric Braking) versus a windowed mean.
- Zac asked whether we should use the max. Max is biased by how often you test:
  +6.02 above a player's own mean at 3 tests, +14.33 at 30, and HOU counts run
  from 1 to 53. His instinct is right though - a CMJ is an effort test - so the
  fix that keeps it is mean-of-best-k with k fixed.

Tina's feedback landed and is applied: six wording changes, plus her question
about what happens to the layout with NordBord and ForceFrame added. Answered it
by building it - 14 metric rows do not fit, the first attempt ran off the bottom
of the page, so it is two pages now. Page 1 is the written guidance at full
height, page 2 is four tables in two columns at the same type size. Zac also
killed the Spanish line under the page title, which the rest of EOY does not do.

The layout guard took three passes to become real, and that is the part worth
remembering. The header collision I could SEE on the render came back green,
because the two labels sat flush without ever intersecting. Once the crowding
check was honest it caught a third defect I had not spotted at all.

**Decisions:** two pages, not one - "Offseason recommendations" used in both
Nutrition and S&C - no Spanish under page titles - P2:P1 shows most recent only.

**Waiting on Tina**, deliberately. Zac is holding here. When she replies, ask for
the template first - her note about structure and fonts burying the takeaways is
the one thing nobody can act on without seeing it.


## Session - Care page: Tina's round 2, then into the deck (12:13)

Two rounds of her PCMP feedback, then the green light to build it into
production. Commits `45223ae6` (round 2) + `4bdfb2f1` (deck integration) on
`feature/pd-goals`.

**Round 2 (her six notes):** subtitle under the page title dropped; the S&C
box in Athletic Training relabelled as an if-applicable JOINT note; **S&C
recommendations moved back to page 1** beside Nutrition and Athletic Training,
which is the change that made the rest fall into place -- all three departments
now one section each, in one visual language, on the sheet the player acts on;
hamstring split off from jump metrics into its OWN card (she asked for a white
gap, a card gives it a border and its own headers); one naming convention across
every sport-science tech, vendor names off the page; translations trimmed.

**Round 3 (she signed off):** "the last version you sent looks great!!! I love
the simplifications, it's easily digestible which is the key." So the pages
moved out of `scripts/` and into `src/eoy_position_report.py` as
`draw_page_care_1` / `draw_page_care_2`, registered **UNGATED at the end** of
`_PAGE_REGISTRY`.

**Decisions Zac made against her suggestions, both deliberate:**
- **Green-to-red stays.** She proposed a neutral palette to avoid "an overly
  negative perception when a player has several negative trends". Zac: "we
  aren't soft, these are grown men." The honest signal beats the softer one for
  this audience.
- **Spanish stays.** She floated removing it entirely. Zac: "we are dying on a
  hill to keep spanish in here." It is on every page of the deck and this page
  is not going to be the exception.

Both are now in `LINEAGE.md` explicitly, so a later reader does not find her
notes unactioned and assume they were missed.

**Her table notes that WERE applied:** "Left Leg Force" instead of "Force Left"
-- naming the leg frees the description line for the Spanish instead of spending
it saying the same word twice, which is a genuinely nice piece of layout
thinking. Groin and shoulder descriptions became the plain-language gloss
("abduction = outwards") because "I doubt players know the difference". And the
TREND column became an arrow: the signed delta was unreadable without units the
page never gave, so the number invited a comparison it could not support.

**P2:P1 is a chart now**, shaped after the LaunchPad panel she sent. No
confidence band -- LaunchPad draws one, ours would be a band we have not defined.

**The bug worth remembering:** a `\n` inside `_care_bi` advances by exactly ONE
line, so a two-line English caption lands its Spanish on top of line two. 72%
overlap, caught on the render, and the guard was then proven red on it.

**Open and NOT solved:** entering bodyweight. Nutrition keeps scale weights in a
spreadsheet that is ingested nowhere, so the box is manual or falls back to the
force-plate weight. The real risk is the mismatch Tina raised herself -- if we
quote a scale number and a dietitian checks LaunchPad's force-plate number, they
read a miss that is not there. That has to be settled before the box ships
either way. Also open: her group's template, and the pitcher deck (a byte-
identical port of Arm Farm, so it is a different worktree's change).
