# PD Onboarding Curriculum — Base-Level Outline (Hitting as Worked Example)

The **new-employee onboarding spine** for Player Development, LTAD-first, all-domains,
with **hitting threaded through every module as the concrete worked example**. This is
the staging/brainstorm layer; the taught product lives in **Astro World**. One module =
one Astro World lesson (deck + manual) later.

**Voice rule (BLOCKING):** these are **Astros** hitting principles. We borrow the *ideas*
from the best hitting research (Driveline HTKCH et al.) but **never name or cite Driveline**
in any candidate-, coach-, or player-facing artifact. (LTAD is the exception — it's public
sport science we cite by name.) The vault notes below carry source attribution for *our*
reference only.

**The two questions that drive everything** (the assessment-to-development engine):
1. *How does this player compare to the best?*  2. *How does he develop to get there?*

## Module 0 — Orientation: Why & How We Develop
The operating philosophy before any content.
- **Optimize for function, not aesthetics.** No single swing/delivery model — anatomy,
  mobility, body comp dictate each athlete's own best mechanics. We never cookie-cutter.
- **The development cycle:** rep → *objective, external* feedback → adjust → repeat.
- **We coach the environment more than the movement** (constraints-led).
- Player-centered: the athlete must own the *why*.
- Backing: [[meta/skill-dev-principles]] · [[hitting-biomechanics]] (intro)

## Module 1 — LTAD: Long-Term Athletic Development
The spine's priority. Stages of the athlete across a multi-year horizon; **training age**
vs chronological age; windows of adaptability; physical literacy → general prep →
**Special Physical Preparation (SPP)** → specialization. Why you sequence, not pile on.
- The macro frame: which stage a player is in, what to emphasize, why you sequence. PHV /
  maturation, the relative age effect, the 5-S trainability windows, early-sampling vs
  specialization, and the **acquisition → development hand-off.**
- Ties to: [[meta/org-training-process]] (periodization / SPP-first / Main Adaptation Cycle /
  Long-Term Delayed Effect) — the cycle-scale backbone under LTAD's career-scale logic.
- Backing: **[[ltad]]** (full stages / PHV / windows / RAE / baseball pipeline) ·
  [[youth-hp-analytics]] · [[meta/org-training-process]]

## Module 2 — Preparation (Assessment → Development)
How a player shows up and what we capture before we prescribe anything.
- **Intake / snapshot:** standardized swings, batted-ball capture, swing sensor, biomech,
  high-speed video, strength + PT screen. (The 5-day in-gym assessment template.)
- **The data report** = the hitter snapshot artifact (EV/LA/POC/spray/heatmap/percentile-
  vs-level). Our Astros analog of the snapshot is the deliverable for *every* prospect.
- **Strength assessment (re-run every 6 wks):** isometric mid-thigh pull · countermovement
  jump · squat jump · repeated-hop (the "physical Big 3": max / explosive / reactive).
- **PT screen:** thoracic rotation+side-bend, hip IR/ER, tibial rotation.
- Backing: [[big-3-hitting]] (in-gym assessment process + report contents)

## Module 3 — Key Tenets & Beliefs
The core hitting beliefs we hire and develop around.
- **The Big 3, in priority order:** **Bat Speed → Bat-to-Ball → Swing Decisions.**
  (Importance order for predicting production; small but real margins.)
- **Trainability inverts the order:** swing decisions = fastest/most malleable; bat speed =
  most *reliable* to move; bat-to-ball = hardest/least certain. Program accordingly.
- Function over aesthetics; train the *game*, not practice (transfer); **external** focus
  beats internal once competent; **variability/differential learning**; **constraints-led**.
- "A perfect plan with an imperfect swing beats a perfect swing with no plan."
- Backing: [[big-3-hitting]] · [[meta/skill-dev-principles]]

## Module 4 — Training Environments
Where and how reps happen so they transfer.
- **Blocked → Random:** blocked to first-learn a move, random to stabilize + retain;
  progressively raise randomness/difficulty, back off when it breaks, ramp again.
- **Game-like + overspeed**; audit by counting "errors" (practice whiff% vs game).
- **Perception–Action coupling:** face a pitcher > short-box/machine > dry. Decoupled
  reps (dry swings, machine-only, catcher-view video) have real costs.
- **The daily routine / warm-up:** low-intensity constraint + weighted-implement priming →
  skill work. A-days / B-days; phased ramp toward competition. A real prescribed session =
  [[example-hitting-workout]] (Chase Fluke): sequenced drill blocks, each with an implement +
  ball + external ball-flight intention.
- **Periodization:** SPP-first, concentrate loads, exploit the delayed training effect;
  season timing (offseason = heavy bat speed → ramp bat-to-ball + decisions toward opening
  day; in-season manage workload).
- Backing: [[meta/skill-dev-principles]] · [[meta/org-training-process]] · [[training-implements]]
  · [[drills-programming]] · [[example-hitting-workout]]

## Module 5 — Biomechanics Literacy
Enough shared vocabulary to diagnose precisely.
- **Phases & checkpoints:** stance → **load** → loaded → **stride** → launch → **swing** →
  impact → finish. Coil amounts, hip-shoulder separation, posture/side-bend, the slot.
- **Segments:** pelvis · torso (the dominant segment) · arms (connect→release) · hands/
  wrists (lag → release) · **ground force** (rear-leg / lead-leg force couple).
- **Bat path:** Attack Angle (~7° MLB ≈ pitch descent), Horizontal & Vertical Bat Angle,
  on-plane benefits, flat vs vertical swing profiles, the contact-point measurement trap.
- The **>72 mph "fast-swings" reference band** = how we benchmark an individual vs elite.
- Backing: [[hitting-biomechanics]] · [[anatomy-physiology-kinesiology]]

## Module 6 — Compensations / Flaws & Drills  (the "what do you see + fix it" core)
Diagnosis → daily constraint program.
- **The 8 flaws by phase:** Undercoil · Closed Pelvis · Early Extension · Push Pattern ·
  Torso Overcoil · Losing Posture · High Attack Angle · Low Attack Angle — each with a
  **camera angle + body landmark** for diagnosis and an **external cue** to fix.
- **Implement → flaw map:** Speed Trainers (bat speed, 20% rule) · Long Bat (sequence/
  separation) · Bazooka (push pattern + bat drag) · Short Bat (postural adjustability/
  top-down) · Smash Bat + Plyo/Smash balls (bat path + flush contact / plane-matching).
- **Drill catalog by phase/flaw** ([[drills-programming]]): Offset Open/Closed · Step Backs ·
  Hook · Walk Through (load); Keep it Fair (separation/late rotation); Around the World ·
  High-Middle-Low (adjustable bat path); Depth Ladder (contact depth); Launch Angle Ladder
  (barrel/ball-flight precision); High/Low Tee (pitch-height posture). Each maps to a phase +
  flaw + implement; the LA Ladder teaches **change ball flight via contact point, not AA**.
- **Bat-path flaws require sensor AA + outcome data** (K%/in-zone contact for high AA;
  GB%/barrel/xwOBACON for low AA). Watch the **timing/contact-point artifact**.
- Backing: [[swing-flaws-programming]] · [[drills-programming]] · [[training-implements]] ·
  [[hitting-biomechanics]] · [[example-hitting-workout]]

## Module 7 — Metrics & Tech Literacy
- Top-8th EV · Smash Factor · swing-decision run value · attack angle · point of contact.
- Tools: batted-ball (HitTrax/Rapsodo/TrackMan) · swing sensor (Blast/Diamond Kinetics) ·
  biomech (K-Vest/markerless/mocap) · force plates · high-speed video.
- **Blast / bat-sensor metrics** ([[blast-metrics]], deep): bat speed (~1 mph → ~1.2 mph EV),
  peak hand speed + efficiency ratio, time to contact (~0.140 s), rotational acceleration,
  attack angle (ideal **4–16°**), vertical bat angle, early connection + connection at impact.
  *(4–16° AA is the source of the deck's `AACon% (4-16)`.)*
- **Astros stack analogs:** gcOBA · SwDec · Damage% · attack-angle/PoC tracking ·
  bat-speed-canonical cleaning. Backing: [[blast-metrics]] · [[big-3-hitting]] ·
  [[swing-path-bat-tracking]] · [[xwoba]] · [[swdec]] · [[barrel-pct]]

## Module 8 — Game Planning & Approach
- **Approach archetypes:** Zone hitter (heatmaps) · Target hitter (combination) · Guess
  hitter (usage rates). Match the visual to the hitter type.
- **Leverage (default) vs Two-Strike** swing goals; "win the pitch / better 0-1 than 0-for-1."
- Build a plan from **arsenal · usage · location**; **distill, don't dump** (no info overload).
- Backing: [[big-3-hitting]] (game-planning section)

## Module 9 — Communication, Buy-in & Scaling
- Teach the **why**; build **ownership**; language/cues/progressions.
- **Cross-functional:** align with S&C, ATC, biomech, analytics.
- **Scaling a principle** across affiliates: clear, teachable framework; alignment without
  micromanaging; accountability; success indicators.
- Backing: [[meta/skill-dev-principles]]
