## Captured resource: Savant Outfielder Jump

- Link: <https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/leaderboard/outfield_jump>
- What it is: Statcast leaderboard of outfielder jumps; Mazzo's model for "Launch".
- **Jump** = feet covered in the right direction in the first 3 s after **pitch release**. **Reaction** = feet in any direction, first 1.5 s (first step). **Burst** = feet in any direction, 1.5-3 s (acceleration). **Route** = feet in any direction vs feet in the correct direction over 3 s.
- Only Two Star or harder plays (catch probability 90% or lower); qualifier = more 2-star opps than team games / 5.
- Reported as feet vs MLB average; the average is play-specific (running back or not, near the wall, hang time).
- Mazzo's names: Launch = Jump, LReact = Reaction, LAccel = Burst (he wrote 1.6-3 s), LRoute = Route.
- Sources: [MLB glossary - Jump](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/jump), [MLB.com - new jump/burst/route metrics](https://www.mlb.com/news/new-outfield-jump-burst-route-metrics-at-baseball-savant).
