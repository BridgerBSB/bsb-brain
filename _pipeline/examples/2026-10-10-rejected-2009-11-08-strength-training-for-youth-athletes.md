What the writer said it covered:

- Youth strength training is safe when properly designed; the "it stunts growth" myth has no research backing.
- ACSM, AAP, and NSCA all endorse structured, supervised youth strength programs, and a kid ready for organized sports at 7 or 8 is ready for some form of it.
- Pre-puberty athletes (under biological age 13-14) should use a linear progression model, add load sparingly, and get closer recovery supervision than post-puberty athletes.
