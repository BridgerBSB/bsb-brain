# Attack angle read conditional on contact depth

Hypothesis from Eric Kozak's Volpe threads: AA rises as contact moves out front, so an AA
that is fine deep turns too steep pull-side (Volpe 13.1 deg overall, 17.5 deg at pulled
contact) and shows up as pulled grounders and topspin liners. Test it on our hitters: AA vs
PoC depth per hitter, and whether steep-AA-out-front predicts Pull GB% vs Pull Air%. Feeds
the paused swing-angle study as hypothesis H1. Observational: no coaching cue ships from it.
