# Reliever / Starter Role-Fit Profiler (Tier 12)

Flags which starters' stuff plays up in relief (velo-decay-across-outing plus arsenal depth) and which relievers could stretch out. Serves the role-assignment and bullpen-build call, since a 4.50-ERA failed starter can be a dominant reliever. The label is soft (usage-imitation, not value) so keep the output descriptive, not prescriptive.
