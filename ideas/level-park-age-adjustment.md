# Level / Park / Age Adjustment Layer (Tier 0B)

The layer that lets us answer "is he improving or just facing weaker comp." Three parts: MiLB park factors from our own pitch data, DSL to MLB level-translation via matched difference-in-differences (nets out survivorship), and age-relative-to-level plus survivorship-corrected aging curves. Reporting layer only, never fed back into the promotion model (circular). Poisons every cross-level comparison if absent.
