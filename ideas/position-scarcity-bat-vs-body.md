# Position-Scarcity "Bat vs Body" (Tier 10)

Fuse offensive value with earned defensive value (Tier-1-gated OAA/PAA-EO/Arm) and weight premium positions (C, SS, CF). A rangy shortstop with an average bat outranks a corner bat that has to mash. Defense Matrix exists but is not fused with the bat or weighted by scarcity, and the defensive credit must be gated by real reps.
