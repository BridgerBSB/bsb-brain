# Hierarchical Projection Baseline (Tier 13)

A transparent Marcel-class Bayesian projection whose job is to be the baseline-lift yardstick for the 14 LightGBM models. If the ML does not beat this out-of-fold, the complexity is not earning its keep. Composes the reliability, level-translation, and aging layers, and gives calibrated intervals the black-box models do not.
