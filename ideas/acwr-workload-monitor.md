# Workload / Injury-Risk System (ACWR to survival) (Tier 1)

The council's unanimous pick, green-lit and scoping. Rolling Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (7-9d vs 28d throw load) flagged against the validated 0.8-1.3 band, validated against our own DL_Stints, then a gradient-boosted time-to-IL survival model. Serves the activate / option / handle-with-care call on the most expensive event in pitcher development. The label genuinely exists in DL_Stints, which most orgs cannot say.
