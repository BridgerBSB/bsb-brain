# Stuff+ / Location+ Pitch-Quality (Tier 8)

Grade each pitch on physics (velo, movement, spin, release, extension) independent of outcome. Stabilizes an order of magnitude faster than outcome metrics, which is exactly what you want for a 40-IP teen, and separates good-stuff-bad-results from bad-stuff. Cross-validate by pitcher not by pitch, keep features strictly physical, and remember a ~0.10-0.20 R2 is normal and useful here.
