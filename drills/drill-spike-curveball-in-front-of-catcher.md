# Spike curveball in front of catcher

**Setup:** catcher set up normally mid-plate (glove side for a slurve), or a net with tape marking the target; throw curves to land a foot in front, not for strikes
**Builds:** pitch quality and curveball movement without strike pressure
**For:** pitchers with a flat or inconsistent curveball
