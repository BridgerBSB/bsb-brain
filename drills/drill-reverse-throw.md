# Reverse Throw

**Setup:** start from the throw's finish position (scap protracted, shoulder internally rotated, forearm pronated) and reverse the pattern with a weighted ball, 1 lb for pitchers 13 and under up to 2 lb for older pitchers, a few sets of 10 reps a few times a week
**Builds:** strength and sequencing in the backside of the shoulder
**For:** high school and college pitchers as a pre-throw warmup or post-throw/arm-care exercise
