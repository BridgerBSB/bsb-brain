# Post-exhaustion superset (pec/lat)

**Setup:** compound lift (dumbbell row, floor press, lat pulldown) immediately followed by a single-joint isolation move for the same muscle (straight-arm pulldown, band chest flies, band pullover, cable row)
**Builds:** fuller lat and pec fiber recruitment than compound lifts alone provide
**For:** pitchers whose pecs and lats are under-stimulated by standard pressing/rowing work
