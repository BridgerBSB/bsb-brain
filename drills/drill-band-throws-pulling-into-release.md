# Band throws (pulling into release)

**Setup:** the band forces the arm to lag behind the shoulders, permitting only a pull of the humerus from behind the midline into horizontal adduction/internal rotation, never a push
**Builds:** a pec/lat-driven pulling arm action in place of a tricep push
**For:** pitchers who push the elbow into release; credited to a Ron Wolforth Combat Pitcher drill; 2x10 reps daily
