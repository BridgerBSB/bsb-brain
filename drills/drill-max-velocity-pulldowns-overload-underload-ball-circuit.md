# Max velocity pulldowns (overload/underload ball circuit)

**Setup:** crow-hop pulldown throws moving from a 7 oz overload ball through standard baseballs down to a 2 oz underload ball, each weight thrown once at roughly 80 percent and once at 100 percent effort
**Builds:** max-effort arm speed the description claims transfers to mound velocity
**For:** an experienced pitcher (Casey Weathers) in an off-season velocity block, not touching a mound until late November
