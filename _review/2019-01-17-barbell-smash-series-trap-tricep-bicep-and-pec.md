# Barbell Smash Series (Trap, Tricep, Bicep, and Pec)

## TL;DR
- Tread demonstrates four self-myofascial "smash" techniques (upper trap, tricep, bicep, pec) using a barbell, a ball, and a voodoo band, meant as solo tissue work for pitchers.
- Dosing guidance: press into the tissue to a 4-6 out of 10 pain level, then work each tender spot for roughly 3-5 minutes per side (2-4 for the pec).
- The pec smash progresses in load from 5 lb up to about 20 lb, the point at which the drill can be dropped from a daily frequency.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/N4oJj53p4XU?t=0) Upper trap smash is framed as a corrective to add before mobility work; test overhead shoulder flexion before and after to gauge the change.
- [01:02](https://youtu.be/N4oJj53p4XU?t=62) Setup: voodoo-band a 3 oz ball (baseball or lacrosse ball) to a barbell, squat under it, and press into the tissue just lateral to the neck to a 4-6/10 pain level, then move through shoulder flexion and opposite-side neck lateral flexion, 3-5 minutes per side.
- [03:07](https://youtu.be/N4oJj53p4XU?t=187) Tricep smash: sink body weight into the tricep against a racked barbell, work through elbow flexion/extension and internal/external rotation, moving down the muscle, 3-5 minutes per side.
- [04:09](https://youtu.be/N4oJj53p4XU?t=249) Bicep smash: use the barbell collar (bar alone, or up to 5-10 lb), starting high on the bicep and rolling down to the tender spots, 3-5 minutes per side.
- [05:10](https://youtu.be/N4oJj53p4XU?t=310) Pec smash: wrap a ball to the end of the barbell starting at 5 lb, place it in the pec minor, control the bar with the opposite arm; add weight as tissue adapts, and once about 20 lb is tolerable without extreme pain the daily frequency can be reduced.
- [06:11](https://youtu.be/N4oJj53p4XU?t=371) From the tender pec spot, take the elbow to about 90 degrees and work external rotation, then progress to overhead pressing as it loosens, 2-4 minutes per side, prioritizing the throwing arm.

## Drills
- **Upper Trap Barbell Smash** - baseball/lacrosse ball voodoo-banded to a barbell, squat under it and press into the tissue lateral to the neck while moving through shoulder flexion and neck lateral flexion; builds overhead shoulder range of motion; for pitchers with upper trap tightness or overhead impingement feel.
- **Tricep Barbell Smash** - body weight sunk into the tricep against a racked barbell, worked through flexion/extension and rotation down the length of the muscle; builds tricep tissue quality and range.
- **Bicep Barbell Smash** - barbell collar rolled down the bicep from proximal to distal, weight added as tolerated (5-10 lb); builds bicep tissue quality.
- **Pec Barbell Smash** - ball voodoo-banded to the end of a weighted barbell (progressing from 5 lb toward 20 lb), placed in the pec minor with the elbow taken through external rotation and overhead pressing; builds pec and anterior shoulder tissue quality and overhead range; for throwing arm priority.
