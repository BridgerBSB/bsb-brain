# Thomas Test - Applications in Baseball

## TL;DR
- Runs the Thomas Test on clients over 16 to catch a tight rectus femoris on the pitcher's stride leg, not just the classic fixed hip-flexion deformity it was designed for.
- Frames the delivery itself as the cause: the posting leg's hip adductors drive a closed sideways stride, then the stride leg straightens fast and takes the pitcher's full weight, building a left/right hip flexor imbalance.
- Prescribes foam rolling (self-myofascial release) as the fix, tying tight hip flexors to weak glutes, lower squat output, and lost velocity, with no protocol or dosage given.

## Claims
- Thomas Test - Applications in Baseball - Driveline performs the Thomas Test on clients over age 16; beyond the traditional check for a fixed flexion deformity of the hip, they use it on baseball players specifically to test for a tight rectus femoris, since the stride leg works single-leg-dominant (left leg for a RHP).
- Thomas Test - Applications in Baseball - During the lead leg block phase, the stride leg straightens quickly and bears all the pitcher's bodyweight, after having been driven sideways and closed to the target by the posting leg's hip adductors.
- Thomas Test - Applications in Baseball - The delivery typically leaves the posting leg with tighter hip flexors and the stride leg with a tighter rectus femoris; tight hip flexors are described as going with weak glutes, which can cause postural problems, lowered power output in lifts like the squat, diminished velocity, higher injury susceptibility, and general discomfort.
- Thomas Test - Applications in Baseball - Prolonged sitting (school desks, office chairs) is named as a contributing cause of the same hip flexor tightness.
- Thomas Test - Applications in Baseball - Driveline teaches self-myofascial release (foam rolling) to loosen the hip flexors, regain glute strength, and relax fascia.
- Thomas Test - Applications in Baseball - Driveline offers a $25 screening session that checks for muscle imbalances and postural problems and produces a personalized workout plan.
