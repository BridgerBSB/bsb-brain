# What They Don't Tell You About College Baseball Recruiting

## TL;DR
- Roster math, not talent, is squeezing high schoolers out: of the 40 top-10 Washington recruits from 2021-2024, only 4 played significant D1 freshman innings and 39% left their first school by sophomore year.
- Coaches recruit on measured skill (velocity, exit velocity, pop time, 60 time) because only 8.8% of high schoolers play any NCAA baseball, so HS stats don't compare across states.
- The new NCAA "5-for-5" eligibility model (5 seasons in a 5-year window, effective for anyone first enrolling fall 2027 or later) rewards starting the clock in juco rather than waiting.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=0) Tracked all 40 PBR Washington Top 10 recruits, 2021-2024: 7 signed pro out of HS, 2 went juco, 31 enrolled D1; of those 31, only 4 played significant freshman innings and 12 (39%) left their original school by sophomore year.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=65) Per Baseball America, average D1 player age rose from 20.1 (2022) to 21.9 (2024); freshmen accounted for barely 15% of D1 innings.
- [01:05](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=65) Three forces reshaping recruiting: the transfer portal (6,255 entrants in 2025), roster limits (D1 cut from ~40 to 34 spring roster spots, full effect starting 2026-2027), and rising average age.
- [02:10](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=130) Only 8.8% of HS players reach any NCAA level (2.7% D1, 2.6% D2, 3.5% D3), so coaches weight measured skills like velocity, exit velocity, pop time, and 60 time over HS stats.
- [03:12](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=192) D1 coaches cannot initiate or respond to recruit contact before August 1 of junior year; official visits open September 1 of junior year; silence before that date is not rejection.
- [04:13](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=253) A recruiting email subject line should lead with class year, position, top velocity, exit velocity, and 60 time; video should show real delivery/swing mechanics, not a highlight reel with music.
- [07:21](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=441) A free video of a real number can earn showcase invites without paying for feeder events; presenter's son got his first invite off a gym video posted to social media.
- [10:26](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=626) D1 scholarships are now headcount-based, so all 34 roster spots can be fully funded, but programs aren't required to fund all of them; revenue sharing for baseball comes only after football and basketball.
- [12:36](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=756) Recruiting emails should name one specific real reason for interest, list metrics, link video, and ask each coach the same four questions (role fit, recent transfers/drafts, development tools, development plan).
- [13:37](https://youtu.be/irF0epAy26k?t=817) New NCAA "5-for-5" model (adopted June 2026): 5 seasons of competition in a 5-year window, starting at first college enrollment or the academic year after turning 19; applies to anyone first enrolling fall 2027 or later; juco players now retain 3 years of eligibility post-juco versus 2 under the old rules.

## Evidence cited
- Driveline's internal tracking of all 40 PBR Washington Top 10 recruits, 2021-2024.
- Baseball America reporting on average D1 player age (20.1 in 2022 to 21.9 in 2024) and freshman share of innings (~15%).
- NCAA/industry figures: 6,255 transfer portal entrants in 2025; D1 roster limit change from ~40 to 34; HS-to-NCAA participation rates (8.8% overall, 2.7%/2.6%/3.5% by division).
- Presenter's son's showcase invite sourced from a free gym video, cited as a personal example.

## Open questions
- The presenter says juco-then-transfer usually beats a D1 redshirt, but also says a D1 redshirt "has actual value" in the right program, without saying how to tell which situation applies.
- The read that "5-for-5 punishes waiting, not juco" is presented as settled, but the presenter notes pending antitrust litigation could still change the rules.
