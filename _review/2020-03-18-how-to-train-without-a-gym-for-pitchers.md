# How to Train Without a Gym for Pitchers

## TL;DR
- General no-weight-room template for pitchers: a 3-day (total body) or 4-day (2 upper, 2 lower) split hitting each major muscle group twice per week.
- Gives substitution exercises across hip, quad, lateral, push, pull, core, and arm-care categories, each with a way to add difficulty without a bar or plates (tempo, isometrics, reactivity, unilateral loading).
- Argues strength loss during a 2 to 6 week layoff is minor and returns quickly, so the bigger risk in this window is mobility regression, not strength loss.

## Claims
- [01:13](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=73) Over a 2 to 6 week layoff without weight room access, muscle and strength loss is not significant because training residuals last a long time.
- [02:14](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=134) Even a 5 lb loss and a 10% drop in strength numbers returns quickly, within a couple good weeks back in the gym, via muscle memory.
- [03:15](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=195) Recommends a 3-day (total body) or 4-day (2 upper, 2 lower) split, hitting each major muscle group twice per week.
- [04:15](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=255) Advanced/pro athletes should emphasize power variations of exercises; less advanced athletes should emphasize building a general strength base first.
- [11:20](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=680) Contrast sets: pair a strength movement (e.g. leg curl) immediately with its power equivalent (e.g. broad jump) for potentiation into the power movement.
- [12:23](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=743) Bulgarian split squat isometric holds at the bottom position can run 30 to 60 seconds.
- [15:26](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=926) Frontal-plane strength is important for the throwing motion; throwers commonly have tight, "wound up" adductors.
- [20:33](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=1233) Arm care is divided into three categories: serratus anterior work, lower trap work, and posterior cuff work.
- [23:34](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=1414) Forearm work is done as a six-wave-form series: wrist flexion, wrist extension, pronation, supination, radial deviation, ulnar deviation.
- [28:39](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=1719) During reduced training, eat at caloric maintenance; don't try to cut significant weight (e.g. 10 lbs in 4 weeks) if training stimulus isn't strong enough to support the cut.

## Cues
- **"Pull yourself down into the hole and then explode as fast as you can"** - fixes a slow eccentric-to-concentric transition; for reactive Bulgarian split squats and bar/plyo push-ups
- **"Steer lis (sic) tilt the pelvis, squeeze the glutes, and brace the abs hard"** - fixes low-back compensation in advanced plank variations; general population

## Anti-cues
- none

## Drills
- **Leg curl on rolling chair/slider** - single-leg or bilateral leg curl using an office chair or socks on a wood floor; builds hamstring strength without equipment; for hip-dominant work
- **Broad jump contrast set** - broad jump (flat or up stairs) done immediately after a strength movement; builds hip power via post-activation potentiation
- **Bulgarian split squat** - backpack-loaded, with slow eccentrics, 30-60s isometric holds, or a reactive drop-and-explode variation; builds quad strength/power and single-leg stability; for throwers
- **High step-down** - single-leg squat stepping down from a high box or counter; builds ankle mobility and eccentric quad/glute control
- **Cossack lunge** - deep lateral lunge, backpack-loaded or with an isometric pause; builds adductor mobility and lateral strength; for throwers with tight adductors
- **Manual resisted hip abduction** - partner applies resistance to side-lying hip abduction; builds glute medius strength for frontal-plane stability in throwing
- **Bar/plyo push-up** - push-up with a fast eccentric-to-concentric reversal, hands leaving the ground; builds upper-body power; paired with heavy push-up sets as a contrast set
- **Assisted single-arm push-up** - one arm carries most of the load, the other assists for balance; builds unilateral pressing strength without external weight
- **Advanced plank / body saw** - lengthened-lever plank or a sliding body saw on socks; builds anterior core strength with a posterior pelvic tilt and glute squeeze
- **Doorway posterior cuff isometric** - external rotation hold at 90/90 against a doorway; builds posterior cuff strength/arm care with no bands needed

## Evidence cited
- Tread's internal numbers: over 650 pitchers trained since 2015, 27+ athletes drafted since 2017. [29:40](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=1780)

## Open questions
- Claims minimal strength loss over a "two to six week" layoff and quick regain via "muscle memory," but cites no study or data behind that timeline. [01:13](https://youtu.be/-RgicqHSr7I?t=73)
