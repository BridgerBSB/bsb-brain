# Upgrading Your J-Band Routine Part 2b: Patterning - Fixing a "Pushing" Arm Action

## TL;DR
- Teaches a low-load J-Band drill, adapted from Ron Wolf's fourth drill, to convert a "pushing" arm action into a pulling one.
- High-level throwers pull the elbow into release with the pecs and lats, letting the arm lag slightly behind shoulder rotation; pushers lead with the elbow and finish with the tricep.
- The coach says the pattern change alone can put a pitcher "into better positions right away," even ones who are tight through the shoulder.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/4CBdShFhn0Y?t=0) High level throwers pull the ball to release, firing from the pecs and lats with a pulling sensation, versus pushers who let the elbow get out front of shoulder rotation and finish the throw with a push.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/4CBdShFhn0Y?t=0) Some pitchers fix the pushing pattern just from verbal cues; others cannot because they have thrown with the tricep their whole life and need additional patterning drills.
- [01:00](https://youtu.be/4CBdShFhn0Y?t=60) The drill is adapted from Ron Wolf's fourth drill, which the coach first saw around 2007-2008.
- [01:00](https://youtu.be/4CBdShFhn0Y?t=60) Setup: split stance, band on the throwing arm only (not the glove arm), elbow held at 90 degrees and shoulder height, working toward max external rotation and horizontal abduction until there is close to no band resistance, then a hair past that point.
- [02:00](https://youtu.be/4CBdShFhn0Y?t=120) The drill should not be fatiguing; it is meant to build the feel of the arm lagging into horizontal abduction and pulling the elbow to release with the pec and lat instead of pushing the ball at the target.
- [02:00](https://youtu.be/4CBdShFhn0Y?t=120) The coach has seen pitchers go straight from this drill into a pivot pickoff drill and change how the arm moves and which muscles fire through release.
- [03:03](https://youtu.be/4CBdShFhn0Y?t=183) Even pitchers who are tight with "medicine restrictions" (sic) can often be put into better positions right away just by changing the recruitment pattern.

## Cues
- **"Pull the elbow into release with the pec and lat, don't push the ball into your target"** - fixes a pushing arm action where the elbow gets out front of shoulder rotation; for tricep-dominant throwers.

## Drills
- **J-Band Ron Wolf 4th drill (pulling pattern)** - split stance, band on throwing arm only, elbow at 90 degrees and shoulder height, work to near end-range external rotation/horizontal abduction with light band tension, then pull the elbow to release; builds a pulling (pec/lat) arm action in place of a pushing (tricep) one; for pitchers who throw with the tricep and can't fix the pattern from cues alone; low-load and non-fatiguing.
