# Proposed hitting faults

Grouped from 99 cue/drill fault strings in the **hitting** domain only.
Nothing has been filed. This is a proposal to read and correct.

## Standing Up Early to Lift the Ball
**Is:** The hitter extends the pelvis and rises out of posture early, especially against pitches down in the zone, instead of staying down through the ball.
**Looks like:** The belt line tilts up and the spine straightens before contact, like the hitter is trying to golf the pitch.
- `cue-belt-buckle-down-towards-the-ground` - Belt buckle down towards the ground
- `cue-turn-your-body-like-you-re-going-to-hit-a-ground-ball` - Turn your body like you're going to hit a ground ball
- `drill-feet-in-cement-rotation` - Feet-in-cement rotation
- `drill-step-behind` - Step-behind

## Squatting or Arm-Driven Load
**Is:** The load is created by squatting down or bending at the arms rather than hinging properly at the hips, leaving it shallow and disconnected from the legs.
**Looks like:** The hitter sinks straight down like a squat, or the "load" shows up mostly in the hands with little hip movement.
- `cue-create-a-little-hinge-in-your-load-not-a-massive-one` - Create a little hinge in your load, not a massive one
- `cue-tilt-your-belt-buckle-down-towards-the-ground` - Tilt your belt buckle down towards the ground

## Chest Leaning Out Over the Plate
**Is:** The upper body bends forward and to the side instead of staying tall through the hinge, collapsing spine angle toward the pitcher.
**Looks like:** Shoulders and chest drift out over the front foot, and posture flattens instead of staying upright.
- `cue-don-t-be-afraid` - Don't be afraid
- `cue-stay-just-over-the-ball` - Stay just over the ball
- `drill-step-behind-load` - Step-behind load
- `drill-step-in-load` - Step-in load
- `drill-kershaw-barrel-load-handle-load-kick-out` - Kershaw (barrel load / handle load, kick-out)
- `drill-no-stride-short-bat` - No-stride short bat
- `drill-short-bat` - Short bat

## Drifting or Rocking Forward Out of the Load
**Is:** Weight rocks or drifts toward the pitcher during the load instead of coiling back into the rear hip and leg.
**Looks like:** Center of mass creeps forward before rotation starts, or the front leg cheats over the back leg.
- `cue-keep-your-center-mass-in-front-of-your-back-foot-never` - Keep your center mass in front of your back foot, never over it
- `cue-pick-it-up-and-let-it-happen-naturally` - Pick it up and let it happen naturally
- `cue-throw-that-ass-back` - Throw that ass back
- `drill-walk-in-long-flow-bat` - Walk-in, long flow bat
- `drill-kershaw-into-hook-em` - Kershaw into hook'em

## Reaching for the Ground Out of the Leg Kick
**Is:** The hitter reaches the lifted leg toward the ground and pops up out of the leg kick instead of holding it and rotating out of it.
**Looks like:** The front foot stabs down early and the back hip loses its loaded position before rotation begins.
- `cue-keep-that-foot-in-the-air-and-then-rotate-out-of-it` - Keep that foot in the air and then rotate out of it
- `cue-your-front-leg-is-how-you-stay-back` - Your front leg is how you stay back
- `drill-big-papi` - Big papi

## Hips Opening Before the Upper Body
**Is:** The lower half fires and opens before the upper body and hands are ready, losing the back hip too soon.
**Looks like:** The front hip and belt line rotate toward the pitcher well before the hands start forward.
- `cue-keep-your-hips-level-keep-them-down-the-whole-time` - Keep your hips level, keep them down the whole time
- `cue-push-your-back-hip-as-far-back-as-possible` - Push your back hip as far back as possible
- `cue-push-your-back-hip-back-as-your-first-move` - Push your back hip back as your first move

- `drill-underload-bat-back-hip-pull` - Underload bat back-hip pull
- `drill-step-back-no-stride-oppo-angle` - Step-back no stride, oppo angle

## Rotating as One Piece (No Separation)
**Is:** Torso and pelvis rotate together instead of the pelvis leading and the torso lagging, so the barrel gets guided rather than whipped through the zone.
**Looks like:** Hands and hips arrive at the same time, and the barrel gets steered toward center field instead of accelerating through it.
- `cue-catch-it-deep-and-still-pull-home` - Catch it deep and still pull home
- `drill-big-poppy` - Big poppy

## Hands Taking Over the Swing
**Is:** The hands push or steer the barrel out and around instead of letting body rotation deliver it, producing a pull-heavy or hooking swing.
**Looks like:** The barrel casts away from the body early, and contact drifts to the pull side even on pitches away.

## Catching the Ball Too Deep or Too Shallow
**Is:** The contact point relative to the body is inconsistent, producing a narrow, pull-oriented path and unreliable attack angle.
**Looks like:** Contact point wanders swing to swing; balls that should be driven the other way get pulled foul, or contact feels jammed.
- `cue-keep-this-closed-a-tiny-bit-longer` - Keep this closed a tiny bit longer
- `cue-keep-your-back-heel-on-the-back-of-the-mat-and-get-the` - Keep your back heel on the back of the mat and get the barrel to the front of the mat
- `cue-turn-behind-the-plate-catch-it-as-deep-as-possible` - Turn behind [the plate], catch it as deep as possible
- `drill-offset-open` - Offset open
- `drill-offset-open-step-behind` - Offset open step-behind
- `drill-offset-open-with-handle-loaded-bat` - Offset-open with handle-loaded bat

## Releasing the Back Foot Too Early
**Is:** The back foot comes off the ground before rotation finishes, leaking ground force before it transfers into the swing.
**Looks like:** The back heel lifts or slides early, and the swing loses push off the rear leg.
- `cue-feet-in-cement` - Feet in cement

## Rushing the Load Into Rotation
**Is:** The hitter moves out of the loaded position into rotation too quickly, without holding it long enough to sequence the swing.
**Looks like:** Load and turn blur together with no pause; stride and rotation happen at once instead of separately.

## Muscling the Swing for Power
**Is:** The hitter tenses up and tries to force bat speed with extra muscular effort instead of trusting rotation and mechanics.
**Looks like:** The swing looks tight and effortful on pitches the hitter feels they should crush, and the path flattens out.
- `cue-don-t-let-your-brain-get-convinced-this-thing-is-light` - Don't let your brain get convinced this thing is light, it is just a bat
- `cue-just-trust-the-same-swing` - Just trust the same swing
- `cue-let-it-soak` - Let it soak
- `cue-nice-and-easy` - Nice and easy

## Tentative, Undercommitted Turn
**Is:** The hitter holds back or hesitates instead of committing fully to the turn, out of fear of missing or getting beat.
**Looks like:** A short, cautious-looking turn, low effort on reps meant to be full-speed, or the swing decelerates and blocks extension out front.
- `cue-just-focus-on-moving-as-fast-as-you-can` - Just focus on moving as fast as you can
- `cue-let-it-get-to-a-point-where-you-think-you-re-going-to` - Let it get to a point where you think you're going to break your bat, then swing 82 miles an hour
- `cue-let-it-turn-commit-to-an-early-miss-let-it-get-out-there` - Let it turn, commit to an early miss, let it get out there
- `cue-miss-by-over-rotating-like-a-madman` - Miss by over rotating like a [ __ ] madman

## Overly Vertical Bat Path
**Is:** The bat angle gets too steep everywhere in the zone instead of matching pitch height, or the swing becomes vertical/chopping instead of rotational.
**Looks like:** A steep, up-and-down path on pitches that don't call for it, sometimes with low hands producing popups or fouls.
- `cue-turn-to-the-top-inside-part-of-the-ball` - Turn to the top-inside part of the ball
- `drill-offset-open-poppy-with-stride` - Offset-open poppy with stride
- `drill-offset-open-stride-underload-bat` - Offset-open stride, underload bat
- `drill-poppy-with-handle-loader` - Poppy with handle-loader

## Breaking Down the Front Foot on Landing
**Is:** The front foot lands stiffly and points or collapses instead of touching down soft and stable.
**Looks like:** The front foot turns or breaks at landing rather than planting flat and quiet.
- `cue-land-soft-like-you-re-landing-on-an-egg` - Land soft, like you're landing on an egg
