## Hip-Flexor Squat Instead of a True Hip Hinge
**Is:** The hitter loads by squatting/bending at the knees and hip flexors rather than hinging the pelvis, leaving a shallow, arm-driven load with no real turning space created.
**Looks like:** Load looks upright and knee-bendy instead of tilted; belt buckle stays pointed out rather than down, and the chest sits flat instead of tall.
- `cue-belt-buckle-down-towards-the-ground` - Belt buckle down towards the ground
- `cue-tilt-your-belt-buckle-down-towards-the-ground` - Tilt your belt buckle down towards the ground
- `cue-create-a-little-hinge-in-your-load-not-a-massive-one` - Create a little hinge in your load, not a massive one
- `drill-feet-in-cement-rotation` - Feet-in-cement rotation
- `drill-kershaw-barrel-load-handle-load-kick-out` - Kershaw (barrel load / handle load, kick-out)
- `drill-step-behind` - Step-behind
- `drill-step-back-no-stride` - Step-back no stride

## Chest Drifting Forward / Losing Posture in the Load
**Is:** The upper body leans or drifts forward over the front side during the load instead of staying centered over the back leg.
**Looks like:** Chest tips out over the plate, shoulders get ahead of the hips, posture rounds forward rather than staying tall.
- `cue-stay-just-over-the-ball` - Stay just over the ball
- `drill-step-behind-load` - Step-behind load
- `drill-step-in-load` - Step-in load
- `drill-no-stride-short-bat` - No-stride short bat

## Bat Angle Doesn't Adjust to Pitch Height
**Is:** The hitter can't get the barrel to match the plane of the pitch — either over-bending to try to lift low pitches or forcing one steep angle regardless of location.
**Looks like:** Barrel stays flat/blocked on pitches down in the zone, or comes in steeply steep on everything, including pitches up.
- `cue-belt-buckle-down-towards-the-ground` - Belt buckle down towards the ground
- `cue-don-t-be-afraid` - Don't be afraid
- `cue-turn-to-the-top-inside-part-of-the-ball` - Turn to the top-inside part of the ball

## Pelvis Drifting or Extending Forward Off the Back Side
**Is:** The hips slide or thrust toward the pitcher — either as a general forward drift out of the load, or specifically as an early pelvis extension ("turning up") when trying to lift the ball.
**Looks like:** Center of mass slides onto the front foot early, or the hitter's hips pop open/rise up while chasing a fly ball, front leg cheating over the back leg.
- `cue-keep-your-center-mass-in-front-of-your-back-foot-never` - Keep your center mass in front of your back foot, never over it
- `cue-pick-it-up-and-let-it-happen-naturally` - Pick it up and let it happen naturally
- `cue-turn-your-body-like-you-re-going-to-hit-a-ground-ball` - Turn your body like you're going to hit a ground ball

- `drill-kershaw-into-hook-em` - Kershaw into hook'em

## Swaying Over the Back Knee
**Is:** The hitter collapses or leans laterally over the rear leg instead of staying stacked and behind the ball.
**Looks like:** Upper body tips away from the plate over the back knee rather than holding a centered posture.
- `drill-underload-back-shoulder-work` - Underload back-shoulder work

## Front Foot Breaks Down at Landing

**Looks like:** Front foot opens or breaks instead of landing flat and stable.
