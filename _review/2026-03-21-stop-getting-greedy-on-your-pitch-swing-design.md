# Stop Getting Greedy on Your Pitch (Swing Design)

## TL;DR
- A constraint-drill progression to separate load from stride in a hitter whose hands take over and cast the barrel out and around when he isn't swinging at full effort.
- Alternates long bat and underload/lighter bat to expose upper-body cheating that a heavier implement hides.
- Central fault: sequencing collapses below 100% swing intensity because load and stride happen together; the fix is a pivot-pick progression that finishes the load before the stride starts.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=0) Jacoby's sequencing breaks down when he takes intensity below 100%; his load and stride happen together and his hands take over.
- [00:28](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=28) Offset close with speed trainers is meant to get the pelvis fully open before the upper body catches up; relying on the torso or arms to get the head out creates a long path.
- [02:02](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=122) Even with mishits, offset close forces rotation into a tighter path and works as a warmup before higher-intent swings.
- [02:58](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=178) Jacoby's center of mass shifted during his load and he never found a stable position, so the session moved to drills that separate load from forward move.
- [02:58](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=178) Offset open pivot pick hybrid: coil into a full load, then pick the front foot up and move forward, to separate load and stride.
- [05:10](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=310) Alternating long bat and underload bat into a pivot-pick "hook 'em"; Jacoby struggled to hold the static position given his natural rhythm, calling it more a mental challenge than physical.
- [06:11](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=371) Hitters who over-focus on creating direction let their hands take over and guide the barrel out and around instead of rotating and sequencing naturally.
- [08:18](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=498) With the long bat Jacoby can hide arm/upper-body cheating because it's longer and heavier; switching to a lighter bat exposed him pulling across the ball instead of squaring it up.
- [09:21](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=561) On his "honey hole" pitch, Jacoby tends to get greedy and add extra effort, which drops him down and flattens his path, causing him to miss under it; the fix is trusting his normal swing.
- [11:22](https://youtu.be/F-od2QhIRGY?t=682) The catch-it-deep, pull-side drill let Jacoby create separation and turn the barrel with a clean path.

## Cues
- **"Stay vertical" / "stay tight"** - fixes an out-and-around bat path caused by over-focusing on direction; for Jacoby (a bat-speed-first hitter whose hands take over)
- **"Just trust the same swing"** - fixes getting greedy and adding extra effort on a hittable ("honey hole") pitch, which flattens the path
- **"Hold, hold, and go"** - fixes rushing the lower body into rotation instead of easing into it

## Drills
- **Offset close with speed trainers** - closed offset stance; builds pelvis opening fully before the upper body catches up, used as a warmup for lower-intent swings; for hitters who lead with arms/torso
- **Offset open pivot pick (hybrid)** - offset open stance with a back-foot pivot pick; coil into the load, then pick the front foot up and move forward; builds separating load from stride
- **Pivot pick into "hook 'em," long bat and underload bat** - pivot pick into a hook 'em position, alternating implements; builds holding a static loaded position without rushing; for athletic movers who struggle to come to a full stop
- **Catch it deep, pull side/home run** - set target at the pull-side corner, catch the pitch deep; builds staying inside the ball and vertical posture, prevents hands taking over
- **Pivot pick into a "poppy," long bat vs. Smash bat** - pivot pick into a small hand pump, alternating a long bat with a lighter Smash bat; builds separating and getting out of the load without arm cheating; exposes upper-body compensation when the implement gets lighter
