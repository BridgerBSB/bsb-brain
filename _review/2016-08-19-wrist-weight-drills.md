# Wrist Weight Drills

## TL;DR
- Wrist weight throws (up to 10 lb) train the arm's decelerators and build shoulder strength and endurance, and are framed as safer than throwing a baseball because arm speed is slower.
- The exercises encourage pronation and train the elbow spiral to stop an athlete from casting the forearm or pushing the ball.
- The Cuban press is named as the specific exercise for strengthening the posterior shoulder.

## Claims
- [00:15](https://youtu.be/rroYrqBO1MQ?t=15) Throwing a 10 pound wrist weight is safer than throwing a baseball because the arm speed is slower, applying less stress to the arm.
- [00:15](https://youtu.be/rroYrqBO1MQ?t=15) Wrist weights train the decelerators and build strength and endurance in the shoulder.
- [00:15](https://youtu.be/rroYrqBO1MQ?t=15) Wrist weight work encourages pronation and strengthens the posterior shoulder through exercises like the Cuban press.
- [00:15](https://youtu.be/rroYrqBO1MQ?t=15) Wrist weights train the elbow spiral, which deters an athlete from casting out the forearm or pushing the ball.
