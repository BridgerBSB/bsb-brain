# Luis Patiño Is Back.

## TL;DR
- Luis Patiño, recovering from an April 2024 Tommy John surgery and a re-aggravated shoulder at the 2026 World Baseball Classic, threw a bullpen at Tread that staff called a top-five session for the facility, topping out at 97 mph.
- His velocity climbed from 90-91 mph three months earlier to 95 on his first outing back and 96-97 in recent weeks, which he credits to full-time work with Tread coach John.
- This is a comeback/confidence story, not an instructional video: no mechanical cues, drills, or program details are given.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=0) Patiño says his confidence is back and he is enjoying the game again.
- [03:17](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=197)-[04:18](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=258) Bullpen radar call-outs climb through the mid-90s to 97 mph, with staff noting a consistent slider shape.
- [06:25](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=385) Staff call it a "top five bullpen at Tread ever," topping out at 97 mph with a consistent slider.
- [06:25](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=385) Per the video description, Patiño had Tommy John surgery in April 2024 and dealt with lingering shoulder issues through his 2025 season in the Padres system.
- [06:25](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=385) Patiño says he started working with coach John (Tread) in January after his shoulder began bothering him during winter ball.
- [07:26](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=446) At the 2026 World Baseball Classic he touched 94 mph but got hurt again, leaving his shoulder "really bad" afterward.
- [07:26](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=446) He says Jose Quintana urged him post-WBC to "go [to] Tread, working with John," calling Quintana "like my dad."
- [07:26](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=446) Three months before this session he was sitting 90-91 mph; his first outing back he hit 95, and in recent weeks he has thrown 96-97.
- [08:26](https://youtu.be/0Q8p_3B7az0?t=506) Patiño says pitchers overthink after surgery, and his current goal is to enjoy playing and show he is healthy rather than chase a hard return timeline.
