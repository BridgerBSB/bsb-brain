# How We Helped This 95 MPH Big-League Sidearmer CUT HIS ERA IN HALF

## TL;DR
- BPC took Grant Anderson's delivery from a crossfire drift back to landing in line with the catcher, restoring the velocity and vertical approach angle on his four-seam.
- They rebuilt his pitch mix from a two-pitch, top-of-zone approach (39% sweeper / 32% four-seam in 2024) to a three-pitch mix (40% sweeper / 28% four-seam / 23% sinker in 2025).
- They reshaped his sweeper from a rising shape (+5 vert / 7 horz) to a true sweeper (2 vert / 15 horz) to open the bottom of the zone; Anderson has cut his ERA in half since.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=0) Anderson debuted in 2023 (2.2 IP, 7 K), then ran about a 5.00 ERA early in 2024 and finished that year at an 8.10 ERA over 26 innings, allowing 11 home runs.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=0) He was DFA'd by the Texas Rangers two weeks into the offseason and claimed by the Milwaukee Brewers.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=61) In 2023 he landed in line with the catcher, which let him use his four-seam's vertical approach angle at the top of the zone; by late 2024 he had drifted into a crossfire delivery chasing sinker deception, which cost him four-seam velocity.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=61) The early-offseason focus was getting him back in line mechanically so he could rotate through the catcher and command the four-seam.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=61) In 2024 his usage was 39% sweeper and 32% four-seam, both mostly up in the zone, which let hitters sit on them.
- [02:02](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=122) The 2025 plan cut four-seam usage overall but kept it for two-strike counts and finishing up, added sinker in to righties, kept the sweeper away to righties, and brought the changeup back early in counts to lefties.
- [02:02](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=122) 2025 usage became 40% sweeper, 28% four-seam, 23% sinker.
- [03:07](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=187) The sweeper shape changed from +5 vertical / 7 horizontal (rising) in 2024 to 2 vertical / 15 horizontal (true sweeper) in 2025, built with the Brewers' analytics department to open the bottom of the zone for chase and in-zone whiffs.
- [03:07](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=187) The new sweeper now pairs with the sinker and four-seam, used to seal strikes to lefties and put away righties, and is his most-used and most successful pitch.
- [03:07](https://youtu.be/U7Ah61uhaM8?t=187) Anderson has cut his ERA in half, raised his strikeout rate, and lowered his walk rate in 2025 versus 2024.
