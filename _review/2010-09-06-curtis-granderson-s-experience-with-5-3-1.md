# Curtis Granderson's Experience with 5/3/1

## TL;DR
- Blog profiles Curtis Granderson's offseason training at Driveline during his move from the Tigers to the Yankees, built around the 5/3/1 strength system and Prowler sled sprints.
- Granderson is held up as a model of work ethic who trained hard alongside Driveline's other athletes and pushed them to work harder too.
- The post cites his 2010 advanced fielding numbers, DRS +6 and UZR +14.9, and 3.2 WAR (Fangraphs) as evidence the season was better than his batting average suggested.

## Claims
- Curtis Granderson's Experience with 5/3/1: Granderson trained at Driveline the winter he moved from the Detroit Tigers to the New York Yankees.
- Curtis Granderson's Experience with 5/3/1: he had previously used a workout given to him by his pro strength coaches, but this offseason said he needed to get stronger and faster and would do whatever it took.
- Curtis Granderson's Experience with 5/3/1: his program included the 5/3/1 system and short Prowler sprints.
- Curtis Granderson's Experience with 5/3/1: in 2010 he posted DRS +6, UZR +14.9, and 3.2 WAR per Fangraphs, in the AL East.
