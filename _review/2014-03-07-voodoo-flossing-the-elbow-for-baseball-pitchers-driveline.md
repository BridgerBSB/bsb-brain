# Voodoo Flossing the Elbow for Baseball Pitchers | Driveline Baseball

## TL;DR
- Kyle Boddy demonstrates wrapping the elbow tightly with a Rogue compression ("voodoo floss") band, then moving it through flexion/extension and internal/external rotation, as a quick post-throwing recovery routine for cutting down acute elbow swelling and "hot elbow."
- The wrap goes on right after throwing, the athlete works through a set sequence of ranges while wrapped, then it comes off; the whole demo runs about 5 minutes, and Boddy says to work quickly since the wrap cuts off blood flow.
- The routine borrows from Kelly Starrett's *Becoming a Supple Leopard* and targets flexion-extension ROM, forcing toward full extension, internal/external rotation, and, some days, elbow distraction or a valgus stress test.

## Claims
- [00:01](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=1) Presents this as a "quick post throwing recovery routine" for cutting down swelling in the elbow using Rogue Fitness compression/flossing bands, citing material from Kelly Starrett's *Becoming a Supple Leopard*.
- [00:01](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=1) Wraps from the bottom of the bicep across the medial/lateral elbow into the forearm, with tension increasing over the top of the elbow.
- [01:03](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=63) After wrapping, does elbow flexion/extension (not full extension yet) "to really get out the swelling," then internal/external rotation with the forearm propped at 90 degrees, emphasizing external rotation range.
- [02:04](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=124) Adds internal rotation (hand driving toward the ground), pulls into full extension for a couple seconds, calls it painful because of the compression, then does elbow distraction (pulling on the wrist) and trigger-point compression on the medial forearm before unwrapping.
- [03:05](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=185) Notes handedness changes leverage: he can wrap right-handed arms tighter, but gets more leverage on lefties, "just due to how the anatomical structures work."
- [03:05](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=185) Repeats the sequence on a right-handed thrower: flexion/extension with some shoulder abduction, internal/external rotation focusing on external, then a "quick 90 degree stretch" without forcing range of motion.
- [04:06](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=246) Finishes with internal rotation (palm face down), an extension hold for a couple seconds, elbow distraction, and trigger-point work before unwrapping; notes resulting "tiger stripes" (bruising) that "goes away pretty quickly."
- [04:06](https://youtu.be/EVp8gbkwFT8?t=246) Lists the routine's main targets: flexion/extension ROM, forcing toward full extension, internal/external rotation, and "sometimes" elbow distraction or a valgus stress test, varying by day.
