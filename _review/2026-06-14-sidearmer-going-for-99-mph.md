# Sidearmer Going For 99 MPH

## TL;DR
- Dalton Mall lowered his arm slot to near-sidearm over six to eight weeks for arm and knee health, and held his velocity at 96-98 mph while filling the zone more.
- The lower slot didn't cost him shape: VAA on the fastball improved and the sweeper picked up vertical break, with the heater still averaging about 12 inches of vertical break.
- Command, called his "biggest question mark" historically, looked better in this session, though it's one live pen, not a study.

## Claims
- [00:03](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=3) Dalton had knee flare-ups in the off-season and only got clearance last week to start ramping intent back up.
- [00:03](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=3) Last week he sat 96-98 mph at roughly 90-95% effort; today is his first full-intent pen.
- [00:03](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=3) Over the past six to eight weeks he dropped his slot to more sidearm, partly for arm and knee health.
- [01:03](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=63) Since the change he's filling the zone more, and the coach says the sweeper now has more vertical break and the heater has better VAA.
- [03:08](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=188) A fastball read 97.9 mph after a release-height adjustment.
- [04:11](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=251) Even from the lower slot the fastball held about 12 inches of vertical break.
- [09:21](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=561) His last two heaters were 94 and 92.8 mph, but the day's average held 96-97; he says pre-change he was "97 plus."
- [11:22](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=682) Average release height for the session was about 4'5"-4'6", lower than the prior week, with velocity still 96-98.
- [11:22](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=682) The coach says command has historically been Dalton's biggest question mark and calls this session more dominant in the zone.
- [09:21](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=561) Dalton says he's more confident throwing his sweeper and cutter for strikes from the new slot than he ever was throwing his four-seam for strikes from over the top.

## Cues
- **"Scooping underneath it"** - cue for shaping an off-speed pitch that's finishing too high; for a sidearm pitcher working a cutter/slider.
- **"Half a beat later"** (delay when you add intent) - fixes rushing/adding effort too early in the delivery, which was causing yanked pitches; for a pitcher timing his intent.
- **"Rotate your right shoulder under your left shoulder"** - fixes losing posture during rotation; for a pitcher maintaining spine angle through release.

## Evidence cited
- Dalton Mall, live pen data: 96-98 mph from a lowered, more sidearm slot (release height ~4'5"-4'6"), improved VAA on the fastball and increased vertical break on the sweeper, after six to eight weeks in the new slot. [00:03](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=3), [11:22](https://youtu.be/39XXnEmO9hE?t=682)

## Open questions
- Improved command and health are attributed to the slot change, but this is one live pen with no baseline comparison or biomechanical data shown, so rest and effort-ramp confounds aren't separated out.
- "Better VAA" is asserted with no measured number for either slot, unlike the velocity and vertical-break figures given.
