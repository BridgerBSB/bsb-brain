# Dylan Bundy, Radar Guns, Self-Myofascial Release, News on Our Athletes (Q&A - May 10th)

## TL;DR
- Short Q&A covering radar gun choice, self-myofascial release (SMR) technique, and Driveline athlete progress updates from May 2012.
- Driveline moved from a $89 Bushnell (inconsistent, read low) to the Jugs Pro Sport, matching the Stalker Sport on price and accuracy; both are the MLB-endorsed guns.
- Joe Marsh threw 93/92/92 mph with a 3oz ball; Driveline projected 87-88 mph with a 5oz ball by his June 6 departure, revised up from an original 88 mph estimate.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=0) The Bushnell radar gun (~$89) was inconsistent and read velocity too low; Driveline switched to the Jugs Pro Sport, priced similarly to the Stalker Sport, both MLB-endorsed.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=61) SMR self-massage uses dense (black) foam rollers, not soft (white) ones, since the soft ones don't apply enough pressure; rolled areas are quads, IT band, TFL, calves, pecs, and thoracic spine.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=61) Never roll the lumbar spine directly over the sacrum.
- [02:02](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=122) "The stick" is used post-pitching to roll the medial/distal forearm and break up tight tissue; a compact version costs about $15-20.
- [02:02](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=122) Foam roller, stick, and resistance bands (SPRI or J-bands) are described as mandatory parts of the warmup.
- [02:02](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=122) Dylan Bundy uses punching bag work for rotator cuff deceleration training and rapid elbow extension; Driveline says it will "probably" start adding this to its program.
- [03:06](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=186) Jack Shiedeman won a Kinko shot put title at 56 feet and advanced to state; he is committed to the University of Washington for shot put.
- [03:06](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=186) Joe Marsh (UW freshman transferring to UC Davis) threw 93/92/92 mph with a 3oz ball; Driveline expected at least 87-88 mph with a 5oz ball at Friday's velocity reading, up from an original 88 mph estimate, ahead of a June 6 departure.
- [04:07](https://youtu.be/__Jm15gx30k?t=247) Read Martinez was hitting .346-.350, ranked top 10 among qualified NWAC batters, playing center field for Yakima Valley.
