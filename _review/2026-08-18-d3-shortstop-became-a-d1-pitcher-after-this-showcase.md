# D3 Shortstop Became A D1 Pitcher After This Showcase

## TL;DR
- Chase Donahue, a former D3 shortstop who only started pitching after a January wrist surgery, sat 90.5 mph (T91.2) with a flat -4.0° VAA fastball in a Transfer Portal Showcase live AB.
- The coaching staff treated zero walks as the best outcome of the outing, saying strike-throwing is the main separator in a showcase setting, not swings and misses.
- Less than a year and a half after first pitching, he committed to D1 North Alabama.

## Claims
- Description: Donahue sat 90.5 mph (T91.2) with a flat -4.0° VAA fastball that played up in the zone during his Transfer Portal Showcase live AB.
- Description: a January wrist surgery limited him to throwing only, so he began full-time remote training with Tread in early March; he committed to D1 North Alabama less than a year and a half after first pitching.
- [01:14](https://youtu.be/VanYIWlAShc?t=74) coach cues a breath between throws for timing and tells him not to steer the slider so much.
- [03:29](https://youtu.be/VanYIWlAShc?t=209) coach tells him to attack the zone and "induce the most swings out of anybody who throws today" rather than nibble.
- [07:37](https://youtu.be/VanYIWlAShc?t=457) coach says the outing had no walks and calls that the best thing he could do on a day like this, since evaluators are watching stuff and shapes and strike-throwing is the main separator.
- [08:38](https://youtu.be/VanYIWlAShc?t=518) coach says Donahue got too far away from his backside and rushed, and flags holding over the backside as the next step to rotate better and get the ball out front.

## Cues
- **"Don't control it so much"** - fixes steering/overcontrolling the breaking ball; for a pitcher working a bigger slider shape
- **"Take big bites"** - fixes a passive, nibbling approach in the zone; for a pitcher in a live AB/showcase setting
- **"Hang out over the backside, hold things back"** - fixes rushing and getting too far from the backside too early; for a pitcher working on rotation and getting the ball out front
