# Tim Lincecum - What Changed?

## TL;DR
- The post charts Lincecum's steadily dropping fastball velocity in 2010 versus his 2008-09 Cy Young seasons, alongside rising ERA and walks and falling strikeouts.
- It pairs release-point-synced video of the same windup, same inning, same park from June 12, 2009 and August 27, 2010 so readers can spot the mechanical change themselves.
- The author withholds his own diagnosis pending a colleague discussion, so the post ends without a stated mechanical cause.

## Claims
- (body) Lincecum's fastball velocity was steadily dropping through the 2010 season compared to his 2008 and 2009 Cy Young years.
- (body) His ERA and walks were up and strikeouts down in 2010 relative to those prior seasons.
- (body) The author states declining fastball velocity is a strong indicator of an impending injury; velocity loss is typically tied to shoulder trouble, while a steady loss of control tends to indicate elbow trouble, though either can involve both joints.
- (body) The comparison video syncs both clips to release point, both thrown from the windup in the first inning at AT&T Park: left clip June 12, 2009, right clip August 27, 2010.
- (comments) Readers proposed several mechanical differences: more shoulder/hip rotation away from home plate during the windup, an earlier heel lift off the rubber, a more closed landing, a lower arm slot with more forearm flyout, and less complete shoulder rotation through follow-through, which one commenter linked to an earlier, less efficient hip turn.
- (comments) Other readers disagreed on what they saw: one called the two clips close to identical apart from stirrups, one thought the arm looked slightly slower to vertical, and one thought the right-side clip looked rushed.
