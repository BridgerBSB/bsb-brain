# Glove Side Elevation Drill w/ Overload

## TL;DR
- Short demo of a glove-side stabilization drill built around 1 kg overload plyo balls thrown into a bucket.
- Progression runs from a baseball hold, to a baseball simulated throw, to a full baseball throw at a TAP Command Trainer.
- Purpose stated is keeping the glove side stabilized and closed properly into rotation.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/17n2I0Fgj-A?t=0) Drill uses 1 kg Green Driveline PlyoCare Balls thrown into a bucket instead of a baseball.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/17n2I0Fgj-A?t=0) Progression is baseball hold, then baseball simulated throw, then full baseball throw at a TAP Command Trainer.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/17n2I0Fgj-A?t=0) Drill is said to work on keeping the glove side stabilized and closed properly into rotation.
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/17n2I0Fgj-A?t=0) Drill is credited as inspired by Nick Pereira of Elite Velocity Pitching, modified by Driveline.
