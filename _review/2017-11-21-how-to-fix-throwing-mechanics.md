# How To Fix Throwing Mechanics

## TL;DR
- Mike Rathwell (Driveline CEO) describes how Driveline changes an athlete's mechanics: move through throws ordered from most constrained to least constrained.
- The method is to manipulate the environment so the athlete learns the correct movement in a constrained drill, then carries it into a less constrained one.
- Short clip (70 s) with no numbers, cues or study. It states a principle and names the drills on the ladder.

## Claims
- [00:01](https://youtu.be/Fv-AnF-wfH4?t=1) Driveline's "plowshare" (sic, likely plyo care) throws are ordered from most constrained to least constrained.
- [00:01](https://youtu.be/Fv-AnF-wfH4?t=1) The most constrained end is reverse throws and pivot pickups, which are done without the legs involved.
- [00:01](https://youtu.be/Fv-AnF-wfH4?t=1) The least constrained end is walking wind-ups, running throws and mound work, where the athlete has full degrees of freedom.
- [00:01](https://youtu.be/Fv-AnF-wfH4?t=1) For an athlete struggling with lead leg bracing, the coach manipulates the environment (the drill) rather than only describing the fault.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/Fv-AnF-wfH4?t=61) The goal is for the athlete to learn what they are doing right in one drill and have it carry over into the other drill.

## Drills
- **Reverse throw** - most constrained end of the ladder, no legs involved; builds the target pattern in isolation before adding the lower half; for athletes with a fault such as lead leg bracing
- **Pivot pickup** - most constrained end of the ladder, no legs involved; builds the target pattern in isolation; for the same use as reverse throws
- **Walking wind-up** - less constrained throw with a walking approach; carries the learned pattern into a fuller movement
- **Running throw** - less constrained throw with a run-up; carries the pattern over at higher speed
- **Mound work** - fully unconstrained pitching; the end point where the pattern should hold
