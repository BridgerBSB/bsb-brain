# 6'8" HS Vanderbilt Commit Goes For 96MPH In Pre Draft Bullpen | RJ Cope

## TL;DR
- Live, mic'd-up bullpen: Tread coach Paul Hall makes real-time mechanical and grip tweaks with RJ Cope ahead of MLB pre-draft workouts, capped at roughly 20 pitches to keep him fresh.
- Cues target cross-fire in the delivery, hand-speed consistency on the offspeed pitch, and a grip/seam adjustment to stop a pitch cutting into a rise.
- Framed as a success story (Academy Championship walk-off homer, Vanderbilt commitment); the title claims 96 mph but no radar number is confirmed on camera.

## Claims
- [00:02](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=2) Coach cues RJ to feel his chest move back and get compact coming up, working on cross-fire in the delivery.
- [01:05](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=65) RJ's team won the Academy Championship on a walk-off home run.
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=125) RJ has been traveling to MLB organization pre-draft workouts and threw a pen two days earlier, so this session is capped at about 20 pitches to keep him fresh.
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=125) Coach cues RJ to pick up his target a little sooner in his delivery.
- [03:06](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=186) Coach tells RJ to keep the same hand speed on his offspeed pitch so it gets "bite" late instead of slowing his arm to signal it.
- [04:08](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=248) Coach adjusts RJ's grip, splitting the seam and moving his fingers back because of his hand size, to stop the pitch pulling into a rise/side-spin shape.
- [05:08](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=308) Coach cues RJ to "drop" and "drive through the baseball" to finish his fastball, noting he stayed in the pitch better than before.
- [06:10](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=370) Coach says RJ held velocity well in season and is "on the right track," telling him to keep improving every time he comes back.
- Title: the video title states RJ touched 96 mph in this bullpen; the transcript gives no confirmed radar number, only a garbled "five nine" (sic) at [03:06](https://youtu.be/BQ75llArinI?t=186).

## Cues
- **"Feel the chest over and then get compact coming up"** - fixes cross-fire/spinning off in the delivery; for RJ Cope
- **"Keep that same hand speed"** - fixes an offspeed pitch that loses bite because the arm slows to tip it
- **"Split that seam" / "offset it"** - fixes a pitch pulling into a rise from side-spin; for pitchers with bigger hands
- **"Drop. Drive through the baseball."** - fixes a fastball that misses arm-side by not finishing through release
