# A Salute to Two Trainees: Jack and Eli

## TL;DR
- Two novice trainees, a pitcher and a thrower, made steady linear strength gains from basic compound lifts in a cramped garage gym.
- Top numbers: Eli deadlifted 425 lb at 169.5 lb bodyweight; Jack back squatted 405 lb and power cleaned 255 lb at 230-240 lb.
- The author says the strength base carried over: Eli's fastball sat consistently over 80 mph with weighted ball training, and Jack qualified for KingCo in shot and discus.

## Claims
- Strength Development: Both trainees were rank novices and made good linear progress on basic compound lifting in a garage about 14 by 35 feet.
- Strength Development: Eli posted a 425 lb deadlift at 169.5 lb bodyweight while cutting weight as a wrestler.
- Strength Development: Jack posted a 405 lb back squat, 255 lb bench press, 255 lb power clean and a power snatch of about 200 lb at 230-240 lb bodyweight.
- Strength Development: Both kept building strength despite setbacks (Jack's hip flexor tear running the 60, Eli's concussion and weight cutting).
- Strength Development: The author's own numbers at 220 lb were a 405+ lb squat (est. 1RM about 420), 465 lb deadlift, 225 lb power clean and 250 lb bench.
- Intro: Training mixed sled push/pull circuits, plate carries, and two-handed plate swings as metabolic conditioning.
- Athletic Carryover: Eli's fastball sat consistently over 80 mph through weighted ball training and other velocity concepts.
- Athletic Carryover: Eli developed very good bat speed and finished top three in batting average on his varsity team.
- Athletic Carryover: Jack qualified for KingCo in his first year of throwing, with a 43' 1.25" shot put and 116' 1" discus.

## Drills
- **Sled push/pull circuits with plate carries** - custom sled pulled with blast straps, paired with plate carries as farmer's walk substitutes; builds general strength and conditioning; no specific population stated
- **Two-handed plate swings** - plate on an adjustable dumbbell simulating a kettlebell swing; builds metabolic conditioning; no specific population stated

## Evidence cited
- Eli: 425 lb deadlift at 169.5 lb; fastball consistently over 80 mph.
- Jack: 405 lb squat, 255 lb bench, 255 lb power clean, about 200 lb power snatch; 43' 1.25" shot put, 116' 1" discus.
- Author: 455 lb squat at 260 lb bodyweight, later 405+ lb squat at 220 lb.

## Open questions
- The carryover to velocity and bat speed is asserted with no before/after numbers or controls; the 80 mph fastball is a level, not a gain.
