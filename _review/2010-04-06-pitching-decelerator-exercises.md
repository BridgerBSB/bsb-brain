# Pitching Decelerator Exercises

## TL;DR
- Argues that generic rotator cuff band programs like Thrower's Ten aren't enough on their own because they don't match what happens in a real pitching delivery; band work still matters for scap stabilization and mobilization, just not as the whole answer.
- Since true max-strength training is hard to run mid-season, gives five in-season or off-season lifts for the arm decelerators: chin-ups, pull-ups, rear delt flys, chest-supported rows, suitcase deadlifts.
- Gives no reps, sets, or weeks; includes two demo videos, for the rear delt fly and the suitcase deadlift.

## Claims
- Pitching Decelerator Exercises: states band-only rotator cuff programs such as Thrower's Ten don't reflect what happens in the typical pitching delivery, referencing the author's own prior post "Is Resistance Band Work Overrated?"
- Pitching Decelerator Exercises: states resistance band work still makes up a large part of Driveline training, especially for scapular stabilization and mobilization.
- Pitching Decelerator Exercises: states maximum strength training helps pitchers decelerate the arm safely but is hard to program mid-season.
- Pitching Decelerator Exercises: lists five exercises for in-season or off-season decelerator work: Chin-Ups, Pull-Ups, Rear Delt Flys, Chest-Supported Rows, Suitcase Deadlifts.
- Pitching Decelerator Exercises: states pulling and rowing variants are "king" for building upper back musculature and endurance.
- Pitching Decelerator Exercises: states TRX lateral sagittal pulls, unilateral work, and suitcase deadlifts develop the trunk musculature that controls trunk lean.
- Pitching Decelerator Exercises: links two demonstration videos, one for the Rear Delt Fly and one for the Suitcase Deadlift.

## Drills
- **Chin-Ups** - bodyweight vertical pull; builds upper back musculature and endurance for arm deceleration; for pitchers in-season or off-season
- **Pull-Ups** - bodyweight vertical pull; builds upper back musculature and endurance for arm deceleration; for pitchers in-season or off-season
- **Rear Delt Fly** - isolation raise for the rear deltoid; builds postural strength that supports arm deceleration
- **Chest-Supported Rows** - chest-supported horizontal pull; builds upper back musculature and endurance
- **Suitcase Deadlift** - unilateral loaded carry-position deadlift; builds trunk musculature that controls trunk lean
