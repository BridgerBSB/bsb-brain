## Off-Center Load
**Is:** The pitcher loses balance over the back leg during leg lift, letting weight drift forward off the middle of the foot toward the toes.
**Looks like:** The leg lift wobbles forward, weight rocking onto the toes instead of staying centered over the foot.
- `cue-almost-like-the-toes-are-going-to-lift-off-a-little-bit` - Almost like the toes are going to lift off a little bit
- `cue-stay-stacked` - Stay stacked

## Torso Gets Ahead of the Lower Half
**Is:** The torso leaks or tips forward ahead of the front leg during the stride instead of staying stacked over the pelvis.
**Looks like:** The chest gets out in front of the hips early, and the arm arrives late, often producing a cutting fastball.
- `cue-follow-the-ball-with-your-chest` - Follow the ball with your chest
- `cue-stay-stacked-over-the-pelvis` - Stay stacked over the pelvis

## Passive Back Leg
**Is:** The back leg coasts through the delivery instead of gathering weight and actively driving, so the load never transfers into the throw.
**Looks like:** The back leg looks dead at push-off, with no sense of loading before the drive.
- `cue-gather-your-weight-and-drive-off-the-back-leg-rather` - Gather your weight and drive off the back leg, rather than coasting off a dead back leg
- `drill-step-back-roll-in` - Step-back roll-in

## Back Leg Rides Out Instead of Turning
**Is:** The back leg extends into a quad-dominant ride instead of turning and flipping the hip through rotation.
**Looks like:** The trail leg drags straight out behind instead of pivoting, giving a stiff, non-athletic hip turn.
- `cue-heel-to-the-sky` - Heel to the sky

## Front Leg Blocks Into a Push
**Is:** The front knee drives past the toe at landing, turning what should be a rotational block into a linear push off the quad.
**Looks like:** The front leg caves forward over the toe instead of firming up to redirect rotation through the glute.
- `cue-keep-the-knee-behind-toe-line` - Keep the knee behind toe line

## Hip-Shoulder Separation Failure
**Is:** The hips and shoulders rotate together as one unit, or the torso pulls open early, instead of the upper half staying closed while the lower half turns.
**Looks like:** Chest and hips arrive open at the same time, with no visible stretch or lag between them at foot strike.
- `cue-lock-the-back-hip-forward` - Lock the back hip forward
- `cue-get-moving-out-through-him-turn-instead-of-pulling-your` - Get moving out through him... turn instead of pulling your torso left
- `drill-stationary-roll-in-separation-drill` - Stationary roll-in / separation drill
- `drill-roll-in-abbreviated-arm-high-cock-start` - Roll-in, abbreviated arm, high-cock start
- `drill-roll-in-full-arm-action` - Roll-in, full arm action

## Faking Separation With Lumbar Rotation
**Is:** The pitcher manufactures the appearance of separation by rotating through the low back rather than truly closing off the thoracic spine.
**Looks like:** The front shoulder looks preset, but the twist is coming from the lumbar spine rather than the upper back.
- `cue-preset-the-front-shoulder-as-close-as-possible-from-the` - Preset the front shoulder as close as possible, from the c-spine, not the low back

## Stalling Rotation and Bleeding Forward
**Is:** Rotation stops too early and the delivery bleeds forward linearly instead of continuing to turn through release.
**Looks like:** The body drifts toward the plate rather than finishing the turn, leaving rotational speed unused.
- `cue-if-you-would-just-rotate-a-little-bit-you-d-probably` - If you would just rotate a little bit you'd probably throw this 100 miles an hour

## Overly Rotational, Not Enough Linear Drive
**Is:** The delivery is dominated by rotation with too little linear direction toward the target.
**Looks like:** The pitcher spins in place without much drive down the mound, so momentum doesn't carry toward home plate.
- `cue-try-to-dip-into-the-velo-bucket` - Try to dip into the velo bucket

## Arm Path Off the Shoulder-Rotation Plane
**Is:** The throwing arm works on a different plane than the shoulders are rotating on, whether from tension or a structural mismatch.
**Looks like:** The arm and shoulder line don't move together — the elbow drags outside or behind the shoulder's turn instead of syncing with it.
- `cue-keep-the-glove-and-throwing-arm-loose-with-the-elbows` - Keep the glove and throwing arm loose, with the elbows syncing into the plane of shoulder rotation
- `drill-pvc-pipe-broomstick-plane-check` - PVC pipe / broomstick plane check

## Muscled, Hand-Driven Long Arm Action
**Is:** The arm gets actively yanked or hand-led into position instead of being allowed to wind up dynamically off the elbow.
**Looks like:** The hand leads the arm up into a long, muscled action rather than a loose, elbow-driven whip.
- `cue-arm-spiral-spiral-staircase` - Arm spiral" / "spiral staircase
- `cue-don-t-worry-about-getting-your-hand-up-worry-about` - Don't worry about getting your hand up, worry about getting your elbow up

## Early Arm Layback/Flip-Up
**Is:** The arm lays back and flips up too early relative to the rest of the delivery's timing.
**Looks like:** High-cock position shows up ahead of schedule instead of holding flexion into foot strike.
- `cue-hold-flexion-longer` - Hold flexion longer

## Excessive Spinal Extension Early in the Throw
**Is:** The spine extends/arches too early in the delivery instead of staying neutral through rotation.
**Looks like:** The ribs flare and the upper back arches well before release.
- `cue-keep-the-ribs-down` - Keep the ribs down

## Scapular Control Loss During Presses/Pulls
**Is:** During rowing, fly, and press work, the shoulder blade fails to control the movement, so the ribcage/spine extends or the humeral head glides forward to compensate.
**Looks like:** The chest and low back arch to finish the rep, or the shoulder rounds forward, instead of the shoulder blade doing the work.
- `cue-scaps-apart-scaps-together` - Scaps apart, scaps together
- `drill-row-fly-press-with-scap-reach-and-squeeze` - Row/fly/press with scap reach-and-squeeze
