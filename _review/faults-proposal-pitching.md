# Proposed pitching faults

Grouped from 33 cue/drill fault strings in the **pitching** domain only.

## Torso Drifting Forward Instead of Rotating
**Is:** the pitcher lets the torso translate toward the plate instead of completing rotation around a stable pelvis, cutting the rotational sequence short.
**Looks like:** the chest gets out in front of the hips early, the fastball cuts, and the arm arrives late.
- `cue-follow-the-ball-with-your-chest` - Follow the ball with your chest
- `cue-stay-stacked-over-the-pelvis` - Stay stacked over the pelvis
- `cue-if-you-would-just-rotate-a-little-bit-you-d-probably` - If you would just rotate a little bit you'd probably throw this 100 miles an hour

## Losing Balance Off the Back Leg During the Leg Lift
**Is:** weight drifts forward off the center of the back foot during the leg lift/gather, rather than staying stacked over it.
**Looks like:** the toes look like they're about to lift, or the pitcher visibly tips forward before the leg lift is finished.
- `cue-stay-stacked` - Stay stacked
- `cue-almost-like-the-toes-are-going-to-lift-off-a-little-bit` - Almost like the toes are going to lift off a little bit

## Passive Back Leg / No Load-and-Drive
**Is:** the back leg doesn't actively load and push, so the lower half coasts through the delivery instead of driving it.
**Looks like:** a "dead" back leg with no sense of gathering weight before firing forward.
- `cue-gather-your-weight-and-drive-off-the-back-leg-rather` - Gather your weight and drive off the back leg, rather than coasting off a dead back leg
- `drill-step-back-roll-in` - Step-back roll-in

## Quad-Dominant Push Off the Back Leg
**Is:** the back leg extends/pushes through the quad instead of turning through the glute, so rotation becomes a straight-line push.
**Looks like:** the knee drives out past the toe and the hip turn looks stiff and linear rather than athletic and rotational.
- `cue-heel-to-the-sky` - Heel to the sky
- `cue-keep-the-knee-behind-toe-line` - Keep the knee behind toe line

## Hips and Shoulders Rotating Together (No Separation)
**Is:** the hips and shoulders rotate as one unit instead of the hips leading and the shoulders staying closed.
**Looks like:** no stretch or lag between hip and shoulder rotation at any point in the delivery.
- `cue-lock-the-back-hip-forward` - Lock the back hip forward
- `drill-roll-in-abbreviated-arm-high-cock-start` - Roll-in, abbreviated arm, high-cock start
- `drill-roll-in-full-arm-action` - Roll-in, full arm action
- `drill-stationary-roll-in-separation-drill` - Stationary roll-in / separation drill

## Faking Separation Through the Low Back
**Is:** the pitcher creates the appearance of hip-shoulder separation by rotating the lumbar spine rather than truly closing the upper back.
**Looks like:** the front shoulder looks "preset," but the stretch is coming from the low back, not the thoracic spine.
- `cue-preset-the-front-shoulder-as-close-as-possible-from-the` - Preset the front shoulder as close as possible, from the c-spine, not the low back

## Opening the Torso Early
**Is:** the torso rotates open before the delivery has finished extending toward the target.
**Looks like:** the pitcher looks like they're "pulling" the torso around early instead of continuing to work out through release.
- `cue-get-moving-out-through-him-turn-instead-of-pulling-your` - Get moving out through him... turn instead of pulling your torso left

## Too Rotational, Lacking Linear Direction
**Is:** the delivery is dominated by rotation with too little forward/linear drive toward the target.
**Looks like:** the pitcher spins in place rather than working downhill and out toward the plate.
- `cue-try-to-dip-into-the-velo-bucket` - Try to dip into the velo bucket

## Forced, Muscled Arm Action
**Is:** the arm is actively muscled or yanked into position rather than being allowed to move dynamically off the body's momentum.
**Looks like:** a tense, hand-driven arm path that looks effortful instead of loose and whip-like.
- `cue-don-t-worry-about-getting-your-hand-up-worry-about` - Don't worry about getting your hand up, worry about getting your elbow up
- `cue-arm-spiral-spiral-staircase` - "Arm spiral" / "spiral staircase"
- `cue-keep-the-glove-and-throwing-arm-loose-with-the-elbows` - Keep the glove and throwing arm loose, with the elbows syncing into the plane of shoulder rotation
- `drill-plyo-ball-weight-progression` - Plyo ball weight progression

## Early Layback / Early Flip-Up Timing
**Is:** the arm reaches the high-cock (flipped-up) position too early relative to the rest of the delivery.
**Looks like:** the ball is already up before the body is ready for it, instead of flexion being held longer into the delivery.
- `cue-hold-flexion-longer` - Hold flexion longer
- `drill-roll-in-abbreviated-arm-flip-up` - Roll-in, abbreviated arm, flip-up

## Arm Working on a Different Plane Than the Shoulders
**Is:** the arm swings on a plane that doesn't match the plane of shoulder rotation.
**Looks like:** the arm and shoulder axis visibly diverge rather than rotating together on the same line.
- `drill-pvc-pipe-broomstick-plane-check` - PVC pipe / broomstick plane check
- `cue-keep-the-glove-and-throwing-arm-loose-with-the-elbows` - Keep the glove and throwing arm loose, with the elbows syncing into the plane of shoulder rotation

## Excessive Rib/Spinal Extension in the Delivery
**Is:** the pitcher falls into excessive spinal extension too early in the throw.
**Looks like:** the ribcage flares up and the back arches well before it should in the delivery.
- `cue-keep-the-ribs-down` - Keep the ribs down

## Substituting Spinal Extension for Scapular Movement
**Is:** during rows, flies, and presses, the finish comes from extending the ribcage/spine instead of moving the scapula.
**Looks like:** the chest and back arch to complete the rep rather than the shoulder blades doing the work.
- `cue-scaps-apart-scaps-together` - Scaps apart, scaps together
- `drill-row-fly-press-with-scap-reach-and-squeeze` - Row/fly/press with scap reach-and-squeeze
