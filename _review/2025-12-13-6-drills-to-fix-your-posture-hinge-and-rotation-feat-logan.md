# 6 Drills to Fix Your Posture, Hinge, and Rotation (Feat. Logan O'Hoppe)

## TL;DR
- A six-drill progression (offset open/closed, no-stride bazooka bat, hook-um/Kershaw loads, long bat step-back, underload bat, pivot pick) rebuilds Logan O'Hoppe's load-rotate-unload sequence after two seasons spent under-rotated and pushing the bat.
- The implements do the coaching: a 50 oz bazooka bat isolates the upper half, a 37 in/37 oz long bat forces an out-front release against an inside fastball, and an underload bat trains bat speed without conscious steering.
- Core teaching point: hold hinge and rotational space through the swing instead of collapsing into forward or side bend, then unload it late and let the barrel release.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=0) Logan spent much of 2024 and 2025 under-rotated, which forced him to push his bat through the zone.
- [02:05](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=125) Offset open angles the hitter's body toward center field, his new opposite-field direction, to teach turning the barrel deeper and staying connected while working oppo.
- [03:14](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=194) Offset closed is harder for Logan because it cheats him into holding space and closing off from the pitcher, since his hinge is normally hidden behind excessive forward and side bend.
- [06:21](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=381) The no-stride bazooka bat drill (34 in, 50 oz) isolates the upper half from the lower half as a static-position turn, targeting posture and connection instead of tipping the barrel out.
- [08:30](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=510) Hook-um and Kershaw target loading patterns; the Kershaw's kick-out keeps the chest taller and forces hinge rather than forward bend.
- [10:36](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=636) Switching from barrel load to handle load synced Logan's move because the implement's weight sits closer to the body, forcing a tighter, more connected turn.
- [12:02](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=722) Failing to decelerate the torso and pelvis at the end of rotation keeps the body turning and never lets the hands catch up and release.
- [13:06](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=786) Step back with a 37 in, 37 oz long bat against an inside fastball stresses space maintenance and posture, forcing the hitter out front to let the barrel release with explosion.
- [14:07](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=847) The underload bat drill has the hitter swing as fast as possible so athleticism takes over instead of muscling the bat.
- [15:10](https://youtu.be/NJejEG8CUkE?t=910) The pivot pick is called the most dynamic drill because it forces a load, a hold, and an unload in one move.

## Cues
- **"Don't be afraid"** - fixes excessive forward/side bend that blocks working bottom-to-top in the zone; for Logan O'Hoppe (over-postured hitters)
- **"Don't let your brain get convinced this thing is light, it is just a bat"** - fixes fighting or muscling an underload bat instead of letting speed happen; for hitters training bat speed
- **"Let it turn, commit to an early miss, let it get out there"** - fixes blocking extension out front with a long bat; for hitters afraid to extend

## Drills
- **Offset open** - stance angled toward center field; builds turning the barrel deeper and staying connected to the opposite field; for hitters who push the bat from under-rotation
- **Offset closed** - stance closed off from the pitcher; builds holding hinge and space through the whole swing; for hitters whose hinge is hidden behind forward/side bend
- **No-stride bazooka bat** - static turn, no stride, 34 in/50 oz bat; builds upper-half stability and control isolated from the lower half; for hitters who over-posture and tip the barrel
- **Hook-um** - short bat, stride element; builds the loading pattern position
- **Kershaw (barrel load / handle load, kick-out)** - up-down-stride with a kick-out; builds a taller chest posture and true hinge instead of forward bend
- **Long bat step-back** - step back against an inside fastball with a 37 in/37 oz bat; builds space maintenance, posture, and getting out front to release the barrel; for hitters who push the bat under constraint
- **Underload bat** - swing as fast as possible; builds raw bat speed without conscious steering
- **Pivot pick** - dynamic load-hold-unload move; builds tying together load, hold, and unload in sequence
