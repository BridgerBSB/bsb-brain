# 6 Upper Body Lifts For Pitchers

## TL;DR
- Six upper-body lifts split between deceleration work (shoulder IR eccentric, pull-up, corkscrew row, bicep eccentric) and acceleration/stability work (parallette push-up, landmine press, bamboo bar press).
- Eccentric lifts are dosed by time under tension, not reps: accumulate a full minute per exercise or position rather than counting to 6-8 reps.
- Bicep eccentrics should be done post-throw only, not 1-2 days before high-intensity throwing, because the added fatigue would leave the elbow underprotected.

## Claims
- [00:00](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=0) Upper body work, done correctly, improves arm health and in-game output over a full season; done incorrectly, it increases injury likelihood.
- [01:01](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=61) Shoulder IR eccentric on a cable stack strengthens the posterior capsule to decelerate the arm as it goes into internal rotation during the throw.
- [03:03](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=183) Eccentric sets should accumulate a full minute of workload rather than hit a fixed rep count (6-8 reps takes about 20 seconds and isn't enough).
- [04:03](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=243) Full range of motion pull-ups let the scaps upwardly rotate and strengthen the lats, which help decelerate the arm; half pull-ups don't produce that benefit.
- [06:06](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=366) In-season, use wrist straps on pull-ups to offload the forearms so they stay fresh to help decelerate and protect the elbow when throwing.
- [08:07](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=487) Parallette push-ups reach a deeper range of motion than a barbell or dumbbell bench and target the pecs, which accelerate the arm; load progression is a plate or chains.
- [11:10](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=670) The corkscrew row works the mid-back through full scap retraction and protraction plus internal rotation at the bottom, mirroring the tissue lengthening that happens in the throw.
- [12:12](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=732) The landmine press targets the serratus anterior, which helps the scap upwardly rotate; it can be performed rotationally, starting sideways to the landmine, for more sport specificity.
- [13:14](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=794) The bamboo (earthquake) bar overhead press, done with a light load (26 lb kettlebells in the demo), builds scap and shoulder stability under an unstable bar; recommended for experienced lifters only, not novices.
- [15:15](https://youtu.be/uDb-K_j1W0s?t=915) Single-arm bicep eccentrics on an incline bench (45-65°) train the biceps and flexor-pronator mass to decelerate the elbow and protect the UCL; best programmed post-throw, not before high-intensity throwing.

## Cues
- **"Help yourself on the way up and then slow on the way down"** - fixes rushing the eccentric phase; for shoulder IR eccentrics and single-arm bicep eccentrics
- **"Accumulate a minute"** - fixes chasing a rep count instead of time under tension; for eccentric-loaded exercises
- **"Don't ego lift this"** - fixes overloading the bamboo bar overhead press; for lifters without much gym experience

## Drills
- **Shoulder IR eccentric** - cable stack, bench for support, hold at end-range external rotation and resist into internal rotation; builds posterior capsule strength to decelerate the arm; for all pitchers
- **Full ROM pull-up** - regular or wide grip, full range down to a dead hang; builds scap upward rotation and lat strength for arm deceleration; band-assisted if full ROM isn't available, straps in-season to protect forearms
- **Parallette push-up** - locked wrists, deeper stretch than a barbell bench, 45° elbow angle; builds pec strength for arm acceleration; load with plate or chains to progress
- **Corkscrew row** - three-point stance, row to full scap retraction then corkscrew down into internal rotation; builds mid-back strength and throw-specific internal rotation range
- **Landmine press** - barbell in line with shoulder, brace abdominals, optional sideways rotational start; builds serratus anterior and scap upward rotation; can be made rotational for sport specificity
- **Bamboo bar overhead press** - unstable bamboo (earthquake) bar, light kettlebells, press overhead and hold; builds scap and shoulder stability under load; for experienced lifters, not novices
- **Single-arm bicep eccentric** - incline bench 45-65°, dumbbell curl down slow to end range then assist up; builds biceps and flexor-pronator mass to protect the UCL; program post-throw, not pre-high-intensity throwing
