# Proper Warmups For Weight Training

## TL;DR
- Lays out a three-part warmup order for heavy lifting: foam rolling/SMR first, then dynamic mobility work (hip and ankle focus), then resistance band work for blood flow and scapular prep.
- Argues static stretching of the hip flexors before squatting or jumping improves performance, pushing back on the common view that static stretching before power work hurts output.
- No reps, sets, or durations given for any piece; the value is the sequencing logic and the named drills, not numbers.

## Claims
- **First: Foam Rolling / Self-Myofascial Release (SMR)** - cites Cressey and Robertson's "Feel Better for 10 Bucks": muscles need strength and pliability through full range of motion; stretching improves muscle length while SMR and massage adjust muscle tone.
- **Second: Dynamic Stretching / Mobility Work** - baseball players, pitchers and hitters, tend to show mobility deficits between the plant leg and the free leg at the ankle; recommends training both ankles evenly rather than just the dominant one.
- **Second: Dynamic Stretching / Mobility Work** - simple leg swings can make a large difference in hip mobility over the long run.
- **Second: Dynamic Stretching / Mobility Work** - claims static stretching of the hip flexors before squatting or jumping has been shown to improve performance, despite static stretching's reputation for hurting power output.
- **Third: Band Work** - uses resistance bands between sets and before lifting to increase blood flow and mobility, naming facepulls, scapular mobility work, X-band walks, glute activation work, and shoulder internal/external ROM work.

## Drills
- **Ichiro stretch** - static hip flexor stretch performed before squatting or jumping; builds hip flexor length and, per the source, pre-lift power output; for lifters doing squat or jump work.
- **Leg swings** - simple dynamic leg swing; builds hip mobility; for baseball athletes generally.
- **X-band walks** - lateral walk against a resistance band; builds glute activation before lifting.
- **Facepulls** - band pull to the face; builds scapular mobility and shoulder health before lifting.
- **Homemade foam roller ("The Stick")** - 1/2 inch PVC covered in 3/4 inch PVC, wrapped in dense foam with handles; builds soft tissue quality, especially in the calves.

## Evidence cited
- Eric Cressey and Mike Robertson, "Feel Better for 10 Bucks" (T-Nation) - basis for the SMR/muscle tone and length claim.
- Stronglifts.com article on dynamic hip mobility stretches - cited as a companion resource for the leg swing recommendation.
