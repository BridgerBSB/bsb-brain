## 2026-05-02 — Bat speed canonical helper + cross-app refactor

### Bug class graduated

Sullivan AA bat speed showed 74.6 mph in Affiliate Tracker, ~73.6 in
Postgame, 72.0 in Weekly Hitter Player Report — three values for the
same player in the same window. Audit found 4 independent root causes:

- Tracker INNER JOINed `Events_View` on `cur_event_id` (PA-ending pitches
  only) — silently dropped ~70% of swings (mid-AB fouls + whiffs). Same
  bug on `_AACON_QUERY`. Bug introduced Apr 27 2026 to support `{ha_filter}`.
- Postgame ran TWO independent ±2.5σ filter passes (Pass 1 in
  `_enrich_pitches`, Pass 2 in `_compute_summary_stats`). Each shave of
  the tails on a left-skewed distribution cumulatively pulled mean DOWN.
- PD Goals bar chart did `AVG()` directly in SQL with NO cleaning, while
  the percentile pool DID clean — bar value vs pool calibration mismatch.
- Six surfaces all had their own inline 3-step filter that drifted apart.
- I (Claude) wrongly claimed whiffs can't have bat-speed-at-contact
  because "no contact = no SCV row." User corrected with screenshot of
  whiffs showing valid BS values (HawkEye tracks bat continuously
  through contact zone regardless of strike outcome).

### Fix shipped

**ONE canonical helper**: `clean_bat_speed_per_player(df, batter_id_col, bs_col)`.
- 57 mph floor always
- Per-batter p10 also (only if ≥20 swings in pool)
- p10 computed on RAW pre-floor speeds
- No bunt exclusion (57 floor catches them — bunts can't exceed 57)
- No ±2.5σ trim (trims legitimate elite swings on small samples)
- No post-clean min count (1 surviving swing publishes)
- NULLs `bat_speed_mph` in place; downstream consumers compute `mean()`

**8 surfaces refactored to call the helper:**
- Barrelsville (commit `1981f5c` on `feature/barrelsville`):
  - `tracker_data.py` (per-batter + per-org) + JOIN bug fix on _BS_QUERY + _AACON_QUERY (cur_event_id → ab_event_id)
  - `hitter_kpi_data.py` (KPI weekly per-batter)
  - `weekly_hitter_data.py` (Weekly Hitter Player Report)
  - `postgame_data.py` (Pass 1 routed through helper, Pass 2 deleted)
  - `postgame_percentiles.py` (percentile pool + min 50 inclusion gate)
- PD Goals (commit pending on `feature/pd-goals`):
  - `stats.py` (PD Goals bar chart — first time with cleaning)
  - `org_kpi_data.py` (PD Goals org KPI report)
  - `percentiles.py` (percentile distribution + min 50 inclusion gate)
  - `drift_hitting.py` (PD Flag Tracker drift detection)

**3 standalone scripts deferred** (not user-facing daily):
- `barrelsville/scripts/generate_bat_speed_draft_class.py`
- `barrelsville/scripts/generate_bat_speed_report.py`
- `barrelsville/scripts/generate_milb_org_rankings.py`
